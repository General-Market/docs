# Vision Cold Emails — Final Version

> **Rules applied**:
> 1. Subject = their problem, not our product
> 2. Under 80 words
> 3. Problem-first (Girardian: someone else has what should be yours)

## WHY THIS WORKS (the framework underneath)

**Problem-first**: Every email opens with THEIR pain, not our product. They feel seen before they feel sold to.

**Girardian tension**: The problem is never "you're missing out." It's "someone less qualified than you is doing the thing you should be doing." That's mimetic rivalry — the most primal motivator. You don't want the thing. You want it because someone who SHOULDN'T have it, has it.

**No CTA / humor CTA**: Instead of "book a call?" the emails end with:
- "Probably not for everyone though."
- "Kind of annoying, no?"
- "Seems like a waste."
- "They'll never know."
- "Take that however you want."
- "Just saying."

These work because they DON'T ask. They imply. The reader's brain completes the thought: "...wait, I should actually look at this." The decision feels like theirs, not yours.

**Under 80 words**: Every email above is 37-58 words. The brevity says: "I respect your time. I'm not desperate. This is real and I don't need to explain it."

**Subject = their problem**: Not "Vision prediction markets" or "25K markets." Instead:
- "50,000 competitors"
- "your models, someone else's PnL"
- "your predictions don't pay"
- "4 people"
- "this headline doesn't exist yet"

They open the email because the subject is about THEM. Not us.
