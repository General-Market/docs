# ITP Ideas: The Macro Strategist

Investor archetype: the geopolitical thinker, the Ray Dalio disciple, the Zoltan Pozsar reader. Crypto exposure through the lens of macro themes -- inflation hedges, de-dollarization, commodity cycles, monetary policy, demographic shifts.

---

## Inflation & Currency Debasement

**1. Hard Money Index**
- **Ticker:** HARD
- **Thesis:** Pure hard-cap, supply-inelastic assets that benefit from fiat currency debasement. The digital version of owning gold bars in a Swiss vault while central banks print.
- **Composition:** BTC, LTC, BCH, BSV, STX

**2. Inflation Breakeven Basket**
- **Ticker:** BREAK
- **Thesis:** Assets with proven positive correlation to CPI surprises. When the Fed says "transitory" and the market disagrees, this basket moves.
- **Composition:** BTC, ETH, SOL, LINK, AVAX, DOT, ATOM

**3. Sound Money Protocol Index**
- **Ticker:** SOUND
- **Thesis:** Protocols with explicit deflationary mechanisms -- burning, supply caps, or real yield that outpaces inflation. Monetary discipline enforced by code.
- **Composition:** BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, HBAR, CAKE, ENA

**4. Anti-Debasement Core**
- **Ticker:** DEBASE
- **Thesis:** Maximum convexity to central bank credibility erosion. When trust in monetary authorities declines, these assets become Schelling points for capital flight.
- **Composition:** BTC, ETH, XRP, LTC, BCH, ATOM, DOT

**5. M2 Money Supply Hedge**
- **Ticker:** MTWO
- **Thesis:** Assets historically correlated with global M2 expansion. When the liquidity spigot opens, these catch the flow first and hardest.
- **Composition:** BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, AVAX, NEAR, SUI

**6. Cantillon Effect Index**
- **Ticker:** CANTL
- **Thesis:** First-mover assets in the crypto credit creation chain. DeFi lending and staking protocols that sit closest to new money creation, capturing the Cantillon premium.
- **Composition:** AAVE, COMP, CRV, ENA, ETHFI, MORPHO, JUP

**7. Weimar Basket**
- **Ticker:** WEIMR
- **Thesis:** The hyperinflation hedge. Maximum allocation to assets with absolute supply scarcity and no governance ability to inflate. When confidence collapses, scarcity wins.
- **Composition:** BTC, LTC, BCH, BSV, STX, HBAR
