# DTF Ideas: The Risk Manager

Archetype: Portfolio allocator, hedge fund risk analyst, Sharpe ratio optimizer, drawdown minimizer. Wants crypto exposure with guardrails — capital preservation, risk-adjusted returns, and sleeping well at night. Sees crypto as an asset class to be tamed, not ridden.

---

## Blue Chip Quality Screens

**1. Crypto Quality Core**
- **Ticker**: QUAL
- **Thesis**: Only tokens with 4+ year track records, consistent development activity, and real revenue. The S&P 500 quality screen applied to crypto — survivorship bias is a feature, not a bug.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, LINK, UNI, AAVE, MKR, SNX, LDO

**2. Large Cap Minimum Variance**
- **Ticker**: MINV
- **Thesis**: Holds the top 10 by market cap but inverse-variance weighted. Allocates more to lower-volatility majors, less to high-vol alts. Smoothest ride through crypto cycles.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, AVAX, DOT, LINK, MATIC

**3. Revenue Weighted Crypto**
- **Ticker**: REVN
- **Thesis**: Weight by actual protocol revenue, not market cap. Protocols that earn fees have proven product-market fit. Revenue is the ultimate quality filter in a market full of vaporware.
- **Composition**: ETH, BNB, SOL, AAVE, MKR, UNI, LDO, GMX, DYDX, JUP, RAY, SNX

**4. Lindy Leaders**
- **Ticker**: LINDY
- **Thesis**: Only tokens that existed before 2021 and are still in the top 100. The Lindy effect says the longer something has survived, the longer it will survive. Time-tested crypto only.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, XRP, LTC, BNB, LINK, ADA, XLM, DOT, UNI, AAVE, ATOM

**5. Developer Activity Index**
- **Ticker**: DEVS
- **Thesis**: Weight by GitHub commits, active developers, and code velocity. Sustained developer interest is the best leading indicator of protocol longevity and fundamental value.
- **Composition**: ETH, SOL, DOT, NEAR, ATOM, AVAX, LINK, ARB, OP, SUI, APT, AAVE

**6. Protocol Moat Index**
- **Ticker**: MOAT
- **Thesis**: Protocols with demonstrable network effects, switching costs, or regulatory moats. These are the Visa and Mastercard of crypto — once entrenched, nearly impossible to displace.
- **Composition**: ETH, BNB, LINK, AAVE, MKR, UNI, LDO, CRV, EIGEN, ENA

**7. Institutional Grade Basket**
- **Ticker**: INST
- **Thesis**: Only assets available in institutional custody solutions and with spot ETF applications or approvals. The compliance-first crypto allocation for fiduciary accounts.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, LTC, BCH, LINK, AVAX, DOT, ADA

**8. Crypto Mega Caps**
- **Ticker**: MEGA
- **Thesis**: Concentrated bet on the two assets that represent 65%+ of total crypto market cap. Maximum liquidity, deepest order books, lowest slippage. The risk manager's default crypto allocation.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP

**9. Low Beta Leaders**
- **Ticker**: LOBETA
- **Thesis**: Tokens with the lowest trailing beta to BTC over 12 months. Lower beta means less amplification of market moves. For when you want crypto exposure without the full rollercoaster.
- **Composition**: BTC, XRP, TRX, BNB, LTC, XLM, LINK, ADA, HBAR, TON

**10. Profit-Taking Majors**
- **Ticker**: PROFI
- **Thesis**: Equal weight top 5 by market cap, with a 30% stablecoin buffer. Forces systematic profit-taking by design. The position sizing alone is the risk management.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, USDC, USDT, DAI

## Stablecoin & Defensive Allocations

**11. Stablecoin Diversification Fund**
- **Ticker**: STBL
- **Thesis**: Not all stablecoins are created equal — USDC has banking risk, USDT has reserve opacity, DAI has smart contract risk. Diversify across peg mechanisms to eliminate single-oracle blowup risk.
- **Composition**: USDC, USDT, DAI, FRAX, LUSD, GHO, PYUSD, CRVUSD

**12. Decentralized Stablecoin Pure Play**
- **Ticker**: DECST
- **Thesis**: Only stablecoins with no centralized oracle who can freeze funds. Pure on-chain, censorship-resistant dollar exposure. For when Circle or Tether become single points of failure.
- **Composition**: DAI, LUSD, FRAX, GHO, CRVUSD, SFRAX, MIM, RAI

**13. Yield-Bearing Stablecoin Index**
- **Ticker**: YSTBL
- **Thesis**: Stablecoins that earn yield natively — savings rates, T-bill backing, or protocol revenue. Capital preservation with a real yield kicker. The money market fund of crypto.
- **Composition**: SDAI, SFRAX, SUSDE, USDE, PYUSD, GHO, CRVUSD, USDM

**14. Flight to Safety Basket**
- **Ticker**: SAFE
- **Thesis**: 70% stablecoins, 20% BTC, 10% ETH. The crisis allocation — when everything is crashing, this is where capital flows. Minimal drawdown with optionality on recovery.
- **Composition**: USDC, USDT, DAI, FRAX, BTC, ETH

**15. Cash Plus Crypto**
- **Ticker**: CASHP
- **Thesis**: 80% stablecoins earning DeFi yield, 20% blue chip crypto. Designed to beat a bank savings account while maintaining near-zero drawdown tolerance. The bridge product for TradFi allocators.
- **Composition**: USDC, DAI, SDAI, SFRAX, BTC, ETH, SUSDE

**16. RWA Stablecoin Basket**
- **Ticker**: RWAST
- **Thesis**: Stablecoins backed by real-world assets — treasuries, money markets, and institutional credit. The safest yield in DeFi because the collateral exists in the real world.
- **Composition**: USDC, PYUSD, USDM, USDY, ONDO, FRAX, SDAI

**17. Inflation Hedge Stables**
- **Ticker**: CPIST
- **Thesis**: Stablecoins plus tokenized inflation-protected securities. Preserve real purchasing power when both crypto and fiat are volatile. The TIPS fund of DeFi.
- **Composition**: USDC, SDAI, SFRAX, ONDO, PAXG, XAUT, SUSDE

## BTC-Anchored Defensive Strategies

**18. Bitcoin Fortress**
- **Ticker**: FORT
- **Thesis**: 80% BTC, 20% stablecoins. The simplest risk-managed crypto position. BTC provides the upside exposure, stables provide the drawdown cushion and dry powder for rebalancing.
- **Composition**: BTC, WBTC, USDC, USDT, DAI

**19. Bitcoin + Gold Digital Store**
- **Ticker**: STORE
- **Thesis**: BTC and tokenized gold are both "digital store of value" plays with low correlation to each other. When BTC sells off on risk-off, gold often rallies. Natural hedge pair.
- **Composition**: BTC, PAXG, XAUT, WBTC, USDC

**20. BTC Drawdown Buffer**
- **Ticker**: BTCDB
- **Thesis**: 60% BTC, 30% yield-bearing stables, 10% ETH. The stablecoin cushion limits max drawdown to roughly 40-50% even in a 70% BTC crash. Rebalancing mechanically buys BTC dips.
- **Composition**: BTC, WBTC, SDAI, SFRAX, SUSDE, ETH, USDC

**21. Bitcoin Volatility Dampener**
- **Ticker**: BTCLO
- **Thesis**: 50% BTC, 50% stablecoins. Halves BTC volatility by definition. Simple, elegant, and historically still outperforms most TradFi allocations. The gateway drug for institutional BTC exposure.
- **Composition**: BTC, WBTC, USDC, USDT, DAI, SDAI

**22. Wrapped BTC Diversifier**
- **Ticker**: WBTCD
- **Thesis**: Spread wrapped BTC risk across multiple bridges and custodians. If one wrapper depegs or gets hacked, the others survive. Redundancy is risk management.
- **Composition**: WBTC, TBTC, CBBTC, RBTC, SBTC, USDC

**23. Digital Gold Standard**
- **Ticker**: DGOLD
- **Thesis**: Equal parts BTC and tokenized gold. Both are scarce, both are monetary premiums, but their return drivers differ. BTC is tech adoption; gold is fear. Together they cover both regimes.
- **Composition**: BTC, PAXG, XAUT, WBTC, USDC, DAI

- **Ticker**: BARBL

**25. Barbell: Stables + DeFi Gems**

**26. Barbell: Gold + AI Tokens**

**27. Core-Satellite Risk Budget**

**28. Conservative Barbell**

**29. Antifragile Crypto**

## Risk Parity & Equal Risk Contribution

**30. Crypto Risk Parity**
- **Ticker**: CRPAR
- **Thesis**: Each position contributes equal risk to the portfolio. High-vol tokens get tiny weights, low-vol tokens get large weights. The Bridgewater All Weather approach applied to crypto.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, LINK, AVAX, DOT, ATOM, USDC, SDAI

**31. Sector Risk Parity**
- **Ticker**: SRPAR
- **Thesis**: Equal risk contribution from each crypto sector — L1s, DeFi, infrastructure, stables. No single sector blowup can sink the portfolio. True diversification, not just token-level.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, SOL, AAVE, MKR, LINK, RNDR, USDC, SDAI, LDO, ARB, FIL

**32. Vol-Weighted Top 20**
- **Ticker**: VOLW
- **Thesis**: Inverse-volatility weighted across the top 20 tokens. The most volatile tokens get the smallest positions. Mathematically designed to minimize portfolio-level variance.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, XRP, SOL, ADA, AVAX, DOT, LINK, MATIC, ATOM, LTC, UNI, NEAR, ARB

**33. Maximum Diversification Crypto**
- **Ticker**: MAXD
- **Thesis**: Maximize the diversification ratio — portfolio vol divided by weighted-average vol. Actively seeks tokens with low cross-correlations. More diversification per unit of risk than any cap-weighted index.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, XRP, BNB, LINK, ATOM, PAXG, SDAI, RNDR, FIL, RUNE, XMR

**34. Equal Risk Budget DeFi**
- **Ticker**: ERBDF
- **Thesis**: Risk parity applied to pure DeFi. Lending, DEXs, derivatives, and staking each contribute 25% of total portfolio risk. No single DeFi subsector dominates.
- **Composition**: AAVE, MKR, COMP, UNI, CRV, SUSHI, GMX, DYDX, LDO, RPL, PENDLE, SNX

**35. Correlation-Minimized Basket**
- **Ticker**: LCORR
- **Thesis**: Select tokens that have the lowest average pairwise correlation over 6 months. When one zigs, the others zag. True portfolio theory — it is not about picking winners but about picking uncorrelated bets.
- **Composition**: BTC, PAXG, RUNE, ATOM, FIL, RNDR, XMR, LINK, TRX, USDC

## Volatility Targeting

**36. 10% Vol Target Crypto**
- **Ticker**: VT10
- **Thesis**: Dynamically adjusts crypto exposure to target 10% annualized volatility — roughly S&P 500 levels. When crypto vol spikes, the basket shifts to stables. When it's calm, it goes full crypto.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, SOL, USDC, USDT, DAI, SDAI, SFRAX

**37. 20% Vol Target Crypto**
- **Ticker**: VT20
- **Thesis**: Targets 20% annualized vol — twice the S&P but half of typical BTC. The Goldilocks zone for institutional allocators who want meaningful crypto exposure without the 80% drawdowns.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, LINK, USDC, USDT, DAI

**38. 5% Vol Conservative**
- **Ticker**: VT5
- **Thesis**: Bond-like volatility from a crypto portfolio. 90%+ stables most of the time, with a sliver of BTC/ETH when realized vol compresses. For pension funds dipping a toe into crypto.
- **Composition**: USDC, USDT, DAI, SDAI, SFRAX, BTC, ETH

**39. Volatility Regime Switcher**
- **Ticker**: VRSW
- **Thesis**: Full crypto in low-vol regimes, full stables in high-vol regimes. Uses trailing 30-day realized vol as the signal. Avoids the worst drawdowns by recognizing when the market is in crisis mode.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, SOL, AVAX, LINK, USDC, USDT, SDAI, SFRAX

**40. Calm Crypto Index**
- **Ticker**: CALM
- **Thesis**: Only holds the 10 tokens with the lowest 90-day realized volatility at any rebalance. Automatically filters out the noise and chaos. The crypto index for people who check prices once a month.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, XRP, TRX, TON, LTC, LINK, XLM, HBAR

## Drawdown & Tail Risk Management

**41. Max 20% Drawdown Basket**
- **Ticker**: DD20
- **Thesis**: Designed to never exceed a 20% peak-to-trough drawdown via dynamic stablecoin allocation. When drawdown approaches 15%, the basket automatically de-risks. Capital preservation first.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, USDC, USDT, DAI, SDAI, SFRAX, SUSDE

**42. Tail Risk Hedged Crypto**
- **Ticker**: TAILH
- **Thesis**: Core crypto exposure plus protocols that benefit from market stress — liquidation engines, oracle networks, and stablecoin infrastructure that see volume spikes during crashes.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, LINK, MKR, AAVE, LQTY, CRV, USDC, DAI, LUSD

**43. Crisis Alpha Basket**
- **Ticker**: CRISA
- **Thesis**: Tokens that historically outperform during 30%+ BTC drawdowns. Stablecoins, gold, and protocols that gain market share from competitor failures. The portfolio insurance policy.
- **Composition**: USDC, PAXG, XAUT, LUSD, DAI, MKR, LQTY, AAVE, BTC

**44. Anti-Liquidation Index**
- **Ticker**: NOLIK
- **Thesis**: Protocols that profit from liquidation cascades — lending markets, DEXs, and oracle networks see fee spikes when leverage unwinds. Own the infrastructure that benefits from others' pain.
- **Composition**: AAVE, MKR, LQTY, CRV, GMX, LINK, UNI, SUSHI, COMP, DYDX

**45. Drawdown Recovery Optimizer**
- **Ticker**: RECOV
- **Thesis**: Assets with the fastest historical recovery times from drawdowns. BTC recovers faster than altcoins, large caps faster than small caps. Optimize for time-to-recovery, not just return.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, LINK, AAVE, UNI, USDC, SDAI

**46. Perpetual Hedge Fund**
- **Ticker**: HEDGE
- **Thesis**: 50% long majors, 50% stablecoins earning yield. The stablecoin yield (5-8%) provides a constant income floor while the crypto half provides upside. Net exposure is halved, income is constant.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, SOL, SDAI, SFRAX, SUSDE, USDC, DAI

**47. Crash-Proof Core**
- **Ticker**: PROOF
- **Thesis**: Only tokens that have survived every major crash since 2020 (COVID, Luna, FTX) and still trade above their pre-crash levels. Battle-tested resilience as the selection criterion.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, LINK, UNI, AAVE, MKR, LTC, XRP, ATOM

## Systematic & Quantitative Strategies

**77. Momentum + Quality Filter**

**78. Mean Reversion Basket**
- **Ticker**: MREV
- **Thesis**: Overweight tokens that have underperformed their 200-day moving average by the most. Contrarian by design. Crypto mean-reverts over 3-6 month horizons more reliably than it trends.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, DOT, ATOM, LINK, UNI, AAVE, AVAX, NEAR, ADA

**79. Low Correlation Alpha**
- **Ticker**: LCALP
- **Thesis**: Select the 10 tokens with the lowest 90-day correlation to BTC. Maximum idiosyncratic exposure. When BTC dumps, these move to their own beat.
- **Composition**: PAXG, RUNE, ATOM, FIL, RNDR, XMR, TRX, TON, LINK, HNT

**80. Sharpe Ratio Maximizer**
- **Ticker**: SHARP
- **Thesis**: Weights optimized to maximize the ex-ante Sharpe ratio using trailing 6-month returns and covariances. The mathematically optimal risk-adjusted portfolio, rebalanced monthly.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, LINK, USDC, SDAI, PAXG, AAVE, MKR

**81. Sortino Optimized Basket**
- **Ticker**: SORTI
