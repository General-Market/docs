# ITP Ideas: The TradFi Migrant

> Investor archetype: Institutional investors and traditional finance professionals entering crypto. They want familiar structures -- sector rotation, market-cap weighted indexes, factor investing, blue-chip baskets. Think Vanguard, BlackRock, State Street -- but for crypto.

---

## Market-Cap Weighted Core Indexes

**1. Crypto Total Market Index**
- **Ticker**: CTMI
- **Thesis**: The S&P 500 of crypto. Market-cap weighted exposure to the entire investable crypto universe, rebalanced quarterly. The default allocation for any institutional portfolio entering digital assets.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, AVAX, DOT, MATIC, LINK, UNI, ATOM, LTC, NEAR, APT

**2. Crypto Mega-Cap Core**
- **Ticker**: MEGA
- **Thesis**: Concentrated exposure to the two assets that represent 60%+ of total crypto market cap. The "VOO" of crypto -- maximum liquidity, minimum tail risk.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH

**3. Crypto Large-Cap Select**
- **Ticker**: CLCS
- **Thesis**: Blue-chip diversification beyond BTC/ETH. Targets the top 10 by market cap excluding Bitcoin and Ethereum, capturing established protocols with proven network effects.
- **Composition**: BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, AVAX, DOT, LINK, MATIC, TRX, TON

**4. Crypto Mid-Cap Growth**
- **Ticker**: CMCG
- **Thesis**: Mid-cap crypto (rank 20-50) historically outperforms during bull markets. This basket captures the growth premium of established-but-still-scaling protocols, analogous to a Russell Midcap fund.
- **Composition**: NEAR, APT, ARB, OP, INJ, SEI, SUI, FTM, ALGO, EGLD, RUNE, FIL, AAVE, GRT

**5. Crypto Small-Cap Opportunities**
- **Ticker**: CSCO
- **Thesis**: Small-cap crypto (rank 50-100) offers asymmetric upside for risk-tolerant allocators. Higher volatility compensated by outsized returns during expansion phases. The "IWM" of crypto.
- **Composition**: PENDLE, GMX, OSMO, KAVA, ZRX, CELO, SNX, COMP, BAL, LRC, CKB, ROSE, ZIL, ONE

**6. Crypto Micro-Cap Venture**
- **Ticker**: CMCV
- **Thesis**: Venture-stage liquid exposure to protocols ranked 100-200. For allocators who want public-market venture returns without LP lockups. High risk, portfolio-level diversification mitigates single-asset blowups.
- **Composition**: API3, PERP, DODO, BADGER, LQTY, SPELL, BTRFLY, GNS, VELO, RDNT, JOE, SUSHI, AUCTION, CHESS

## Factor Investing

**26. Crypto Momentum Factor**
- **Ticker**: CMOM
- **Thesis**: Momentum works in crypto even better than in equities. Systematic long exposure to the top performers over 3-6 month lookback periods, rebalanced monthly. Academic alpha, on-chain.
- **Composition**: *Dynamic -- top 15 tokens by 90-day risk-adjusted return, rebalanced monthly. Example snapshot*: SOL, SUI, INJ, TIA, SEI, PENDLE, RNDR, FET, JUP, PYTH, JTO, WIF, BONK, STX, ORDI

**27. Crypto Value Factor**
- **Ticker**: CVAL
- **Thesis**: Buy protocols trading below fundamental value -- low price-to-fees, price-to-TVL, or price-to-active-addresses ratios. Mean reversion is alive in crypto; the market habitually oversells working protocols.
- **Composition**: *Dynamic -- bottom quintile by P/F ratio from top 50. Example snapshot*: MKR, CRV, SUSHI, SNX, COMP, AAVE, GMX, LDO, BAL, DYDX

**28. Crypto Quality Factor**
- **Ticker**: CQLT
- **Thesis**: Invest in protocols with the strongest fundamentals: consistent revenue growth, high TVL retention, active development, and robust tokenomics. Quality compounds -- these are the Procter & Gambles of crypto.
- **Composition**: ETH, BNB, UNI, AAVE, MKR, LINK, LDO, GMX, DYDX, ARB, OP, SOL

**29. Crypto Low-Volatility Factor**
- **Ticker**: CLVL
- **Thesis**: Low-volatility anomaly exists in crypto: less volatile assets deliver better risk-adjusted returns. For institutional allocators with volatility budgets, this basket maximizes Sharpe ratio within the crypto allocation.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, XRP, TRX, LINK, LTC, HBAR, XLM, ALGO

**30. Crypto High-Beta Factor**
- **Ticker**: CHBT
- **Thesis**: When you want maximum crypto exposure per dollar invested. High-beta tokens amplify market moves -- outperforming in bull markets by 2-3x. The leveraged ETF analog without decay or margin calls.
- **Composition**: DOGE, SHIB, PEPE, BONK, WIF, FLOKI, SOL, AVAX, NEAR, SUI, APT, SEI, INJ, TIA

**31. Crypto Dividend Yield Factor**
- **Ticker**: CYLD
- **Thesis**: Protocols that distribute real yield to token holders. Fee sharing, buybacks, and staking rewards create cash-flow-like characteristics. The SCHD of crypto for income-oriented allocators.
- **Composition**: MKR, GMX, GNS, SNX, SUSHI, CRV, DYDX, BANANA, UNI, PENDLE, LDO, RPL

**32. Crypto Size Factor (Small-Cap Tilt)**
- **Ticker**: CSML
- **Thesis**: The Fama-French size premium applied to crypto. Systematic overweight to smaller, undervalued protocols that institutional coverage misses. Small-cap alpha amplified by crypto's inefficient markets.
- **Composition**: PENDLE, GMX, GNS, RDNT, VELO, JOE, PERP, LQTY, BADGER, CVX, SPELL, BTRFLY, HOP, Y2K

**33. Crypto Multi-Factor Smart Beta**
- **Ticker**: CSMB
- **Thesis**: Combine momentum, value, quality, and low-vol factors into a single allocation. Factor diversification reduces drawdowns while capturing systematic alpha. The AQR of crypto, in one basket.

## Smart Beta & Thematic Strategies

**44. Crypto Core-Satellite: Core**

- **Thesis**: The 60/40 of crypto. 60% BTC, 30% ETH, 10% blue-chip alts. Designed as the anchor allocation for institutional crypto portfolios. Maximum simplicity, minimum tracking error to the broad market.

**45. Crypto Equal-Weight Top 20**
- **Ticker**: CEW2
- **Thesis**: Equal-weighting reduces concentration risk and creates a natural rebalancing alpha (sell winners, buy losers). Outperforms cap-weighted during regime changes when market leadership rotates.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, AVAX, DOT, MATIC, LINK, UNI, ATOM, LTC, NEAR, APT, ARB, OP, INJ, FIL, HBAR *(equal weight)*

**46. Crypto Minimum Variance**
- **Ticker**: CMNV
- **Thesis**: Mathematically optimized to minimize portfolio variance. Uses rolling covariance matrices to find the lowest-volatility combination of liquid crypto assets. For allocators who need crypto exposure within a strict vol budget.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, XRP, TRX, LINK, LTC, XLM, ALGO, HBAR *(optimized weights via minimum variance optimization)*

**47. Crypto Risk Parity**
- **Ticker**: CRSP
- **Thesis**: Each asset contributes equal risk to the portfolio. High-vol assets get lower weight, low-vol assets get higher weight. Balances risk contribution rather than capital allocation -- Bridgewater's All Weather for crypto.
- **Composition**: BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP, ADA, AVAX, DOT, LINK, MATIC, ATOM, NEAR *(risk-parity weighted)*

**48. Crypto Fundamental Weight Index**
- **Ticker**: CFWI
- **Thesis**: Weight by fundamentals (revenue, TVL, active addresses), not market cap. Avoids overweighting overpriced tokens and underweighting underpriced ones. The RAFI index of crypto.
- **Composition**: ETH, BNB, SOL, UNI, AAVE, MKR, LDO, GMX, LINK, ARB, OP, DYDX *(weighted by protocol revenue)*

**49. Crypto Max Sharpe Ratio**
- **Ticker**: CMSR
- **Thesis**: Optimized for the highest risk-adjusted return using rolling 180-day data. Systematic, quantitative allocation that institutional allocators can backtest and audit. Portable alpha for the crypto sleeve.
- **Composition**: *Dynamic -- mean-variance optimized. Example snapshot*: BTC (35%), ETH (25%), SOL (10%), BNB (8%), LINK (5%), MKR (5%), AAVE (4%), ARB (4%), UNI (4%)

**50. Crypto Barbell Strategy**
- **Ticker**: CBAR
- **Thesis**: Nassim Taleb's barbell applied to crypto: 80% in the safest assets (BTC/ETH), 20% in high-convexity small-caps. Protects capital in downturns while maintaining asymmetric upside exposure.
- **Composition**: BTC (40%), ETH (40%), TIA (3%), PENDLE (3%), INJ (3%), GMX (3%), SUI (2%), APT (2%), RNDR (2%), FET (2%)
