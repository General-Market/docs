# Blocks

You think a German train will be late. It will be. The question is whether the seal holds long enough for you to be right alone. Welcome to Blocks.

Blocks is a sealed-bet venue. Pick a number, pick a side, seal the commitment. Nobody sees the bet until the market closes. Nobody can run ahead of it. Nobody can fade it on entry. Nobody can buy your decision off your broker. The mechanism is older than electricity. The novelty is having one that still works.

## Each market thins the predators

Spot is the open field. Every extraction works there. Cross-venue arbitrage, frontrunning, stop hunting, orderflow buying, spoofing, latency races, insider trading, toxic-flow making — eight strategies, all profitable, all at the expense of someone who showed up to take a directional view.

Perpetuals refuse one of them. Options refuse two more. Blocks refuses all of them. Not by policy — by construction.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blocks/predator-matrix.svg" alt="Predator extraction matrix — nine strategies across four venues (SPOT, PERPS, OPTIONS, BLOCKS). Spot is vulnerable to all eight predator strategies; perps refuses cross-venue arbitrage; options refuses stop-and-liquidation hunting in addition; Blocks refuses all eight by construction. The final row, directional bets, is the only honest activity and is possible at every venue. Eight ways to lose, one bet that still works." />
</Frame>

The bets are sealed before they exist on-chain. There is no orderbook to spoof, no mempool to scan, no broker to pay for the flow. Resolution comes from external data verified by an oracle network with BLS aggregate signatures. Winners split the losers' stakes minus a 0.05% fee. The pool is parimutuel. There is no counterparty to fade you, because there is no counterparty.

## How a block resolves

Four steps separate you from the consequences of your convictions.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blocks/four-steps.svg" alt="The four steps — Browse, Seal, Resolve, Claim. Step 1 Browse: pick a batch from 90+ source categories like Crypto, Weather, Trains, Quakes. Step 2 Seal: bet UP or DOWN per market, sealed as keccak256(bitmap) on-chain so nobody can see your block until the tick closes. Step 3 Resolve: tick ends, oracles fetch real data, winners split the losers' stakes under 2-of-3 consensus. Step 4 Claim: withdraw winnings with a BLS-verified balance proof, any time, no lockup." />
</Frame>

<Info>
Your blocks are **sealed** — you submit a hash of your bitmap on-chain, then reveal the bytes to oracles off-chain after the tick closes. Nobody can see or copy your bets in advance. Your folly is your own until the moment of reckoning.
</Info>

## Why this and not the alternatives

Most venues let you take a directional view and then quietly sell the right to copy it. Blocks does not — because the act of placing a block is encrypted. The architecture refuses the abuse, rather than promising not to commit it.

## What can you bet on?

The breadth is the point. Anything that produces a number can become a block. Each one a small confession that someone, somewhere, believes they know what happens next:

Every block follows the same mechanic. A data source produces a number. You bet UP or DOWN. The outcome is resolved from real-world data. The universe does not care about your block. It moves, or it does not. You are paid accordingly.

## The data pipeline

Ninety-eight APIs feed numbers into a Rust process that feeds them into a database that feeds them into smart contracts that redistribute money between strangers. The architecture is sound. The premise is absurd. Both things are true.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blocks/data-pipeline.svg" alt="Data pipeline — external APIs (CoinGecko, USGS, TfL, GitHub, NDBC, and 85 others) feed into a single Rust data node that collects via HTTP, validates with bounds and staleness checks, and stores time-series rows in Postgres. Three independent oracles read snapshots, resolve ticks, BLS-sign their result, and reach 2-of-3 consensus. Smart contracts: VisionPool.sol holds sealed bitmaps, VisionClaim.sol verifies BLS claims, VisionReserve.sol custodies the parimutuel USDC pool." />
</Frame>

<Info>
The on-chain contracts retain their original `Vision*` names from the protocol's earlier identity. The product is Blocks; the bytecode remembers.
</Info>
