# X — Account Targeting Criteria

Working checklist to decide whether to track an account. Use to build any list (inner / middle / outer / monitor-only).

---

## Hard filters — must pass all

- [ ] Niche-aligned (their audience would also follow you)
- [ ] Active in last 14 days
- [ ] Engagement looks real (no round-number bot patterns)
- [ ] Single primary niche, or clear majority niche
- [ ] Not heavy political / drama / dunk content
- [ ] Real identity or substantive anon (not anon + controversial)

If any fail → skip.

---

## Size bands

| Band | Followers | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Peer | 1k–10k | Relationship-building, future scene members |
| **Core** | **10k–100k** | **Primary reply stable. Best ROI.** |
| Reach | 100k–1M | Occasional substantive replies for distribution |
| Lottery | 1M+ | Rare climbs with exceptional takes only |
| Skip | <1k | Unless genuine personal connection |

---

## Quality signals (rank candidates by these)

- Engagement-to-follower ratio ≥ 0.3% on median post
- Posts substance (data, takes, builds) — not mostly memes/RTs
- Engages with replies — pins, likes, or responds to non-mega accounts
- Predictable cadence (1–3 posts/day, knowable times)
- Rising follower velocity (growing month-over-month)
- Adjacent network is also high-quality (recurring substantive repliers)
- Content type matches your reply strength (charts ↔ charts, takes ↔ data, etc.)
- Bookmarks visible on posts (high = high-quality audience)

---

## Red flags — skip immediately

- Linktree to courses, signal services, "make money fast" content
- Mostly retweets (curator, not creator — diffuse author hash)
- Round-number engagement (likely inflated)
- Heavy political content (bad author-hash neighbor)
- Inactive > 30 days
- Audience demographically wrong ( followers are e-com gurus)
- Engagement-pod patterns (same 5 accounts always engaging first)
- Sponsored posts > 1 per 10 (paid content distorts what their audience came for)

---

## 5-minute audit per candidate

1. Bio — niche unambiguous?
2. Pinned post — what do they prioritize?
3. Last 10 posts — topic coherence
4. Last 10 posts — median engagement
5. Reply tab — do they engage substantively?
6. Following/follower ratio — influencer (high) vs peer (low)
7. Account age + recent growth trajectory
8. Profile completeness — link, location, real name

Score 0–8. Track at ≥ 6. Skip below.

---

## List assignment heuristic

After audit, sort into:

- **Inner (10–15)** — Notifications on. Reply within 5–15 min. Follow.
- **Middle (20–35)** — Daily check, no notifications. Reply when relevant. Follow ~half.
- **Outer (50–100)** — In private List, not followed. Occasional climbs only.
- **Monitor (no limit)** — Private List, not followed, not engaged. For watching the niche without diluting your user vector.

Total active orbit: 80–150 accounts. Everything else is noise.

## Posts worth replying to — ranked

| Tier | Parent type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | Genuine question from medium niche account | Low competition, high reply-back rate, perfect VLM scoreability |
| **S** | Data / chart drop from peer | VLM can compare your data against theirs — top score almost automatic |
| **S** | Build-in-public update from peer | Relationship + niche-hash reinforcement |
| **S** | Contrarian take where you have backend evidence | "Can confirm — here's what we see" pins reliably |
| A | Thread opener (50k–500k niche account) | Catches readers in the "deciding whether to read" moment |
| A | Long-form X article from niche thinker | Underexploited, uncrowded, deep VLM context from article body |
| A | Announcement / launch / fundraise in niche | Captive audience, congrats + substantive question wins |
| A | Video from niche talking-head (≥30s) | Less reply competition (viewers watching, not typing) |
| B | Take from large niche account (>500k) | Higher reach but reply competition fierce — bring data or skip |
| B | Mega-account in niche (>1M) | Lottery odds; occasional climb with exceptional take |
| B | Adjacent-niche post with crossover potential | Use sparingly — too many = hash drift |
| C | Reply-to-reply (depth 2) involving notable account | Narrow reach, high conversion in real conversations |
| C | Community Note thread in niche | Hyper-attentive audience; only with authoritative data |
| C | Spaces follow-up tweet (you attended) | Niche audience, relationship value |
| D | Memes / jokes | VLM can't score; skip unless meme IS the argument |
| D | Depth 3+ replies | No distribution — move to DMs if it matters |
| D | Old posts (>24h, low velocity) | Distribution dead |
| D | Off-niche viral content | Trains author hash in wrong direction |
| **F** | Drama / dunk threads | Block/mute/report risk; long-term hash poison |
| **F** | Sponsored / ad posts | Reply scoring suppressed |
| **F** | Off-niche megastars | Tempting reach, useless audience, hash dilution |
| **F** | Bot / scam content | Clusters you with low-quality engagement |

### Timing window (per parent)
- **0–5 min**: first-reply leverage on medium accounts
- **5–30 min**: sweet spot for large accounts
- **30 min–6 h**: climb-only, needs exceptional take
- **24 h+**: distribution dead

### Engagement state of parent
- **0 replies** → pin by default
- **1–10** → still climbable with strong take
- **10–100** → need ≥0.65 VLM score (chart / data)
- **100–1000** → only top ~5 visible
- **1000+** → lottery

## Reply ingredients — optimized stack

Priority order for every reply:

1. **Specific number, fact, or counter-claim** in line 1 (never an opinion)
2. **Chart / screenshot / receipt attached** — lifts VLM score ~20%
3. **Implicit credential** ("we ran this for X years", "on our backend", "from our oracle")
4. **Close on a question or substance** — never follow-bait
5. **No tags, no external links** in the surface reply (link in self-reply if needed)
6. **Conversational, asymmetric rhythm** — no LLM cadence, no "delve / unlock / leverage"

### Force-multipliers (add if available)
- Self-citation (QT your own earlier post)
- Niche jargon (legible to VLM as expertise)
- Time-stamped reference ("at 1:43 you mention X")
- Counter-data with source
- Anonymized backend screenshot

## CTAs & conversion phrases — what to say, what kills

### ✅ Use freely (positive / neutral)

| Phrase | Effect | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **"More here →"** + native X link to your thread | Strong | Triggers `quoted_click` + `profile_click`. Native = no link penalty. |
| **"I broke this down here →"** + native link | Strong | Same as above; sounds substantive. |
| **"Thread below 🧵"** / **"More in the thread"** | Strong | Drives `dwell` + scroll-through. Only if thread is real. |
| **"Bookmark this for later"** / **"Save this"** | Strong | Bookmarks ≈ 5× like weight. Use on reference content. |
| **"Screenshot this"** | Strong | Triggers dark-social shares — invisible but heavily weighted. |
| **"Send this to a [role] who needs to see it"** | Strong (with caution) | Explicitly drives `SHARE_VIA_DM_WEIGHT`. Use only when content is genuinely role-specific. |
| **"What would change your mind?"** / **"Anyone tested this?"** | Strong | Drives `reply` (0.5× weight). Highest-yield free CTA. |
| **"For the curious / for those building X"** | Soft | Niche signal, no penalty. |
| **"In case useful"** | Soft | Humble disclaimer, neutral. |
| **"→"** at end of line | Soft | Micro-click cue, no penalty. |


### Pinning replies as conversion

If your reply contains the link / CTA you wanted to feature, **pin the reply to your own profile** for a week. Visitors who profile-click see it first. Works as a soft funnel without bloating your timeline.

---

## Re-audit cadence

- Inner: review monthly (drop dead weight, promote rising candidates)
- Middle: review quarterly
- Outer / Monitor: review semi-annually
- Drop any account that goes inactive 30+ days, becomes scatter, or drifts off-niche
