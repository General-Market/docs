# Competitor Keyword Analysis — Vision (General Market)
## Session: 2026-02-27

### Objective
Identify Polymarket and Kalshi's top 20 pages, extract target keywords, estimate search volumes, and produce a prioritized list with difficulty scores.

### Methodology
- No Ahrefs/Semrush access — using public web search, SimilarWeb/Semrush public snippets, and manual analysis
- Cross-referencing competitor page structures, content strategies, and known traffic data
- Difficulty scores estimated from: domain authority of ranking competitors, SERP saturation, content depth needed

### Key Findings So Far

**Traffic (Dec 2025 — Jan 2026):**
- Polymarket: 17.1M visits/mo, global rank ~940, 73.86% direct, ~13% organic search
- Kalshi: 7.41M visits/mo, 65% direct, ~25% organic search
- Kalshi's organic share is higher (25% vs 13%) = more SEO opportunity to compete

**Polymarket top organic keywords (public data):**
- "polymarket" (branded, massive volume)
- "prediction markets" (head term)
- "polymarket api"
- Category pages: /crypto, /politics, /sports, /predictions/all
- Educational: learn.polymarket.com, docs.polymarket.com
- Trending: /predictions/trending-markets

**Kalshi top organic keywords (public data):**
- "kalshi" (branded)
- "prediction market" (head term)
- "event contracts"
- Category pages: /browse (weather, economics, politics, sports)
- Educational: help center, news.kalshi.com blog
- Specific event terms: "will fed cut rates", "super bowl prediction market"

**Competitor Page Structures:**
| Competitor | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Polymarket | /predictions/{category} | /predictions/crypto, /predictions/politics |
| Polymarket | /event/{slug} | /event/will-bitcoin-reach-100k |
| Polymarket | learn.polymarket.com | Educational hub |
| Kalshi | /browse | Main category browser |
| Kalshi | /markets/{category}/{slug} | Individual markets |
| Kalshi | news.kalshi.com | Blog/educational content |

### Vision's Unique SEO Angle
Neither Polymarket nor Kalshi target:
- "AI prediction market" / "AI trading bot prediction markets"
- Exotic/niche market categories (weather, earthquakes, space, wildlife, etc.)
- Parimutuel prediction markets
- Bot/agent-native trading platforms
- 25,000+ market scale across non-political/non-crypto categories

This is the gap. Vision should own the "AI + exotic data + prediction markets" keyword cluster.

### Difficulty Assessment Framework
- **Easy (1-3):** Long-tail, low competition, niche topics. Few quality results in SERP.
- **Medium (4-6):** Some competition from blogs/news sites, but no dominant authority.
- **Hard (7-8):** Polymarket/Kalshi rank, but beatable with better content.
- **Very Hard (9-10):** Branded terms or head terms where Polymarket/Kalshi dominate.

### Volume Estimation Methodology
Without Ahrefs/Semrush paid access, volumes are estimated from:
- SimilarWeb/Semrush public traffic data (Polymarket 17.1M, Kalshi 7.4M visits/mo)
- Google Trends relative interest data (prediction markets at 40/100 in Jan 2026)
- Industry reports ($44B trading volume in 2025, 600K+ MAU)
- Cross-referencing SERP saturation and result quality

Confidence: **Medium.** Volumes are directionally correct but could be off by 30-50%. The relative rankings and difficulty scores are more reliable than absolute volumes.

### Final Analysis

**Spreadsheet produced:** `docs/competitor-keyword-analysis.csv` — 30 keywords prioritized by opportunity.

**Top 5 Quick Wins (Low difficulty, high relevance):**

| # | Keyword | Vol | Diff | Why |
|---|---------|-----|------|-----|
| 1 | AI prediction market | 8.1K | 3 | Zero platform competition. Vision IS this. |
| 2 | prediction market bot | 5.4K | 3 | Bot-native platform, npx generalmarket init |
| 3 | how to build prediction market bot | 2.4K | 2 | Tutorial content → developer funnel |
| 4 | parimutuel prediction market | 1.3K | 2 | Only commercial parimutuel platform at scale |
| 5 | earthquake/space weather prediction market | 0.8K | 1 | Completely uncontested exotic niches |

**Strategic Insight:**
Polymarket and Kalshi compete on politics, sports, and crypto — the crowded categories. Neither targets the AI/bot builder audience or exotic data categories. Vision should NOT compete head-on on "prediction markets" (diff 9) but instead own the intersection of:
- **AI agents + prediction markets** (no competition)
- **Exotic/niche data categories** (weather, earthquakes, space, wildlife — no competition)
- **Developer tooling** (API, CLI, bot tutorials — Polymarket docs rank but Vision's bot-first approach is differentiated)

The comparison pages (polymarket alternative, kalshi alternative, polymarket vs kalshi) are medium difficulty but high intent — these searchers are actively looking for options.
