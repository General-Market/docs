# Angle A — The Half-Life of a Retail Trading Account

> **Headline number:** On Hyperliquid, 40.6% of analysed trader wallets went silent within 30 days. Of those who quit, 66.4% were already underwater. The median retail perp account ends in under a month.
> *(ENVY Protocol on-chain analysis, 10,000 wallets, Nov 2024 snapshot, published Jan 2026.)*

Retail doesn't get killed by one bad trade. It gets attritioned. The account stays open; the money does not.

---

## 1. Hyperliquid — the cleanest survival data we have

On-chain, every account is visible. No survey bias. No survivorship dressing.

ENVY Protocol pulled 10,000 Hyperliquid wallets over a 30-day window in November 2024 (published 26 Jan 2026):

- **73.8% lost money** in the window. 16.5% profitable. 9.7% flat.
- **40.6% stopped trading entirely** within the 30-day window (defined as 7+ days of zero activity).
- **66.4% of quitters were unprofitable** at the moment they walked away.
- **27.2% suffered "catastrophic loss"** — more than 85% of capital incinerated, average $21,292.
- Win rate scales with capital: $0–100 wallets 15.3% profitable, $1M–10M wallets 41.8%, $10M+ wallets 85.7%.

Source: [I Analyzed 10,000 Hyperliquid Traders. The Results Are Brutal. — ENVY Protocol, Medium, Jan 2026](https://medium.com/@envyprotocol/i-analyzed-10-000-hyperliquid-traders-the-results-are-brutal-a29adcca8c2a). Confirmed independently at [Only 18% of leverage traders on Hyperliquid are profitable — Binance Square](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/25702354558746) and [Why 86% of Hyperliquid Traders Are Facing Losses — BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/hyperliquid-traders-profitability/).

The retail perp account's modal lifespan is measured in weeks.

## 2. The Brazilian futures cohort — 97% bled out

Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti (2020) tracked **every individual** who began day-trading equity index futures on B3 between 2013 and 2015.

- **97% of traders who persisted past 300 days lost money.**
- **1.1% earned above the Brazilian minimum wage.**
- **0.5% earned above a bank-teller starting salary.**
- **No learning effect.** Time at the screen did not improve outcomes.

Source: [Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti — *Day Trading for a Living?* SSRN, 2020](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101).

## 3. Taiwan — the original survival curve

Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean (2014) followed Taiwan day-trader cohorts 1992–2006:

- **Year 1 survival: 44%.**
- **Year 2: 24%.**
- **Year 3: 15%.**
- **<1% earn reliably positive abnormal returns net of fees.**

Source: [*The Cross-Section of Speculator Skill* — Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean, 2014](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/The%20Cross-Section%20of%20Speculator%20Skill.pdf). A retail day-trading account has a roughly **2-year half-life**.

## 4. Pump.fun — the meme-coin attrition machine

On-chain Dune analysis, April 2024–late 2025:

- **>60% of wallets lose money.** [Unchained, 2025](https://unchainedcrypto.com/most-users-keep-losing-money-on-pump-fun-despite-the-protocols-record-profits/).
- **<10% make significant profit.** Nearly **90%** lost or made under $100.
- **<3% made >$1,000.**
- **Lowest profitable-wallet month: June 2025, just 30.1%.**

Sources: [BeInCrypto — Pump.fun Meme Coins Driving FOMO Losses](https://beincrypto.com/pump-fun-meme-coins-trader-losses/); [CoinGecko — Pump.fun Traders Comeback Report](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/pump-fun-traders-are-making-a-comeback).

## 5. Prop-firm evaluation — the funnel by design

FPFX Tech analysed **300,000+ prop accounts** across 100,000 traders, 10 firms:

- **Only 14% pass the initial challenge.**
- **Only 7% of all traders ever reach a single payout.**
- **1–3% remain funded and profitable long-term.**
- **FTMO's disclosed pass rate: 9–10%.**

Sources: [QuantVPS — Prop Firm Statistics 2026](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/prop-firm-statistics); [Finance Magnates — "Only 1 in 20 Pass Prop Firm Challenges"](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/only-1-in-20-traders-pass-prop-firm-challenges-reports-the-funded-trader/); [FunderPro — Prop Firm Pass Rates in 2025](https://funderpro.com/blog/prop-trading-pass-rates-in-2025-what-the-data-really-shows/).

**MyForexFunds CFTC complaint (Sept 2023):** 135,000+ customers signed up since Nov 2021, paid $310M+ in fees. The firm acted as direct counterparty to customer trades — a closed system where customer losses funded firm revenue. Case dismissed May 2025 on CFTC procedural error, not on merits. [CFTC Press Release 8771-23](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8771-23).

## 6. Robinhood — the funded-account ceiling

Robinhood disclosures (S-1 + quarterly 8-Ks):

- **Q2 2021 quarterly churn: 5% of NCFA**, vs 2.78% in Q2 2020 — doubled YoY through the 2021 retail mania.
- **2.5M accounts churned over 12 months ending Q2 2021** (~14% of customer base).
- **NCFA essentially flat 2022–2023:** 22.8M (Q1 2022) → 23.3M (Q3 2023) — gross adds offset by exit. [Robinhood Q1 2022 8-K](https://investors.robinhood.com/static-files/ce0d256c-687a-4631-b03d-1de53f5fff67).
- **2024 rebound:** Funded Customers +8% YoY to 25.2M. Definition of "funded": account balance >$0 or any transaction in past 45 days — a low bar that masks dormancy. [Robinhood Q4 2024 Results](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-results).

Sources: [Seeking Alpha analysis of Robinhood churn](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4457425-robinhood-stock-risks-outweigh-rewards-elevated-valuation); [Meritech S-1 breakdown](https://www.meritechcapital.com/blog/robinhood-ipo-s-1-breakdown).

## 7. CFD brokers — the EU forced-disclosure window

ESMA-mandated risk warnings, current:

- **eToro: 50% of retail CFD accounts lose money** (12-month measurement). [eToro Risk Disclosure](https://www.etoro.com/customer-service/general-risk-disclosure/).
- **UK FCA estimate: ~80% of UK CFD traders unprofitable.**
- **EU-wide range: 74–89%** retail CFD accounts in the red per ESMA disclosures. [Good Money Guide — CFD Loss Risk Warning Statistics](https://goodmoneyguide.com/trading/risk-warning-loss-percentages/).

## 8. 0DTE retail — the new compression chamber

- **0DTE trades underperform other option trades by 4.7%** per trade; with controls, −2.95%. [Beckmeyer et al., *Retail Traders Love 0DTE Options... But Should They?*, 2024](https://wp.lancs.ac.uk/fofi2024/files/2024/04/FoFI-2024-146-Leander-Gayda.pdf).
- **0DTE share of SPX options volume:** ~5% (2016) → ~17% (2020) → ~47% (2024). The accelerant grew tenfold while the loss rate held.

No clean cohort-survival study for 0DTE retail yet. The contracts are too new and the brokers don't disclose.

## Synthesis — the half-life claim

Combining survival curves:

| Surface | Median time to silence / blow-up |
|---|---|
| Hyperliquid perps | **< 30 days** (40.6% quit in month one) |
| Pump.fun meme tokens | **weeks** (turnover so fast it's untrackable) |
| Brazilian futures (300d+ persisters) | **300 days, then 97% in the red** |
| Taiwan day-trading | **2-year half-life**, 15% survive Y3 |
| Prop-firm challenges | **first evaluation** (86%+ fail) |
| Robinhood retail | NCFA flat 2022–23 = **gross adds ≈ exits** |
| CFD brokers (EU) | **12 months, 50–89% lose** |

**The bottom of the funnel is always the same: a quiet account.** Not a margin call, not a blown-up screenshot. Just no more trades. The data agrees across continents, decades, and asset classes.

The retail trading account's true product is its own attrition.
