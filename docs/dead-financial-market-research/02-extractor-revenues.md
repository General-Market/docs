## J · The compounding loop — what extraction gets reinvested into

### GPU procurement (the most concrete signal)
- **Jane Street** reportedly placed orders for **thousands of NVIDIA H100s in 2024** — Bloomberg coverage. One report cited "tens of thousands" of GPUs in Jane Street's compute fleet by end 2024.
- **Hudson River Trading** — disclosed $100M+ data-center expansions 2023–2024.
- **Citadel** (the hedge fund, separate from Citadel Securities) — Ken Griffin publicly noted Citadel was the **largest non-tech buyer of H100s in 2023** per various interviews.
- **Susquehanna** — reportedly large H100 buyer.

### New-grad PhD compensation
- 2015: top quant PhD packages ~$300–400K total comp.
- 2020: ~$500–700K.
- 2024: **$700K – $1.2M+** for elite ML PhDs at Jane Street / HRT / Citadel Sec. WSJ Sept 2024 ran "The $1m-a-year fresh PhD".
- Jane Street's "Quantitative Trader" new-grad on-target reportedly **$400-450K base + bonus** as of 2024 per interview-blogs.

### Alternative data spend
- Industry estimates from Neudata / Eagle Alpha / Tabb Group:
  - 2015: ~$0.6B alt-data spend industry-wide
  - 2020: ~$1.7B
  - 2024: **~$4B+**
- Top 50 alt-data buyers consume ~80% of spend; nearly all quant funds and largest HFTs.

### M&A and external acquisitions
- Virtu Financial bought KCG (2017) for $1.4B — eliminated a competitor.
- Citadel Securities bought IMC's options business assets and various smaller players.
- Cboe acquired multiple ATS and dark pool operators (BIDS Trading 2021, etc.).
- ICE bought NYSE 2013, kept consolidating.
