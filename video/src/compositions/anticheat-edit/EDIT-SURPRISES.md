# AntiCheatEdit — Pattern Interrupts (anti-monotony layer)

Thirteen mechanisms in a row build a predictable period: card → chart → talk →
"every exchange does this." A brain that can predict the next beat stops
watching. The fix is not to make all thirteen different (faster monotony) — it
is to **vary the period** (break the rhythm at irregular intervals) and to
thread **one escalating device** through all thirteen so the list becomes a
single story with a finish line.

Anchors are final.mp4 seconds, shared with MUSIC.md and overlays/timeline.ts.

## Ranked by leverage

1. **Edge meter — the spine.** Persistent thin HUD: `YOUR EDGE — 100%`, drains a
   chunk on each mechanism reveal, near 0% at §8 (602–648, dread peak), then
   **refills to 100%** the instant General Market appears (§9, 648). Converts 13
   reveals into one countdown; makes the §9 release visual, not just musical.
   Build first.

2. **Variable mechanism tempo.** Deliver 2–3 mechanisms as rapid-fire machine-gun
   cuts (8–10s, hard cuts, no chart hold) — back-half technical ones (M10 API
   516, M11 funding ~560). Acceleration after slow ones is a jolt. Slam back to
   slow for the liquidation finale (M13).

3. **Hard silence drops (~0.8s).** Kill ALL audio on punchlines beyond §8:
   "there is no free lunch" (§5, ~5:30 / 339–433 band) and "you are the one
   losing" (§6 end). Silence inside a wall of sound is the loudest event.

4. **Court-document slam.** ARTICLE_OVERLAYS already exist. Stop the gentle fade —
   rip a real headline full-frame with `impact-anvil.mp3` once mid-body and again
   on the proof beat (§10, 704–760). Reality intruding = strongest credibility jolt.

5. **"Every exchange" stamp.** He says it ~8×. Make it ritual: red
   `AFFECTS EVERY EXCHANGE` stamp thwacks on with `impact-metal-clang.mp3` each
   time. Viewer waits for it. Ritual satisfies; only accident is dull.

6. **Cha-ching counter.** Subtle `cash-register-classic.mp3` on every "market
   maker gets paid" beat. By the 4th it's a recognized motif.

7. **One full-screen number.** Black frame, one giant stat — Polymarket
   "0.04% take 70%" (§10, ~12:30) — sound dropped. Scale shock after 13 charts.

8. **Exactly one tonal-whiplash beat.** Deadpan humour on "probably you are the
   one being farmed" (~12:41), comedy SFX library. Use ONCE — a second cheapens
   the tone.

## Placement law

Do NOT space surprises on a metronome — that rebuilds the period you're breaking.
Cluster, then leave gaps. Let them escalate toward the dread peak (§8) and
release at the turn (§9), in lockstep with the score. Interrupt layer and music
breathe together.

Rough map (irregular by design):
- 0–2min: meter intro · first stamp · one cha-ching
- 4–6min: silence drop · court slam · machine-gun pair
- 8–10min: meter near-zero · stamps accelerate · §8 dread silence
- 10:48: meter REFILL (the big visual payoff)
- 12:30+: full-screen stat · the one humour beat
