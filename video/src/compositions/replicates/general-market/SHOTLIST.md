# General Market — Explainer

Two grids. One breathes. The other labors.

## Open (black)

- Meet General Market.
- A prediction market to empower algo traders.

## Grids appear

Both grids enter the frame at the same size. OTHERS on the left. GENERAL MARKET on the right. Every beat that follows happens on these two grids. Nothing else lives on screen until the final formula.

## Beat 1 — Liquidity

OTHERS fills in: half cells grey, half green.
GM fills in: every cell green.

- Always liquid.
- Because it's parimutuel.

## Beat 2 — Minimal profitable size

A $1 coin lands beside each grid. A curly bracket springs out from each coin, marking what $1 can reach.

- OTHERS: the bracket spans one cell. One market.
- GM: the bracket spans the entire grid. Every market.

Caption:
- $1 covers every market.
- Because it's parimutuel.

## Beat 3 — Markets

The GM grid expands. Cells multiply. Wider, taller, denser — until it dwarfs OTHERS. A small label appears beside it: 300,000. OTHERS sits small and unchanged.

- Infinite markets. All liquid.
- Because it's parimutuel.

## Beat 4 — Unlistable markets

Grids color in by category.

- OTHERS: politics · sports · econ.
- GM: politics, sports, econ — then Twitch, Steam, films, animal cams, space launches, weather, niche sports, everything that does not fit elsewhere.

Caption:
- Most venues can't list these. Insiders would devour them.
- Because it's batched parimutuel.

## Beat 5 — Settlement

A counter under each grid. Both grids pulse with each tick.

- OTHERS: one slow pulse. 1 / week.
- GM: rapid flicker across every cell. 144 / day.

Caption:
- 30× faster to settle.
- Because it's parimutuel.

## Beat 6 — The formula

Pull back. Both grids fully built, colored, lit, pulsing at their respective speeds. Hold one beat.

The grids dissolve into two horizontal bars, one above the other. Both start the same length, both labeled `edge`. Top: OTHERS. Bottom: GENERAL MARKET.

Above them, the formula:

```
edge  −  fee  −  spread  −  variance  −  knowledge
```

Each term flashes red in sequence:

- `− fee` — a chunk falls off OTHERS. Under GM, `fee` crosses out. GM stays full.
- `− spread` — another chunk off OTHERS. `spread` crosses out under GM.
- `− variance` — another chunk off OTHERS. `variance` crosses out under GM.
- `− knowledge` — final chunk. OTHERS is a sliver. `knowledge` crosses out under GM.

End state: OTHERS is a stub. GM is the full original length. A label slides under the GM bar: `pure edge`.
