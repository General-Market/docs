Vision Tab — What level of detail?

The existing agent page shows aggregate stats + recent bets table. What does the profile need?

1

Aggregate Stats Bar

ROI, total P&L, win rate, volume, total bets — the summary row from the current agent page.

2

Per-Batch Breakdown

List of batches the address participated in: batch name, source, ticks played, P&L, status (active/exited). Expandable to see tick-by-tick results.

3

P&L Chart Over Time

Cumulative profit/loss curve like Polymarket. Shows the trajectory.

4

Active Positions

Currently joined batches with live balance, stake per tick, multiplier, current bitmap.

5

Performance by Source

Win rate and P&L broken down by source category (Crypto, Stocks, Weather, etc.). Shows where they're good.
