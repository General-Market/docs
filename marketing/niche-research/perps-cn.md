# perps-cn — niche map
## TL;DR (5 bullets max: biggest niche, best underserved niche, dominant format, dominant villain, one-line opportunity)
- Biggest niche: centralized-exchange perpetuals, meaning always-open futures contracts on exchanges such as Binance, Gate, OKX, Bitget, WEEX, and CoinW; the strongest local examples frame product changes as margin, liquidity, and platform-power shifts rather than as simple listings. Sources: https://x.com/webktsr/status/2056582985688707514, https://x.com/Btcniumowang/status/2057727411509481510, https://x.com/CoinWZH/status/2060571905480954332
- Best underserved niche: plain-language risk control for Chinese-speaking perp users who already trade but still fear liquidation, forced decisions, and missed rotations. Sources: https://x.com/xiaomustock/status/2062455820273516914, https://x.com/Domingo_gou/status/2062700934430663046, https://x.com/Crypto77qi/status/2062063647736926389
- Dominant format: data drops and structured explainers win because the audience wants a concrete mechanism, a trading implication, and a reason to care now. Sources: https://x.com/ArtofSpecuycky/status/2062333906230391138, https://x.com/webktsr/status/2056582985688707514, https://x.com/PWenzhen76938/status/2055462930175725856
- Dominant villain: the market is not the only enemy; the villain is the hidden constraint that blocks a correct trade, including bad collateral treatment, locked settlement, liquidation lines, platform opacity, and tool overload. Sources: https://x.com/webktsr/status/2056582985688707514, https://x.com/FLS_OTC/status/2062492521008128483, https://x.com/The_Wooo/status/2061729127061946652
- One-line opportunity: build the account as a Chinese perp operator's notebook that turns exchange launches, stablecoin collateral, AI tools, and hedging products into simple rules for preserving optionality. Sources: https://x.com/GameFiLabs/status/2061054969135210503, https://x.com/pjjin574832/status/2062745216650764688, https://x.com/Murphychen888/status/2059899065002086696

## Audience psychology (max-marketing frame)
- Awareness level of the median audience member: solution-aware. They already know perpetuals, margin, liquidation, AI tools, and exchange names; they are evaluating which mechanism solves the next constraint. Sources: https://x.com/webktsr/status/2056582985688707514, https://x.com/GameFiLabs/status/2061054969135210503, https://x.com/Domingo_gou/status/2062700934430663046
- Market sophistication stage: 4 out of 5. Stage 4 means the audience has seen many product claims and now rewards specificity: collateral ratios, settlement status, open interest, liquidation mechanics, and exact workflow details. Sources: https://x.com/PWenzhen76938/status/2055462930175725856, https://x.com/FLS_OTC/status/2062492521008128483, https://x.com/Murphychen888/status/2059899065002086696
- Top 5 pains in the audience's own words:
  - "交易所到底认不认你手里的钱" captures collateral anxiety. Source: https://x.com/webktsr/status/2056582985688707514
  - "下不了单" captures settlement and withdrawal friction. Source: https://x.com/FLS_OTC/status/2062492521008128483
  - "盯盘压力大" captures multi-platform monitoring fatigue. Source: https://x.com/sodislikea/status/2061636273077731335
  - "天天被浮动利率和清算线搞心态" captures DeFi leverage stress. Source: https://x.com/lubijiaocheng/status/2062449648279920741
  - "方向看对了，最后却没赚到钱" captures the core regret of sizing, wicks, and emotional exits. Source: https://x.com/The_Wooo/status/2061729127061946652
- Status games: what gets flexed is early positioning, real use of margin mechanics, correct risk restraint, and knowing hidden platform rules before others do. Sources: https://x.com/daidaibtc/status/2060238765424361975, https://x.com/blockTVBee/status/2059542180738593255, https://x.com/FLS_OTC/status/2062492521008128483
- Status games: what gets mocked is fake wisdom, blind trust, over-levered all-in behavior, and users who mistake visible account balance for settled usable capital. Sources: https://x.com/igeekbb/status/2061751968885100991, https://x.com/nangongyuan/status/2061437654517035132, https://x.com/joakja/status/2061838124100038704, https://x.com/FLS_OTC/status/2062492521008128483

Sources: https://x.com/PWenzhen76938/status/2055462930175725856, https://x.com/HyperEVM_CN/status/2062462751164043544, https://x.com/suwanyu7777/status/2060342449286029323
