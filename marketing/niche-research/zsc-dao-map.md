# ZSC DAO — account map

**TL;DR.** `@zscdao` ("zerosupercycle", 10,619 followers, joined Dec 2022) is a self-styled "official Polymarket community" DAO. It runs a terminal web app at [zscdao.com](https://zscdao.com), is led by **@DavidMozhaev** ("CEO @zscdao"), and sits at the centre of three overlapping rings: Polymarket's own staff, a fleet of third-party Polymarket tools/side-apps, and a large pod of "@zscdao member" content accounts that shill Polymarket markets and the DAO. The map below is built from the DAO's own 182 follows plus a mention search — the highest-signal source for who it is actually wired to.

- **Source:** twitterapi.io via the x-targeting harness (`docs/x-targeting/twapi.py`), called directly to bypass the shared project budget gate. **Spend: ~1.2 cents** (1 followings page + 2 user lookups + 1 last-tweets + 1 mention search).
- **Anchor handle:** `zscdao` (not `zsc_dao` — that username does not exist; the DAO's handle is `zscdao`).
- **Method note:** "associated" = the DAO follows them (182 follows is a curated, high-intent list) and/or they tag `@zscdao` in bio/posts. Polymarket-staff inclusion means the DAO follows that employee, not that the employee endorses the DAO.

## Reading the map

The structure is a **three-ring brand-adjacency play**. At the centre, one DAO and one named CEO. Around it, a fleet of independent Polymarket tools — terminals, analytics dashboards, leaderboards, bots — that the DAO amplifies and is amplified by. Outside that, Polymarket's own staff, followed to borrow legitimacy. And wrapping the whole thing, a recruited pod of "member" accounts that post Polymarket markets all day and carry the `@zscdao` tag like a uniform.

The tool cluster (§2) is where real product exists. The member cluster (§5) is where the volume is. The staff cluster (§3) is the borrowed authority.

*One organisation, wearing a marketplace's name like a coat.*

## Glossary

- **DAO** — here, a self-organised online community, not an on-chain governance contract. ZSC DAO has no published token or contract; the "DAO" framing is branding.
- **Polymarket** — the largest on-chain prediction market; users bet on real-world event outcomes.
- **Side-app / tool** — a third-party product built on top of Polymarket's data or API (terminal, analytics, bot, leaderboard).
- **Shill / member pod** — accounts that repeatedly promote a product or community, here branded `@zscdao member`.
