[@lourdesanchezok](https://x.com/lourdesanchezok/status/2062166979662745789), [@All3in4out](https://x.com/All3in4out/status/2062312373281788295), [@haspidox](https://x.com/haspidox/status/2055584262913486962)

## Audience psychology (max-marketing frame)

- Awareness level of the median audience member: solution-aware. They already know the problem is fast Solana memecoin trading, and they compare tools, CAs, execution quality, and platform mechanics rather than asking what crypto is. [@kunleofweb3](https://x.com/kunleofweb3/status/2055905810237039083), [@TradingTerminal](https://x.com/TradingTerminal/status/2062608169143005351), [@Pumpfun](https://x.com/Pumpfun/status/2058951732227449174)
- Market sophistication stage: stage 4. The audience has seen many "next runner" and "hidden gem" claims, so posts add proof through screenshots, platform revenue, wallet stats, PNL, or anti-scam receipts. [@Mrsolexpert_1](https://x.com/Mrsolexpert_1/status/2055657371624722712), [@meilinxbt](https://x.com/meilinxbt/status/2062188508794454435), [@Flowslikeosmo](https://x.com/Flowslikeosmo/status/2056524494618103846), [@DataC58218](https://x.com/DataC58218/status/2062251993830240370)
- Top 5 pains in the audience's own words:
  - "Just got drained for $26,669" names the fear of social-engineered wallet loss. [@daddyriskbets](https://x.com/daddyriskbets/status/2052478368046592437)
  - "withdraw everything immediately" names the fear of trapped platform balances. [@SoloJayQ](https://x.com/SoloJayQ/status/2060994854532919811)
  - "you ape, you panic sell" names the self-inflicted trading loop. [@haspidox](https://x.com/haspidox/status/2055584262913486962)
  - "aggressive dumping" names the fear that incentives reward short-term exits. [@chooserich](https://x.com/chooserich/status/2059324057439404034)
  - "the market loves to make you open LinkedIn" names the status pain of needing a job after losses. [@Pumpfun](https://x.com/Pumpfun/status/2056058597130342565)
- Status games: what gets flexed is speed, PNL, early entry, big market-cap moves, wallet-level proof, and being able to call a runner before CT, meaning Crypto Twitter, sees it. [@caspianxbt](https://x.com/caspianxbt/status/2055295528775098720), [@meilinxbt](https://x.com/meilinxbt/status/2062188508794454435), [@HijabiEth](https://x.com/HijabiEth/status/2053087256651628603), [@trade](https://x.com/trade/status/2053542849199644791)
- Status games: what gets mocked is being late, using stale tools, getting farmed by platform incentives, trusting affiliates, and calling obvious extraction a community win. [@jackduval](https://x.com/jackduval/status/2061203531873525940), [@jussy_world](https://x.com/jussy_world/status/2058181229862953181), [@tier1haterr](https://x.com/tier1haterr/status/2055737715686093107), [@Amystreet](https://x.com/Amystreet/status/2062669523853918627)
