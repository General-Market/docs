# perps-kr — niche map
## TL;DR (5 bullets max: biggest niche, best underserved niche, dominant format, dominant villain, one-line opportunity)
- Biggest engagement in the harvest is not a perps niche: the Korean word `손절` means stop-loss in trading, but it also pulled relationship cut-off posts; those posts dominate raw engagement, led by @giyommigirl at 22,924 engagement and 1.6M views, so treat them as keyword contamination, not trading demand: https://x.com/giyommigirl/status/2057481995937325119
- Biggest trading niche is beginner wealth-path content: simple ETF, stock, and stop-loss rules outperform deeper perps mechanics, with @JOOWOL2's QQQM post at 4,988 engagement / 1.68M views and @trend_tiger_'s three-step "주린이" post at 5,325 engagement / 753k views: https://x.com/JOOWOL2/status/2061373780052881687, https://x.com/trend_tiger_/status/2058217761386672509
- Best underserved niche is Korean perps risk education for Hyperliquid-style markets: the harvest has HYPE narrative, wallet-drama receipts, and scattered position talk, but few clean explainers on funding, liquidation, open interest, and position sizing in Korean: https://x.com/magonia_B/status/2058865275416752174, https://x.com/WhitePeach/status/2058287553061724571
- Dominant format is the numbered rule list: `formats.json` ranks `numbered_list` highest by median engagement, and the top examples are "3줄 요약" and "알아두면 좋을 내용들" posts: https://x.com/trend_tiger_/status/2058217761386672509, https://x.com/JOOWOL2/status/2062680367740670281
- One-line opportunity: build a Korean "perps without liquidation theatre" account that turns Hyperliquid/HYPE excitement into checklists, trade logs, and risk math for 주린이-to-intermediate traders: https://x.com/gimduha77994334/status/2057085313504452633, https://x.com/grabcrabtrader/status/2062763948831859067

## Audience psychology (max-marketing frame)
- Awareness level of the median audience member: solution-aware moving toward product-aware; they already know ETFs, large-cap stocks, BTC, HYPE, and long/short instruments, and they want clearer rules for when to buy, size, hedge, or exit: https://x.com/JOOWOL2/status/2056587064825790579, https://x.com/magonia_B/status/2058865275416752174
- Market sophistication stage: Stage 4 out of 5; the audience has seen many "get rich" and "next trade" claims, so higher-performing posts either show specific rules, receipts, or sober warnings instead of pure promise: https://x.com/trend_tiger_/status/2058217761386672509, https://x.com/magonia_B/status/2061349007952359602, https://x.com/admi_alts/status/2062824312604881302
- Evidence for Stage 4: creators get traction by saying "do this simple thing" with constraints, by exposing wallets/referrals, or by telling followers what not to do during volatility: https://x.com/JOOWOL2/status/2061937979099320774, https://x.com/magonia_B/status/2061349007952359602, https://x.com/admi_alts/status/2062730069647454712
