# trenches-cn — niche map

## TL;DR (5 bullets max: biggest niche, best underserved niche, dominant format, dominant villain, one-line opportunity)

- Biggest niche: the feed is broad "Chinese X trenches" rather than pure crypto; shock, outrage, and low-context viral posts create the largest attention layer, while trade content clusters around Binance/wallet campaigns, on-chain meme hunting, and US-stock AI alpha. Evidence: @Zhixiajcmj's "X一点都不黄" viral post, @Mantou_naive's nightlife post, @binancezh's blind-box campaign, @nancy_c813's $FOMA giveaway, and @aleabitoreddit's Chinese AI-stock thread. [Zhixiajcmj](https://x.com/Zhixiajcmj/status/2061879931949453681), [Mantou_naive](https://x.com/Mantou_naive/status/2061775111850696978), [binancezh](https://x.com/binancezh/status/2060602509769818594), [nancy_c813](https://x.com/nancy_c813/status/2058416531617533952), [aleabitoreddit](https://x.com/aleabitoreddit/status/2062723902728802341)
- Best underserved niche: structured signal hygiene for Chinese readers moving between meme coins, Binance Alpha, US stocks, and prediction markets; the audience complains about delayed screenshots, "垃圾小作文", and unreliable copy-trading, but the harvest has few neutral, repeatable triage systems. [315728dzp](https://x.com/315728dzp/status/2060386902025572657), [waveking1314](https://x.com/waveking1314/status/2062148740568486239), [Web3wst](https://x.com/Web3wst/status/2058517817570509137), [gmgnai](https://x.com/gmgnai/status/2062500348225605721)
- Dominant format: short bait and meme-short posts dominate raw volume, but the most usable trade formats are numbered lists, tutorial threads, and data drops; examples include $FOMA steps, "如何找当天最低点做T", and Bridgewater AI-position breakdowns. [nancy_c813](https://x.com/nancy_c813/status/2058416531617533952), [Zoozo2025](https://x.com/Zoozo2025/status/2054044492416839839), [DtDt666](https://x.com/DtDt666/status/2055653496108757054)
- Dominant villain: the villain is not one person; it is the "late retail exit" loop: KOLs, hidden insiders, slow information, rug-prone tokens, unsafe wallets, and the user's own greed. [lugeweb3](https://x.com/lugeweb3/status/2059533212121244075), [0xKevin00](https://x.com/0xKevin00/status/2062440705423970399), [315728dzp](https://x.com/315728dzp/status/2060386902025572657), [NorthFan0615](https://x.com/NorthFan0615/status/2057782573385007574), [huahuayjy](https://x.com/huahuayjy/status/2044163030809038882)
- One-line opportunity: build the account as a Chinese "before you ape" desk: fast screenshots translated into a repeatable checklist, with clear labels for signal, bait, scam, late entry, and survivable position size. [thecryptoskanda](https://x.com/thecryptoskanda/status/2052670778832048475), [ScarlettWeb3](https://x.com/ScarlettWeb3/status/2054075451786883533), [xtony1314](https://x.com/xtony1314/status/2062874493476127152), [DongBnb](https://x.com/DongBnb/status/2055640650054238528)

## Audience psychology (max-marketing frame)

- Engagement definition: engagement means cached favorites + retweets + replies + quotes in `tweets.jsonl`.
- Awareness level of the median audience member: solution-aware. They already know the pain is late information, bad KOLs, unsafe wallets, overleverage, and thin alpha; they are looking for better lists, tools, filters, and workflows. [315728dzp](https://x.com/315728dzp/status/2060386902025572657), [lugeweb3](https://x.com/lugeweb3/status/2059533212121244075), [gmgnai](https://x.com/gmgnai/status/2062500348225605721), [Web3wst](https://x.com/Web3wst/status/2058517817570509137)
- Market sophistication stage: Stage 4. "Stage 4" means the audience has seen many versions of the promise, no longer trusts generic alpha claims, and responds better to proof, workflow, screenshots, data, and anti-scam framing. Evidence: @waveking1314 rejects "垃圾小作文", @gmgnai sells filters for traps and bot volume, and @lugeweb3 names promoted scam KOLs. [waveking1314](https://x.com/waveking1314/status/2062148740568486239), [gmgnai](https://x.com/gmgnai/status/2062500348225605721), [lugeweb3](https://x.com/lugeweb3/status/2059533212121244075)
