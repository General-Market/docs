# perps-jp — niche map
## TL;DR (5 bullets max: biggest niche, best underserved niche, dominant format, dominant villain, one-line opportunity)
- Biggest niche: Japanese leveraged-trading pain, especially BTC/perp, stock-margin, and short/long liquidation talk; the clearest high-engagement evidence is margin-call trauma and forced-exit language, not abstract protocol education. Evidence: https://x.com/oniyanogame/status/2056641589552803907, https://x.com/hibitti/status/2060256113778741715, https://x.com/Nishi8mAlert/status/2062323738759070080
- Best underserved niche: Japanese Perp DEX explainers for people moving from FX, Bybit, or Japanese stock margin into on-chain perps; the harvest has scattered high-quality posts, but not a consistent account that translates risk, mechanics, and platform differences every day. Evidence: https://x.com/tommy_love123/status/2061474474822959382, https://x.com/brto_0224/status/2061811508108255440, https://x.com/cosmo20199/status/2061970315849462258
- Dominant format: short fear/greed posts and daily market warnings travel widest, while the strongest niche-native format is the structured explainer with numbered sections, data points, and a clear "what to do now" frame. Evidence: https://x.com/gairu_crypto/status/2062079226602492183, https://x.com/K_Trader_Crypto/status/2062120309055262944, https://x.com/Sky1855961/status/2062132032625623515
- Dominant villain: getting harvested as "養分" by whales, leverage, thin liquidity, fake sites, and opaque exchanges; people talk about being liquidated, misled, or trapped more than they talk about calm portfolio construction. Evidence: https://x.com/gairu_crypto/status/2061718188979794004, https://x.com/lucianlamp/status/2060613957707325498, https://x.com/Goemon_CLOSEPA/status/2062314706308895218
- One-line opportunity: build the Japanese "perp risk desk" account: daily liquidation map, platform-risk translation, and beginner-safe Perp DEX onboarding, with every post tied to a current tradeable setup. Evidence: https://x.com/Nishi8mAlert/status/2062356252735267124, https://x.com/TakayamaJoe/status/2062618866023542937, https://x.com/saizyo_crypto/status/2062858093910978923

## Sub-niches
Perp means perpetual futures: futures-like trading with no fixed expiry. DEX means decentralized exchange: an exchange that runs on-chain instead of through a single centralized operator. The harvest is mixed; broad Japanese stock-margin posts are included only where they reveal the same leverage psychology as crypto perps. Evidence: https://x.com/SOU_BTC/status/2059769933765259448, https://x.com/tonkatsu_index/status/2053258155052990675

## Audience psychology (max-marketing frame)
- Awareness level of the median audience member: solution-aware. They already know BTC, leverage, long/short, margin calls, and major venue names; they need translation from "I can trade this" to "I can survive this venue, this liquidation map, and this position size." Evidence: https://x.com/Nishi8mAlert/status/2062323738759070080, https://x.com/tommy_love123/status/2061474474822959382, https://x.com/K_Trader_Crypto/status/2062155012260970586
- Market sophistication stage: 3 of 5. Stage 3 means the audience has seen many "next Hyperliquid", points, signal, and leverage promises, so it responds to proof, data, and risk framing more than to generic upside claims. Evidence: https://x.com/cryptobaby/status/2060665312140550157, https://x.com/lucianlamp/status/2060613957707325498, https://x.com/SakaneBTC/status/2062870883828666534
