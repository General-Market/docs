# Never sell as a perp DEX again

A positioning note. Seed thought from Max, expanded.

## The seed

> Should never sell as a perp DEX anymore. Lost on Binance Futures, lost on GMX,
> Vertex, Aster, Symmio, Apex, Lighter, Jupiter. The TAM is users who promised to
> never touch a perp again. Even a *better* perp DEX doesn't help — the pain is
> still there. Only a new narrative brings users back: pumpfun, polymarket. Perps =
> CFD in people's heads. Credit on PumpFun tokens would sell far better.

## The truth underneath

Every name on that list is a place someone has already lost money. A better perp
DEX is a better tour of the same graveyard. The category word itself carries the
wound — say "perp" and the trader feels the liquidation wick before he hears the
pitch.

You cannot sell a graveyard tour as a vacation.

## Correcting the CFD point — the instinct is right, the reason is wrong

The stat doesn't hold: by the official disclaimers, *more* retail accounts lose on
CFDs (74–89%) than the folklore around perps. So "fewer people lose on CFDs" is
false.

But the feeling is true, and the feeling is what matters. A CFD loss feels like
*being wrong*. A perp loss feels like *being robbed* — the liquidation cascade, the
funding bleed, the wick that hunted your stop, the insider who saw your order. The
betrayal is the difference.

Therefore: the product that wins the burned trader is not the one that loses him
less money. It is the one where losing does not feel like *theft*. That is exactly
what anti-cheat answers — sealed bets, parimutuel pools, BLS settlement. The wound
names the cure.

## The sharper prize — not the refugees, the newcomers

pump.fun and polymarket did not win by recovering burned perp traders. They won by
pulling in people who *were not trading at all* — degens who wanted to gamble on a
launch, normies who already had an opinion about an election. The new narrative's
power is that it expands the audience past traders entirely.

The burned-perp TAM is real, but it is also broke and bitter. Aim the narrative at
the person who would never open a perp DEX in the first place. That is the larger
field.

## The rule

- Drop "perp," "leverage," "futures," "liquidation," "funding" from the front door.
- Lead with a new category word, not a better version of an old one.
- Let "anti-cheat" carry the betrayal-cure for the ones who do arrive burned.
- Treat "credit on PumpFun tokens" as a *separate* new narrative — borrow against
  your bag without selling it. Own product, own name.

The category word is the first thing the customer believes. Choose it before the feature.
