### POST 3: "Stop Getting Front-Run"
**Target keyword:** sealed prediction market, front-running resistant, private betting

**Title:** On Polymarket, Whales See Your Order Book. On Vision, Nobody Sees Anything.

**Body:**
Polymarket uses a public order book. That means:
- Your bets are visible before resolution
- Larger players copy winning strategies

It's the same reason sealed-bid auctions exist: your strategy stays private.
