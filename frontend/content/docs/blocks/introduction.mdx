# Blocks

Blocks is a sealed-bet venue across 90+ real-world data feeds. Pick UP or DOWN, commit on-chain, win the losers' pool minus a 0.05% fee.

**Key facts**

- **Sealed commitments.** You submit `keccak256(bitmap)` on-chain. Your bet stays private until the tick (the resolution window) closes.
- **Parimutuel payout.** Winners on each market split the losers' stakes proportionally. No house, no counterparty.
- **90+ sources.** Crypto, weather, trains, earthquakes, GitHub, npm, Twitch, Steam, flights, nuclear reactors, ice cream machines.
- **BLS-verified resolution.** Three oracles fetch real data; a 2-of-3 aggregated BLS signature finalizes the result.
- **0.05% fee on profits only.** Break-even ~50.025%.

Jump in: [Getting Started](/blocks/getting-started) (~5 min).

## Each market thins the predators

Blocks refuses every standard extraction strategy by construction, not by policy. Spot is exposed to all eight; perps to seven; options to six; Blocks to zero.

Extraction strategies blocked: cross-venue arbitrage, frontrunning, stop hunting, orderflow buying, spoofing, latency races, insider trading, toxic-flow making.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blocks/predator-matrix.svg" alt="Predator extraction matrix — nine strategies across four venues (SPOT, PERPS, OPTIONS, BLOCKS). Spot is vulnerable to all eight predator strategies; perps refuses cross-venue arbitrage; options refuses stop-and-liquidation hunting in addition; Blocks refuses all eight by construction. The final row, directional bets, is the only honest activity and is possible at every venue. Eight ways to lose, one bet that still works." />
</Frame>

Bets are sealed before they hit the chain. No orderbook to spoof, no mempool to scan, no broker to sell your flow. Resolution comes from external data verified by BLS aggregate signatures (one short signature standing in for many oracles). Winners split the losers' pool minus 0.05%. The pool is parimutuel — no counterparty to fade you, because there is no counterparty.

## How a block resolves

Four steps, from browse to claim.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blocks/four-steps.svg" alt="The four steps — Browse, Seal, Resolve, Claim. Step 1 Browse: pick a batch from 90+ source categories like Crypto, Weather, Trains, Quakes. Step 2 Seal: bet UP or DOWN per market, sealed as keccak256(bitmap) on-chain so nobody can see your block until the tick closes. Step 3 Resolve: tick ends, oracles fetch real data, winners split the losers' stakes under 2-of-3 consensus. Step 4 Claim: withdraw winnings with a BLS-verified balance proof, any time, no lockup." />
</Frame>

<Info>
Your blocks are **sealed**. You submit a hash of your bitmap (your UP/DOWN votes packed into bits) on-chain, then reveal the bytes to oracles off-chain after the tick closes. Nobody sees or copies your bets in advance.
</Info>

## Why this and not the alternatives

Other venues let you take a directional view and then sell the right to copy it. Blocks cannot — placing a block is encrypted at the protocol level.

## The data pipeline

Ninety-eight external APIs feed a single Rust process; the process writes to Postgres; three oracles read snapshots and resolve ticks with BLS consensus; smart contracts redistribute USDC.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/blocks/data-pipeline.svg" alt="Data pipeline — external APIs (CoinGecko, USGS, TfL, GitHub, NDBC, and 85 others) feed into a single Rust data node that collects via HTTP, validates with bounds and staleness checks, and stores time-series rows in Postgres. Three independent oracles read snapshots, resolve ticks, BLS-sign their result, and reach 2-of-3 consensus. Smart contracts: VisionPool.sol holds sealed bitmaps, VisionClaim.sol verifies BLS claims, VisionReserve.sol custodies the parimutuel USDC pool." />
</Frame>

<Info>
**Naming exception.** On-chain contracts keep their original `Vision*` names from an earlier protocol identity. Product surface = Blocks. Bytecode = Vision. They are the same system.
</Info>
