# Risks & Operations

This page enumerates every way to lose money or break a bot on Vision. Read it before depositing real USDC.

Vision is a sealed parimutuel prediction market (winners split losers' stakes; no house counterparty). It is zero-sum minus a 0.05% fee on profits. Half of participants lose, by construction.

**Three risk categories below**

- Capital — losing your deposit through bad bets, fees, or burn rate
- Operational — bot bugs, RPC failures, missing bitmaps
- Smart contract — code that cannot be patched after deploy

---

## Capital Risks

### You Can Lose Your Entire Deposit

Vision is zero-sum minus fees. Every winning dollar comes from a losing dollar. There is no stop-loss. `stakePerTick` is deducted every tick whether you are paying attention or not. Consistent bad predictions trend your balance to zero.

### House Edge

The protocol takes 0.05% of profits — fee on the upside only. A 50/50 strategy loses to fees over time. Break-even win rate ~50.025%.

```
Break-even win rate ≈ 50.025% (with 0.05% fee on profits)
```

### Stake Burn Rate

Each tick risks `stakePerTick` USDC. Worst-case depletion math below.

```
Ticks until depleted = deposit / stakePerTick
Example: 100 USDC deposit, 1 USDC/tick stake → 100 ticks max
At 5-minute ticks = ~8 hours of runway
At 1-hour ticks = ~4 days of runway
```

<Warning>
A high `stakePerTick` relative to deposit burns capital in hours. Use a ratio of 1:50 or lower until your strategy is validated.
</Warning>

### Correlated Markets

Many markets in a block move together. BTC and ETH share direction most days. Bet all-UP on a correlated block during a drawdown: you lose on most markets simultaneously. Mix sources and directions to dilute correlation.

## Operational Risks

Engineering failure modes that cost real money. Each one has a known fix.

### Bitmap Not Submitted

Joining a block and failing to submit the bitmap means no predictions are revealed. You lose `stakePerTick` every tick anyway. Most common bot failure.

**Fix:** verify `{"accepted": true}` on every submission. Retry with 3-second backoff.

### Indexer Lag

After `joinBlock()` lands on-chain, the oracle needs ~6 sec to detect the `PlayerJoined` event. Earlier `POST /vision/bitmap` returns `404: Player not found`.

**Fix:** wait ≥6 sec between `joinBlock()` and bitmap submission. Retry on 404.

### Nonce Collisions

Multiple transactions sent in rapid succession without local nonce tracking collide and revert.

**Fix:** read `nonce = w3.eth.get_transaction_count(address)` once, then increment locally for back-to-back sends.

### RPC Failures

The Arbitrum RPC can drop briefly. A failed transaction mid-cycle leaves the bot inconsistent (USDC approved but block not joined, etc.).

**Fix:** wrap every on-chain call in retry logic. On restart, query on-chain state (has the bot already joined this block?) before re-attempting.

### Gas Exhaustion

Every on-chain action costs ETH. No ETH = silent revert on every transaction.

**Fix:** monitor ETH balance alongside USDC. Alert at 0.01 ETH.

### BLS Proof Unavailability

Claiming requires a BLS-signed proof from oracle nodes. Oracles down = claim fails. Funds are safe but stuck until oracles return.

**Fix:** retry with exponential backoff. Unclaimed rewards do not expire.

## Smart Contract Risks

Risks that live in the bytecode. Rare, but unfixable after deploy.

### Immutable Code

Vision contracts are deployed on Arbitrum without upgrade paths. A bug in contract logic could lose funds.

### Solvency

The contract includes a solvency check (`InsolventPayout`). If the USDC balance cannot cover a payout, the claim reverts. Should not happen under normal operation.

### BLS Key Rotation

If the oracle set rotates BLS keys, old proofs become invalid. Claim promptly. Do not leave profits in the contract for weeks.

## Pre-Flight Checklist

Run this before your first bet or first bot deployment. ~10 min.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fund Your Wallet">
    Your wallet needs both **USDC** (for deposits) and **ETH** (for gas) on Arbitrum.
    - USDC: At least 2x your planned deposit (to cover joins + additional deposits)
    - ETH: At least 0.05 ETH for gas (dozens of transactions)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Contract Addresses">
    Double-check the Vision contract address and USDC address match the [contract reference](/docs/blocks/reference/contract-addresses). Do not trust addresses from unofficial sources.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test with Minimum Stake">
    Join one block with the minimum: 0.1 USDC deposit, 0.1 USDC stake. Verify the full cycle works (join → submit bitmap → claim) before scaling up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate Bitmap Submission">
    After `joinBlock()`, confirm you receive `{"accepted": true}` from the bitmap endpoint. Check the oracle API: `GET /vision/balance/{block_id}/{your_address}` — it should show your position.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Exposure Limits">
    Decide your max total exposure across all blocks. The reference bot uses `max_exposure` and `max_blocks` config fields for this.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor Claim Cycle">
    Verify that `claimRewards()` succeeds at least once before increasing your deposit. A successful claim proves the full pipeline (predictions → resolution → BLS proofs → on-chain payout) is working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bot Operator Checklist

Production hygiene for an automated bot. ~30 min one-time setup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Process Management">
    Run the bot under a process manager (`systemd`, `pm2`, `supervisor`). It should auto-restart on crash.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log & Alert">
    Log every join, bitmap submission, claim, and error. Set up alerts for: repeated failures, low USDC balance, low ETH balance, bitmap rejection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Balance Monitoring">
    Track your USDC balance over time. If it's steadily declining, your strategy has no edge. Stop the bot and re-evaluate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="State Recovery on Restart">
    On restart, the bot should check which blocks it has already joined on-chain (via `getPosition()`) and rebuild its `joined_blocks` set. Never duplicate-join.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Periodic Withdrawal">
    Don't let profits accumulate indefinitely in the contract. Withdraw periodically to your own wallet. Claimed rewards are yours; unclaimed rewards depend on oracle availability.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Strategy Validation">
    Backtest your strategy against historical tick data before running live. Use `POST /vision/backtest` from the [strategies guide](/docs/blocks/bots/strategies#backtesting) to simulate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Multiple Oracle Endpoints">
    Submit your bitmap to all known oracle nodes, not just one. If one oracle is down, the others still store your bitmap. The bot needs at least 2/3 of oracles to accept the bitmap for it to participate in resolution.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Failure Modes & Recovery

| Failure | Symptom | Recovery |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Bitmap not submitted | Balance decreases every tick despite good predictions | Check bitmap submission logs, verify `accepted: true` |
| Submitted too early | `404 Player not found` | Wait 6+ seconds after `joinBlock()`, retry |
| Wrong bitmap hash | `400 Hash mismatch` | Recompute: `keccak256(bitmap_bytes)` must match `joinBlock(bitmapHash)` |
| Nonce collision | Transaction reverts | Track nonces locally, don't rely solely on `get_transaction_count` |
| Out of gas (ETH) | All txns fail | Top up ETH, set balance alert at 0.01 ETH |
| Out of USDC | `InsufficientDeposit` revert | Top up USDC, reduce `max_blocks` |
| Oracle unreachable | Bitmap submission timeout | Retry other oracle URLs, check network |
| Stale BLS proof | `InvalidBLSSignature` on claim | Re-fetch proof from oracle API |
| Bot already registered | `BotAlreadyRegistered` revert | Skip registration (idempotent, catch error) |
| Block paused | `BlockPaused` revert | Filter out paused blocks in poll loop |

## Risk Summary

All risks ranked by severity. Read the severity column before scaling up.

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|
| Losing deposit | **High** | Start small, validate strategy with backtests |
| Fee drag | Low | Maintain win rate above 50.025% |
| Bitmap failure | **High** | Verify `accepted: true`, retry logic |
| RPC downtime | Medium | Retry with backoff, check state on restart |
| Gas exhaustion | Medium | Monitor ETH balance, set alerts |
| Contract bug | Low | Limited to deposited amount |
| BLS proof delay | Low | Retries; unclaimed rewards don't expire |
