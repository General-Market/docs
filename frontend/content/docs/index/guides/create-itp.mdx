Deploy a custom Dex Traded Fund (DTF — a tokenized basket of crypto assets, like an on-chain ETF) in one transaction. ~5 min from asset picking to live market.

**Prereqs:**
- A Web3 wallet with USDC for gas on the Index L3
- An asset selection and target weights (5–50 assets recommended)
- A name and description for the DTF

**What you get:** a live market on the **Markets** page that anyone can trade. You earn fees on every buy and sell.

## Step-by-Step Creation

Four steps. ~5 min total.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1: Select assets">
    Pick from **100+ available crypto tokens**, organized by category (Layer 1, DeFi, AI, Memecoins, etc.).

    No minimum or maximum count enforced by the protocol. Well-diversified DTFs hold between 5 and 50 assets.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2: Set weights">
    Assign a percentage weight to each asset. Weights must sum to exactly **100%**.

    | Asset | Weight |
    |-------|--------|
    | BTC   | 50%    |
    | ETH   | 30%    |
    | SOL   | 20%    |

    <Tip>
    Click **Equal Weight** to distribute evenly across all selected assets, then fine-tune. Equal weight is the cleanest baseline.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3: Add metadata">
    Give the DTF a name and description. Two optional fields support discovery:
    - **Website URL** — link to a project page, blog post, or research document.
    - **YouTube video** — embed an explainer or thesis video on the DTF's market page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 4: Deploy">
    Click **Create DTF**. Confirm the transaction in your wallet. ~30 sec to confirm.

    The deployment transaction does three things:
    1. Registers the DTF on the Index contract on L3.
    2. Converts percentage weights into fixed per-share quantities.
    3. Sets the initial NAV (Net Asset Value — portfolio value per share) to **$1.00**.

    Result: the DTF appears on the **Markets** page and is tradeable immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Weights Become Quantities

At creation, the protocol converts your percentage weights into fixed per-share asset quantities. Quantities are what gets stored on-chain — weights are derived from them.

<WeightToQuantity />

These quantities define how much of each asset one DTF share represents. They **do not change** unless the creator triggers a rebalance — see [Rebalancing](/index/guides/rebalancing).

<Info>
The NAV formula is identical in three places — the Index contract on L3, the oracle nodes, and the frontend. Quantities are the source of truth. Weights are derived.

## Deployer Fees

You earn a fee on every buy and sell of your DTF.

- Denominated in USDC.
- Credited to the deployer's wallet automatically as trades settle.
- No claim transaction needed.

---

## After Deployment

Once the transaction confirms, four things are true:

- The DTF appears on the **Markets** page alongside all other DTFs.
- Anyone with USDC can buy and sell shares.
- Real-time NAV, price charts, and trading volume are visible on the DTF's detail page.
- The deployer earns fees on every trade.

<Note>
DTF creation is irreversible. Verify asset selection and weights before deploying. You can rebalance weights later — see [Rebalancing](/index/guides/rebalancing) — but you cannot delete the DTF.
</Note>
