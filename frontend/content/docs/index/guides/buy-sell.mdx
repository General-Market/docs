Buy or sell a Dex Traded Fund (DTF — a tokenized basket of crypto assets, like an on-chain ETF) in one wallet flow. ~3–5 min end to end.

**Prereqs:**
- A Web3 wallet (e.g. MetaMask) connected to [generalmarket.io](https://generalmarket.io)
- USDC in your wallet on Arbitrum, Base, or the Index L3 (the protocol's own Layer 3 chain)
- Enough USDC to cover the trade amount plus gas

<Snippet file="chain-info.mdx" />

<Tip>
You do not need to be on the Index L3 to trade. The protocol bridges USDC from Arbitrum or Base to the L3 and back automatically. You pay gas on whichever chain you start from.
</Tip>

---

## Buying a DTF

Open the **Markets** page. Pick a DTF. Click **Buy**. Three steps follow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1: Enter amount">
    Type the USDC amount. The estimated number of DTF shares appears below, calculated from the current NAV (Net Asset Value — portfolio value per share).

    The share count is an estimate. Final fill price is set at execution.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2: Configure slippage">
    Set a slippage tolerance — the price deviation you accept between quote and execution. See [Slippage settings](#slippage-settings) below.

    Default is 1.0% (Normal). For volatile or low-liquidity DTFs, raise it to Relaxed (3.0%).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3: Submit order">
    The wallet prompts twice:
    1. **Approve** the USDC spend allowance (first time only, or if the amount exceeds the previous approval).
    2. **Confirm** the buy transaction, which locks USDC into the BridgeProxy contract.

    Result: the order enters the processing pipeline. ~2–8 min until shares appear. See the pipeline table below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Order processing pipeline

Three stages, all automatic after Step 3. Status visible on the **Portfolio** page.

| Stage | What happens | Status | Time |
|-------|-------------|--------|------|
| **Submit** | USDC spend approved and order submitted to BridgeProxy on L1/L2 | Wallet confirmation | ~30 sec |
| **Process** | USDC bridges to L3, order is relayed, blocked by oracle nodes via BLS (Boneless–Lynn–Shacham — a multi-signature consensus scheme), AP (Authorized Participant — the off-chain fill agent) buys underlying assets on exchanges | Automatic | 1–5 min |
| **Deliver** | Collateral recorded on-chain, bridged back to origin chain, bridge finalized, DTF shares minted to the buyer's wallet | Automatic | 1–3 min |

<Info>
Order processing is fully automatic after Step 3. Close the modal — the order does not need your attention. Track progress on the **Portfolio** page.
</Info>

### Real-time order tracking

The UI streams live status updates via Server-Sent Events (SSE). Three signals are exposed:

- Current processing step and stage
- Estimated time remaining
- Transaction hashes for on-chain steps

## Selling a DTF

Open the **Portfolio** page or the DTF's market page. Click **Sell**. Three steps, same shape as buying.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1: Enter share amount">
    Type the number of DTF shares to sell, or click **Max** to liquidate the full position. Estimated USDC proceeds appear below, calculated from the current NAV. Final amount depends on execution price.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2: Configure slippage">
    Same mechanics as buying — set the tolerance for price movement between quote and fill. See [Slippage settings](#slippage-settings).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3: Submit sell order">
    The wallet prompts twice:
    1. **Approve** the DTF share transfer (first time only per DTF).
    2. **Confirm** the sell transaction.

    Result: order enters the same three-stage pipeline (Submit → Process → Deliver). USDC arrives in your wallet after Deliver completes. ~2–8 min total.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Sell orders use the same bridge and settlement pipeline as buys. USDC proceeds arrive after processing completes — typically 2–8 minutes.
</Warning>

## Slippage Settings

Slippage = the gap between the price you saw at quote and the price you got at execution. Three presets cover most cases.

| Setting | Tolerance | Best for |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Tight** | 0.3% | Stable, high-liquidity DTFs |
| **Normal** | 1.0% | Most orders (default) |
| **Relaxed** | 3.0% | Volatile or low-liquidity DTFs, large orders |

<Tip>
If an order fails due to slippage, escrowed USDC is returned automatically — you only lose the gas. Retry with a higher tolerance, or wait for calmer market conditions.
</Tip>
