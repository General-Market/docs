# Settlement Chain

Configure both chains in your wallet, learn the USDC decimal difference, and know which chain handles which operation. ~5 min setup, one-time.

**Two chains, one protocol:**
- **Index L3** — the protocol's own Layer 3 chain. Trading, NAV updates, Vision bets.
- **Settlement** (Sonic Testnet) — Bridge trading, lending (Morpho), Vision deposits.

**What you will do:**
- Add both networks to your wallet.
- Understand the USDC decimal difference (18 on L3, 6 on Settlement).
- Know which operations require which chain.

## When You Need Each Chain

Most activity happens on L3. Settlement is required only for bridge trading, lending, and Vision deposits. The app prompts your wallet to switch when needed.

## USDC Decimals

<Warning>
**USDC uses different decimal precision on each chain.** Understand this before transferring USDC across chains.

- **L3 USDC** = **18 decimals** (like ETH).
- **Settlement USDC** = **6 decimals** (standard ERC-20 USDC).

The bridge converts between the two automatically. **Never** send raw USDC between chains outside the bridge interface — the 10^12 difference between 6 and 18 decimals will destroy or inflate value catastrophically.
</Warning>

What 100 USDC looks like in raw token units on each chain:

<UsdcDecimals />

The bridge handles this conversion transparently. Deposit 100 USDC on Settlement → the protocol credits 100 USDC of value on L3. The decimal conversion is internal and never exposed in the UI.

## How Tokens Appear on Each Chain

When you buy a Dex Traded Fund (DTF — a tokenized basket of crypto assets, like an on-chain ETF) through the bridge, you receive matched tokens on both chains:
- DTF shares on L3.
- BridgedITP tokens on Settlement (a 1:1 representation of the same position).

<BridgeFlow />

<Info>
BridgedITP tokens are full ERC-20 tokens on Settlement. They represent the same position as your L3 shares and can be used as collateral in Morpho lending markets. The bridge enforces the invariant that the two representations are always equal.
</Info>

## Common Operations by Chain

### On L3 (Chain ID 111222333)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy a DTF">
    Open **Markets**, select a DTF, enter a USDC amount, confirm the buy. The order goes through BLS (Boneless–Lynn–Shacham — a multi-signature consensus scheme) and fills within seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sell a DTF">
    Open your **Portfolio**, select a position, enter the number of shares to sell, confirm. USDC returns to your L3 wallet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place a Vision bet">
    Open the **Vision** section, pick a market, choose an outcome, enter the bet amount, confirm. Winnings are claimable on L3 after market resolution.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On Settlement (Chain ID 14601)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bridge-buy a DTF">
    Deposit USDC on Settlement to buy DTF shares. The bridge escrows your USDC, oracles execute the trade, and you receive both DTF shares on L3 and BridgedITP on Settlement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lend USDC">
    Open the **Lend** section, deposit USDC into the Morpho vault. Your USDC earns yield from borrowers immediately. No lock-up period.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Borrow against DTF">
    Deposit BridgedITP shares as collateral in a Morpho lending market, borrow USDC. Monitor your health factor to avoid liquidation — see [Risks](/index/guides/risks).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deposit USDC for Vision">
    Vision prediction markets require USDC deposited on Settlement. Approve USDC, confirm the deposit transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>
