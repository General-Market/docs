# Order Lifecycle

Every buy or sell order travels through six on-chain steps across two chains (L3 and Settlement), gated by BLS (Boneh-Lynn-Shacham aggregate signature) consensus and a 0.1% price tolerance. End-to-end settlement completes in under 10 seconds.

- Two chains: L3 (Chain ID `111222333`) for orders, Settlement (Chain ID `14601`) for USDC custody
- Four BLS consensus rounds per order
- 0.1% maximum AP fill deviation from reference price
- 60-second order timeout with up to 3 retries before refund

## Buy Flow

Six steps from your USDC to your DTF (Dex Traded Fund) shares:

<Steps>
  <Step title="User Submits Buy on L3">
    `submitOrder(itpId, amount, BUY)`. Your USDC is **escrowed** on L3. It is no longer yours, not yet theirs — held by the protocol until consensus completes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Oracle Bridges Buy to Settlement">
    Oracle nodes detect the pending buy, reach BLS consensus, and relay the order to the Settlement chain. The intent crosses the chain boundary with a cryptographic signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="completeBuyOrder Releases USDC to AP">
    `completeBuyOrder()` runs on Settlement with a valid BLS signature. The contract verifies the signature and releases the escrowed USDC to the AP (Authorized Participant) vault. This step **must succeed** before any shares are minted. The backing invariant is enforced here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AP Buys Underlying Assets">
    The AP receives the USDC and buys the underlying assets on **Bitget** at real market prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="confirmFills Mints Shares on L3">
    `confirmFills()` runs on L3. The contract checks each fill against the **0.1% price tolerance**. If it passes, DTF shares are **minted** to your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="BridgedITP Minted on Settlement">
    `mintBridgedShares()` runs on Settlement with BLS verification. A BridgedITP token (ERC-20 replica of your L3 shares) is minted on Settlement. You hold the same position on both chains.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sell Flow

The buy flow in reverse, with USDC arriving on Settlement instead of L3:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit Sell Order on L3">
    `submitOrder(itpId, amount, SELL)`. Your DTF shares are **escrowed** on L3.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Oracle Bridges Sell to Settlement">
    Oracle nodes detect the pending sell, reach BLS consensus, and relay the order to the Settlement chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AP Sells Underlying Assets">
    The AP receives the `TradeRequest` and sells the underlying assets on **Bitget**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AP Returns USDC to Settlement Custody">
    The AP deposits the USDC proceeds into the Settlement custody contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="completeSellOrder Transfers USDC to User">
    `completeSellOrder()` runs on Settlement with a valid BLS signature. The contract transfers USDC from the AP vault to your wallet on Settlement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="confirmFills Burns Shares on L3">
    `confirmFills()` runs on L3. The escrowed DTF shares are **burned**. The position ceases to exist on both chains.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cycle Timing

Oracle nodes run a 1-second, 5-phase cycle continuously:

<OrderCycleGantt />

| Phase | Duration | What Happens |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **COLLECT** | 200ms | Oracle nodes scan for new pending orders on-chain |
| **PROPOSE** | 200ms | Leader proposes a block of collected orders |
| **SIGN_SUBMIT** | 200ms | Peer oracle nodes validate and BLS-sign the block; leader aggregates and submits |
| **CONFIRM** | 200ms | Block confirmation transaction is processed on-chain |
| **REBALANCE** | 200ms | Any pending rebalance operations are executed |

<Info>
An order is blocked within 1–2 seconds. Full settlement, including exchange execution, completes in under 10 seconds.
</Info>

## Order States

Four states, no ambiguity:

<OrderStateMachine />

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `PENDING` | Order submitted, USDC/tokens escrowed. Waiting for next cycle. |
| `BLOCKED` | Included in a confirmed block with valid BLS signature. AP is executing. |
| `FILLED` | AP reported fills, tokens minted/burned, settlement complete. |
| `TIMEOUT` | Order was not filled within 60 seconds. Retried up to 3 times before escrowed funds are returned. |

## Safeguards

Four protections sit between your money and a bad outcome:

### BLS Consensus

Every block requires a supermajority: `ceil(2n/3)` of registered oracles. Three nodes → at least two must sign. Twenty nodes → at least fourteen.

<Warning>
BLS verification is **never bypassed** — not in production, not in testing, not in local development. There is no test mode and no override.
</Warning>

### Price Tolerance

The AP must execute within **0.1%** of the reference price. `confirmFills()` validates every fill on-chain. Fills outside the band are rejected.

```text
|reference_price - fill_price| / reference_price <= 0.001
```

### Order Timeout

If an order is not filled within 60 seconds, it enters timeout:

1. First timeout: automatic retry
2. Second timeout: automatic retry
3. Third timeout: order cancelled, escrowed funds refunded

<Note>
Timeouts are rare. They exist for the moments when exchanges go dark or networks congest.
</Note>

### Oracle-AP Separation

Oracle nodes and the AP cannot communicate directly. Oracles read on-chain fill data only. This separation prevents collusion between the consensus layer and the execution layer.

## Netting

Buyers and sellers in the same cycle cancel each other out. $10,000 in buys against $7,000 in sells means only $3,000 net goes to the exchange. The protocol matches internally first, sending only the residual to external venues. Less trading, less cost, less exchange exposure.
