# Bridge Architecture

The bridge moves USDC and DTF shares between L3 and Settlement, enforcing one rule: no DTF share is minted without confirmed underlying collateral.

Key terms:

- **L3** — Index's Arbitrum Orbit chain, 18-decimal USDC.
- **Settlement** — Arbitrum, 6-decimal USDC, where bridged DTF shares live as ERC-20s.
- **AP** — Authorized Participant. The keeper that executes the asset purchase on Bitget after USDC is released.
- **BLS quorum** — `ceil(2n/3)` aggregated signatures from registered oracle nodes; required for every state-changing bridge call.

## Decimal Conversion

The bridge converts decimals at the chain boundary: 6 on Settlement, 18 on L3.

<BridgeDecimalConversion />

<Warning>
USDC is not 6 decimals everywhere. L3 uses 18-decimal USDC. A decimal mismatch in bridge logic ships users 1e12× too much or too little.
</Warning>

## Bridge Buy Flow

Ten steps, four BLS consensus rounds, two chains. The invariant: USDC must reach the AP on Settlement before any share is minted on L3.

<BridgeBuyFlow />

<Warning>
**The Backing Invariant:** `completeBuyOrder` on Settlement must succeed before `mintBridgedShares` is called. If `completeBuyOrder` fails (gas, revert, timeout), the order is rolled back via `refundBuyOrder` — USDC returns to the user and zero shares are minted. There is no optimistic minting.
</Warning>

### Crash Recovery

`pendingMints` in `SettlementBridgeCustody` is the recovery anchor. On restart, oracles query it to find orders where USDC was released but `BridgedITP` was not yet minted, then resume.

## The Backing Invariant

Every minted DTF share is 1:1 backed by underlying assets. The bridge is the contract that enforces this.

<BridgeBackingInvariant />

<Warning>
**Never mint DTF shares without confirmed backing.** The bridge enforces this by requiring `completeBuyOrder` (which releases escrowed USDC to the AP for asset purchases) to succeed before `mintBridgedShares` is called. If `completeBuyOrder` reverts, the oracles execute `refundBuyOrder` — USDC returns to the user, zero shares are minted.
</Warning>
