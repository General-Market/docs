## Milestones this round funds

The investor is buying a path to the next round. Here it is.

1. **By month 3** — mainnet live with audited contracts.
2. **By month 6** — first distribution shipped; data-native traders producing real settled volume.
3. **By month 12** — settled volume ramping toward the $10M/month base case.
4. **By month 18** — $50k MRR, Series-A-ready metrics.

Each milestone has a number. "Launch mainnet" is not a milestone. "Mainnet live with audited contracts by month 3" is.

## Why this amount

$400k buys exactly the path above: two engineers, mainnet, a legal wrapper, and the first distribution — with eighteen months of runway to reach $50k MRR. Less would force a choice between shipping mainnet and reaching users. More would dilute the team for milestones this round does not need. The machine is built; the raise funds the launch and the first users, nothing speculative.
