# Solution & Traction

## The three primitives

| Primitive | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Sealed bets** | Orders stay encrypted until the round closes | Nothing to front-run, no flow to leak or sell |
| **Parimutuel pools** | Winners split the losers' pool | No order book to attack, no house on the other side |
| **BLS-verified oracles** | Three independent oracles sign every resolution | No single point of capture, no oracle gaming |

These compose. Sealed inputs feed parimutuel pricing; parimutuel pricing feeds BLS-signed resolution. The surface a cheater needs simply isn't there.

## Two mechanisms

The seal defeats the front-runner: you cannot step in front of an order you cannot see.

The block defeats the insider: every trade is forced across thousands of markets at once. An insider can move one market. He cannot move five hundred thousand. And because the block is netted, the collateral required to back it collapses far below the naive sum — the freed capital is what lets liquidity reach a billion markets cheaply.

> Stand behind the trader, and the predators have nothing to stand on.
