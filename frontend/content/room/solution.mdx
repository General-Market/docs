## The three primitives

| Primitive | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Sealed bets** | Orders encrypted until round closes | No front-running, no flow leakage |
| **Parimutuel pools** | Winners split losers' pool | No order book to attack, no market maker dependence |
| **BLS-verified oracles** | Three independent oracles sign every resolution | No single point of capture, no oracle gaming |

These compose. Sealed inputs feed parimutuel pricing. Parimutuel pricing feeds BLS-signed resolution. The cheat surface collapses.
