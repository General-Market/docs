# Due Diligence

> **What goes on this page:** Document index for the legal, financial, IP, HR, and compliance materials. Most of this gets uploaded as PDFs or linked to a shared Drive. This page is the table of contents.

## How to use this section

Investors typically only ask for due-diligence materials after a term sheet is signed or close to signed. This page exists so that when they do ask, the materials are organized and ready. **Don't share anything in this section until the investor has crossed that threshold.**

If you're sharing this room before that point, you can either:
- Delete this page from the sidebar (move it out of `content/room/<slug>/`)
- Or leave it visible but mark documents as "Available on request"
