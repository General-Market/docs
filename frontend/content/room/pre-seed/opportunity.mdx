## The problem

Prediction markets are broken in three specific ways:

1. **Front-running.** Centralized order books leak flow to operators, market makers, and anyone watching the wire.
2. **Geographic exclusion.** KYC and licensing carve the global population into eligible and ineligible. Most of the world cannot bet on most events.
3. **Resolution gaming.** A single oracle is a single point of capture. Whoever controls the oracle controls the payout.

Each of these is treated as inevitable. None of them is.
