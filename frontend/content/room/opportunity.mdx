# The Opportunity

## The problem

Ninety percent of traders quit. They will tell you the market beat them. It didn't. The venue did.

Walk one hundred trades through any modern venue and watch where the money goes:

- The **insider** moves before the news is public, and takes the first cut.
- The **front-runner** reads your order off the wire and steps in front of it.
- The **house market maker** prices against you, because the house is the counterparty.

By the time the position is yours, the edge is gone. A two-percent leak per trade compounds, quietly, into an eighty-percent loss. The trader feels stupid and leaves. *He was not stupid. He was farmed.*

This is not a flaw in one product. It is the business model of the whole stack — perps, options, prediction markets, launchpads. Each one is the house.

## Why now

Three things changed in the last twenty-four months.

- **Retail trades through code.** AI trading-tool adoption rose +340% from 2022 to 2025 (eToro reports +75% in a single year). Code chooses venues on math, not marketing, and the math favors the venue that doesn't skim.
- **Algorithms became the majority.** In India, algorithmic orders are now the majority of stock-market trades (NSE, 2025). The behavior is already mainstream. It only needs a venue built for it.
- **The rails matured.** L2 and L3 chains finally have the throughput and finality for sealed-bet settlement that was impossible in 2021.

> Code routes to the venue that doesn't skim. General is built for code.
