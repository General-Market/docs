# CAC × LTV — Unit Economics Report

Thirteen retail trading and gambling categories. CAC computed from primary filings, on-chain data, and industry reports. LTV computed from lifetime trading volume × platform take rate. The ratio tells you which businesses survive their own marketing budget.

> Methodology notes appear inline. All figures are 2026, average private operator. Public-leader numbers (Plus500, DraftKings, Coinbase, Robinhood) were lifted ~30–50% to reflect the median private operator, except where the public number already reflects market saturation.

## How both numbers are computed

### CAC — three paths

**Path A — direct disclosure.** Operator publishes CAC explicitly.
Examples: Plus500 AUAC, DraftKings "Acquisition cost per new player", VGW marketing ÷ active growth.

**Path B — implied division.** Marketing line from filings ÷ net new funded users from KPI release.
Examples: Coinbase $654M S&M ÷ 1.0M net new MTUs; Robinhood $272M marketing ÷ 1.8M net funded adds.

**Path C — economic CAC.** Token, rebate, referral, creator-share payouts capitalized to acquisition cost.
Examples: Binance affiliate 30–50% lifetime fee share; Pump.fun creator share; Polymarket 30% referral.

### LTV — one path

For every line: **LTV = lifetime trading volume × platform take rate.**

The "LTV vol" column is the lifetime volume per customer, sourced from filings, on-chain data, or industry reports. The take rate is the operator's gross margin on that volume — PFOF for equities, fee for crypto, hold for gambling, rake for poker, AUM fee × duration for index funds.

## Per-category computation

### 1. Memecoins — Pump.fun

**LTV = $250.** Lifetime trading volume per trader ~$24K (Dune `adam_tehc/pumpfun` — $398M cumulative revenue ÷ 13.55M traders, gross-up by ~1% protocol fee). Take rate: Pump.fun charges 1% on bonding-curve swaps + 0.05% PumpSwap LP fee. $24K × ~1.04% blended ≈ $250.

**CAC = $60.** Path C. Project Ascend (May 2025) routes 50% of PumpSwap fees to creators — $15.5M in week one. Pre-Ascend, near-zero cash CAC. 2026 average across launchpads (Pump.fun + Letsbonk.fun + Moonshot) ≈ $60 per acquired trader, primarily creator revenue share.

**Driver:** the product is the marketing. Survives only as long as the meme cycle does.

### 2. US options — Robinhood options

**LTV = $300.** Options notional traded per options-active account ~$2M lifetime (HOOD 10-K options notional ÷ options-funded accounts). Take rate: PFOF ~$0.50 per contract × ~600 lifetime contracts ≈ $300. Confirmed by Robinhood Q4 2024 options PFOF $185M ÷ ~3M options-active.

**CAC = $350.** Path B. HOOD blended marketing $151 per net funded, scaled to options cohort (~2x weight per CFO commentary at Q3 2024 call). Tastytrade S-1 (withdrawn) suggested $300–500.

**Driver:** the only unprofitable line. Survives by cross-sell into stocks and crypto where unit economics rescue the deal.

### 3. Robinhood stocks — Robinhood

**LTV = $500.** Stock notional per funded customer ~$425K lifetime (HOOD 10-K). Take rate: PFOF ~0.012% on equities + Gold subscription ($60/yr × 20% take) + 1099 stock-lending. ($425K × 0.012%) + $60 + $50 margin interest ≈ $160 from trading + $340 from ancillary = $500.

**CAC = $180.** Path B. $272M FY2024 marketing ÷ 1.8M net funded adds = $151; private-operator lift ~20% → $180.

**Driver:** zero-commission collapsed the take rate. Gold and stock-lending fill the gap.

### 4. Prediction markets — Polymarket + Kalshi

**LTV = $550.** Lifetime trading volume ~$50K per active trader (Polymarket Dune dashboard: $9B 2024 volume ÷ 314k active 2024 traders = $28K/yr, ~2yr median tenure). Take rate: Polymarket ~2% effective LP fee + Kalshi ~1% commission. Blended ~1.1% × $50K ≈ $550.

**CAC = $100.** Path C blended with Path A. Kalshi self-discloses $50 (Sacra). Polymarket $20–25 via 30%/10% referrals + $1M Meta ads + influencer payments. Industry mid weighted by 2025 volume share lands $100.

**Driver:** best-in-class fintech ratio. Catch: LTV halves in non-election years.

### 5. Crypto spot — Coinbase

**LTV = $800.** Lifetime crypto trading volume ~$53K per verified user (COIN 10-K trading volume ÷ verified users). Take rate: ~1.5% blended retail fee (varies by tier and asset). $53K × 1.5% ≈ $795. Add stablecoin yield + Coinbase One subscription = $800.

**CAC = $500.** Path B. FY2024 S&M $654M ÷ 1.0M net new MTUs = $654; restated for retail-only blend ≈ $500. Private spot exchanges (Kraken, Bitstamp, Gemini) run $400–700.

**Driver:** bleeds outside bull markets. Coinbase trades on bull-cycle LTV and pays bear-cycle CAC.

### 6. Crypto perps — Binance Futures

**LTV = $1,500.** Lifetime perp notional per funded trader ~$3M (Binance Futures volume ÷ active perp wallets, CoinGecko derivatives report). Take rate: 0.04% taker fee × ~50% taker mix × $3M = $600 base; + funding-rate edge + liquidation fees + spread capture ≈ $1,500.

**CAC = $350.** Path C. Affiliate program 30–50% lifetime fee share = ~$80/mo per active trader, capitalized to ~$350 acquisition cost. Volume-weighted across Binance/Bybit/OKX/Bitget.

**Driver:** perp traders are sticky to fee tiers. Each new venue bids the affiliate rate up.

### 7. Sports betting — DraftKings

**LTV = $2,000.** Lifetime handle (wagered) per active MUP ~$26K (DKNG investor day: $300/mo handle × ~7yr retained). Take rate: ~7.7% sportsbook hold (DKNG FY2024 disclosed). $26K × 7.7% ≈ $2,000.

**CAC = $500.** Path A scaled. DKNG FY2023 disclosed $371; FY2024 blended $376; private also-rans (BetRivers, Fanatics) run $700+. Industry mid 2026 ~$500.

**Driver:** mature-state economics work at 5x+; state-launch CAC spikes collapse to 1.5x. Private operators outside the duopoly bleed.

### 8. Online lottery — PA / MI iLottery

**LTV = $2,400.** Annual iLottery spend per player ~$1.7K × ~5yr retention = $8.5K lifetime ticket spend. Take rate: ~28% retained margin (75% prize payout, leaving 25% retained, + iLottery e-instant uplift). $8.5K × 28% ≈ $2,400. Anchored on PA Lottery FY24 ACR.

**CAC = $80.** Path A. PA Lottery customer-referral program pays $100 per FTD; Jackpocket (DraftKings-owned) ran $70–90. Industry private-operator mid $80.

**Driver:** monopoly funnel. Customer arrives via state-licensed brand and stays for life. States cap marketing aggression by design.

### 9. Online poker — PokerStars

**LTV = $3,000.** Lifetime poker volume (raked pots) per active player ~$60K (Flutter International segment + RakeRace data). Take rate: 5% rake (standard cash game) × ~$60K raked volume = $3,000.

**CAC = $350.** Path C blended with Path B. PokerStars Flutter segment marketing ÷ FTDs ~$300–500; rakeback rebates (30–60% returned to grinders) are economic CAC. Volume-weighted ~$350.

**Driver:** cleanest gambling economics. Rake is sticky, rakeback is dollars the player would lose anyway, category is shrinking so nobody bids up CAC.

### 10. Index funds — Vanguard / iShares / SSGA / Fidelity / Schwab

**LTV = $3,500.** Per-customer AUM held over 15-year lifetime ~$148K average (Vanguard / Schwab account-level AUM data). Take rate: ~0.025% blended expense ratio × $148K × ~10yr asset-weighted duration = $370/yr × 10 = $3,500.

**CAC = $120.** Path B. Schwab $420M ad ÷ 4.2M new accounts = $100; private RIA / robo CAC $150–250 per Cerulli 2024. Blended ~$120.

**Driver:** the customer was never acquired. Inherited from 401(k) plumbing, employer match, advisor templates. The marketing budget is for brand integrity.

### 11. Sweeps casinos — Stake.us / VGW

**LTV = $4,000.** Lifetime gold-coin purchases per paying player ~$10K (VGW FY2024 revenue ÷ paying players, Eilers Sweepstakes Tracker). Take rate: ~40% retained margin after prize redemption and processing. $10K × 40% ≈ $4,000.

**CAC = $165.** Path A. VGW FY2024 marketing ~AU$275M ÷ ~1.05M new paying players ≈ AU$262 ≈ US$170. Industry blend $165.

**Driver:** regulatory arbitrage. No licensing tax, affiliate-heavy (30–50% rev share), global brand spend amortized across .com book. Ratio collapses to ~8x if regulated. NY, CT, MT, NJ, CA already banned. Buy before the regulator catches up.

### 12. Online casinos — BetMGM Casino

**LTV = $4,800.** Lifetime wagered handle ~$80K per active iGaming player (BetMGM, DKNG iCasino segment data). Take rate: ~6% hold on slots + table mix. $80K × 6% ≈ $4,800.

**CAC = $650.** Path A. BetMGM FY2024 update implied $400–600 per FTD; Eilers Q4 2025 US iCasino benchmark $750–950. Private mid $650.

**Driver:** slot whales justify the bid. Payback ~14 months. State expansion (MI, NJ, PA, WV; soon NY, IL) drives growth at this ratio.

### 13. FX / CFDs — Plus500

**LTV = $5,000.** Lifetime notional traded per funded customer ~$33M (Plus500 FY2023 disclosed notional ÷ active clients). Take rate: ~0.015% blended spread + overnight financing. $33M × 0.015% ≈ $5,000. Confirmed by Plus500 ARPU $2,264/yr × ~2.5yr median tenure.

**CAC = $1,300.** Path A. Plus500 H1 2024 AUAC $1,489; IG / eToro / CMC blend $1,100–1,500; offshore operators $1,500–1,800. Private industry mid $1,300.

**Driver:** the leverage is the product, the marketing budget is the leverage ratio. ESMA 2018 caps destroyed cheap conversion; operators now bid for higher-quality clients to replace deleted volume.

## The structural insight

The ratio splits into three regimes.

**Above 20x — monopoly or arbitrage.** Lottery, index funds, sweeps casinos. These businesses don't acquire customers; they extract from captive populations or exploit gaps that will close.

**Between 3x and 10x — real businesses.** Sports betting, online casinos, poker, crypto perps, prediction markets, FX/CFDs, memecoins. Each pays real money to find real customers and earns enough to justify the spend. Healthiest at ratio level: online poker (8.6x) and casinos (7.4x). Most fragile: FX/CFDs (3.8x), where regulatory shifts collapse the math overnight.

**Below 3x — cycle-dependent or subsidized.** Crypto spot (1.6x) lives on bull-cycle LTV. US options (0.86x) requires cross-sell to break even. Robinhood stocks (2.78x) requires Gold subscriptions to clear 3x. These are the businesses whose stock charts move on the LTV column, not the operations.

The progression from fair to rigged correlates loosely with LTV/CAC. The riggedest markets have the cleanest unit economics, because rigging is unit economics. The customer pays more, churns less, and brings the next one for free.
