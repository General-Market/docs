# General

**The anti-cheat of all traders.** The first exchange that cannot cheat you — and the first that can carry liquidity to a billion markets at once.

<YouTube id="nIw_bIKk9BI" title="General — pitch overview" />

## Sixty seconds

Every trading venue you have used is the house. Perps desks, options books, prediction markets, launchpads — each one sees your order before the public does, and each takes its cut on the way through. Insiders move first. Front-runners read the wire. The market maker is the operator. You don't lose because you were wrong. You lose because someone saw the cards.

General sits *behind* the trader and behind every market. Orders are sealed until the round closes, so there is nothing to front-run. Pools are parimutuel, so there is no house to take the other side. Resolution is signed by a BLS oracle quorum, so no single party owns the payout. Cheating is not discouraged here. It is structurally impossible.

And because a single sealed block prices thousands of markets at once, liquidity costs almost nothing to provide. A venue that pays a market maker millions per market dies of its own fees. A venue that nets a whole block lives on two users. *That* is how one product reaches a billion markets.

> Remove the skim, and the only edge left is being right.
