# The Business Model

> **What goes on this page:** Revenue model, unit economics, projections. The financial model itself can be a link to a spreadsheet — but the headline numbers and assumptions live on this page.

## How we make money

Replace with the real revenue model. Two or three sentences. Then a bulleted breakdown:

- **Transaction fee:** X% of every settled market, taken from the winning pool
- **Spread on parimutuel pricing:** Y% baked into payout math
- **Premium features:** Bot API access, advanced analytics, custom market creation — pricing TBD
- **Treasury yield:** Settlement collateral earns yield in stable-asset vaults while pending resolution

State the primary revenue driver clearly. If 80% of revenue comes from one mechanism, say so.

## Unit economics

> **Format:** A single table with the key per-user or per-market economics. Investors will read this twice.

| Metric | Today | At scale (target) |
|---|---|---|
| Average market volume | $X | $Y |
| Take rate | Z% | Z% |
| Revenue per active user / month | $A | $B |
| Cost per active user / month | $C | $D |
| Contribution margin | E% | F% |
| Payback period | G months | H months |

Cite assumptions for every "at scale" column. Bare projections without assumptions look amateurish.

## Revenue projections

> **What goes here:** 3-year revenue forecast. Conservative, base, aggressive. Replace placeholders with real numbers from the financial model.

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | $X | $Y | $Z |
| **Base** | $A | $B | $C |
| **Aggressive** | $D | $E | $F |

State the key assumption that differentiates each scenario. Examples:
- Conservative: organic growth only, no paid acquisition
- Base: paid acquisition starts month 6, $X CAC
- Aggressive: major exchange listing or licensing deal in year 2
