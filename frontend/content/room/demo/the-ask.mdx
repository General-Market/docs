## Milestones this round funds

The investor is buying a path to the next round. Define that path.

1. **By month 3** — Replace with measurable milestone (e.g., "mainnet live with audited contracts")
2. **By month 6** — Replace with milestone (e.g., "$X cumulative volume, Y weekly active addresses")
3. **By month 12** — Replace with milestone (e.g., "first major exchange integration, ARR of $Z")
4. **By month 18** — Replace with milestone tied to the next round (e.g., "Series A-ready metrics")

Each milestone should have a number attached. "Launch mainnet" is not a milestone — "Mainnet live with $X TVL and audited contracts by Q3 2026" is.

## Why this amount

One paragraph. Justify the size of the raise. Why not less (would extend runway but cap growth)? Why not more (would dilute team unnecessarily, and we don't need it for these specific milestones)? Investors respect founders who can defend the exact number.
