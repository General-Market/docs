# General Market

Prediction markets that can't be cheated.

## The problem

Every centralized prediction market leaks. Order books get front-run. Operators see flow before the public does. KYC creates two markets — one for the credentialed, one for everyone else.

## The mechanism

- **Sealed bets.** Orders are encrypted until the round closes. No one — not even the operator — sees them in flight.
- **Parimutuel pools.** No order book to attack. Winners split losers' pool. Liquidity is the crowd, not a market maker.
- **BLS-verified oracles.** Three independent oracles sign every resolution. No single oracle, no single point of capture.

Cheating isn't discouraged. It's structurally impossible.
