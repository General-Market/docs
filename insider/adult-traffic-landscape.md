## 4. Social-graph NSFW (Reddit, X/Twitter)

Different animal. These are social graphs that tolerate porn, then profit from the engagement.

| Platform | Total monthly visits | NSFW share | Implied NSFW traffic |
|---|---|---|---|
| X / Twitter | ~5–6B | ~13% of posts adult (Reuters 2022 leak) | ~700–800M/mo |
| Reddit | ~2–3B | 5% of submissions NSFW; 96 of top 100 most-interconnected subs are NSFW | ~150–300M/mo, concentrated |

X officially legalized adult content in June 2024. Reddit went the other way in July 2025 — UK age verification under the Online Safety Act. One platform leans into the funnel, the other builds a wall.

Reddit and X don't compete with Pornhub. They feed OnlyFans and MYM. A creator posts teaser clips to social graphs (free), converts a fraction (1–2%) to paid subscriptions. The social graph is the top of the funnel. Pornhub is the bottom of someone else's funnel. OnlyFans sits in the middle and takes 20%.

## 5. What the numbers reveal

Tubes eat everything. One Pornhub equals ten OnlyFans, fifty Stripchats, one hundred Toomics, one thousand FYPTTs.

OnlyFans is the only subscription platform in the same order of magnitude as a top tube, and it still trails Xvideos by a factor of seven.

MYM is a French regional affair. It generates European millionaires and remains invisible in American traffic reports.

AI porn is the category everyone underestimates because no single site is large. The aggregate is already meaningful. The CAGR is vertical. Over a decade it will eat hentai first, then cams, then tubes — in that order.

Pornwha has a ceiling. TikTok porn is a format pretending to be a destination. Taboo is a tag pretending to be a genre. Undress AI is a felony waiting for jurisdiction.

Cams are both bigger and smaller than they look. Semrush's "Adult" filter hides them. Industry trackers inflate them. The truth is somewhere between Similarweb's 216M and the industry's 700M — which is another way of saying nobody actually knows.

The modern porn economy is routes, not destinations. Directories show destinations. That is why they mislead.

## 6. Category size disclaimers

- **Web traffic only.** App-based activity (TikTok, Instagram, Telegram, Discord) is not counted. The real adult economy on apps is larger than anything on this page.
- **Public trackers estimate.** Semrush, Similarweb, and Ahrefs all model traffic — none of them measure it. Differences of 2–4x between providers are normal.
- **Category classification is political.** Semrush's "Adult" excludes cams, hentai, AI generators, and most creator platforms. Reclassify and rankings change.
- **Mirror domains inflate leaders.** xvideos.com + xvideos.es + xvideos-related mirrors probably exceed 3.5B combined.
- **Conflicting cam numbers reflect real measurement difficulty.** Cam sites run WebRTC streams that don't always register as conventional pageviews.

## 7. Free API access per platform

What can actually be queried for free in 2026, and where the walls begin.

### Traffic/analytics providers (apply to any site)

| Provider | Free access | What you get | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Similarweb** | Chrome/Firefox extension + undocumented public endpoint | Monthly visits, global rank, bounce rate, geo, traffic sources, category | Hard daily cap, sample-only data, CloudFront blocking since late 2025 |
| **Similarweb public API** | Trial only | Same as paid | 10 req/sec max, speak to an account manager — effectively paid |
| **Semrush** | None | — | Paid only |
| **Ahrefs** | Free Webmaster Tools (your own domain only) | Backlinks, keywords | Cannot query competitor sites |
| **Cloudflare Radar** | Fully free public API | Aggregate internet traffic, domain popularity rank, DNS trends | No per-site visit numbers |
| **Google Trends API (unofficial)** | Free | Search interest over time per query | No absolute volumes |
| **DataForSEO** | Free trial | Similar to Semrush | Paid after trial |

Practical path for free traffic estimation: Similarweb Chrome extension + Cloudflare Radar + Google Trends. Combine three signals, triangulate. No single free source is authoritative.

### Platform-specific APIs

| Platform | Official API | Free tier | What you get for free |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reddit** | Yes (OAuth) | 100 QPM authenticated, 10 QPM unauth | Subreddit subscriber counts, post/comment data, search — non-commercial only. Use PRAW (Python) |
| **X / Twitter** | Yes (v2) | Effectively dead | ~500 writes/month, no meaningful read. Real access starts at $100/mo (Basic), $5k/mo (Pro) |
| **Pornhub** | None | — | Unofficial scrapers only (PHUB, phub-api, Apify actors). Against ToS. Video metadata, search results, tags |
| **OnlyFans** | Private B2B only (2024+) | None | Third-party services (OnlyFansAPI.com, oFANS API) offer 3.5M+ creator profiles via paid REST. Scraping against ToS |
| **Chaturbate** | Yes — free affiliate API | Full access with affiliate account | Live model feeds, online status, tags, revenue stats (your own). Third-party CB-Stats / CamStats free dashboards |
| **Stripchat** | Affiliate API | Requires signup | Live model data, referral tracking |
| **BongaCams / LiveJasmin** | Affiliate API | Requires signup | Similar — model feeds, referral stats |
| **MYM.fans / Fansly / Patreon** | Partial — Patreon has an official OAuth API; MYM/Fansly do not | Patreon API free for creator's own data | Patreon: pledges, posts on your own account |

### What this means in practice

**For traffic comparison across adult sites:** Similarweb's free extension is the only tool that produces consistent per-site monthly numbers. Hit the rate limit quickly, rotate IPs if scraping (against their ToS).

**For real-time data on live cam platforms:** Chaturbate is the most open — their affiliate API returns structured JSON of every online model. Stripchat and BongaCams require affiliate approval but work similarly. This is the only corner of the adult web that publishes real-time data for free.

**For content metadata on tubes:** Pornhub has no official API but the site is heavily scraped. Several mature open-source libraries (PHUB, lustpress) work. Rate limiting and bot detection are aggressive.

**For creator platforms:** OnlyFans is a wall. Third-party APIs exist but are grey-market, paid, and violate ToS. MYM and Fansly have no viable API. Only Patreon plays fair, and only for the creator's own data.

**For social-graph NSFW:** Reddit remains genuinely useful under the free tier — 100 QPM covers almost any research need. X is closed. Paying $100/month for the Basic tier is the minimum to do anything real on X.

The economics of free API access inversely track the profitability of each platform. Tubes are free to scrape and make their money on ads. Cams give affiliates an open API and take a revenue cut. OnlyFans makes money on subscription — and its API is a locked room with a bouncer.

A platform's API openness is always a confession about its business model.

### What I initially missed — free sources with real data

The first pass framed the problem as APIs. APIs are a fraction of what's available.

**Official free data published by the platforms themselves:**

- **Pornhub Insights** — every December, Pornhub publishes its "Year in Review" with real numbers: 5.5 billion searches in 2024, country rankings, top search terms (hentai #1 for four years running, milf #2), average visitor age (38), time-on-site trends (-29s in 2024), gender split (38% female viewers). Free PDF download. More detailed than anything Similarweb sells. The data is theirs — they can afford to publish what they cannot afford to hide.
- **XVideos Red / xHamster / Pornhub public sitemaps** — XML sitemaps expose every URL and in some cases view counts per video. Legal to crawl.
- **Per-video view counts** — every tube displays views on every video. A nine-figure catalog of popularity data, publicly visible, scrape-at-your-own-risk. Aggregate to category level and you reconstruct category popularity with no API.
- **Per-model stats on cam sites** — Chaturbate, Stripchat, and BongaCams each show total viewers, token volume, online history on model pages. Public.
- **Reddit subscriber counts and post scores** — public on every subreddit page, no API needed. r/GoneWild at 3.6M members is public info.

**Google-level infrastructure data, free:**

- **Chrome UX Report (CrUX) on BigQuery** — Google's real-user performance dataset, per origin, monthly granularity, free tier via BigQuery. Covers every site Google has enough Chrome data on — which is all the major adult sites. You get Core Web Vitals, LCP, CLS, real user traffic patterns from 2017 onward. This is the cleanest free dataset about real user behavior on adult sites that exists.
- **Google Trends** — free query-volume proxy. Search "pornhub" vs "onlyfans" vs "stripchat" and get real relative interest trends over time.
- **Google autocomplete API** — undocumented, free. Hitting `suggestqueries.google.com` reveals implicit query volume ordering.

**Reddit historical (non-API) free access:**

- **Arctic Shift** — the Pushshift successor. Full Reddit data dumps, a web interface, a limited API. Free. Covers NSFW subreddits up to mid-2025. 2.38B posts. Self-hostable.
- **Academic torrents** — Reddit metadata, rules, wiki dumps updated through 2025.

**Third-party aggregators, free tiers:**

- **HypeStat, SiteWorthTraffic, ReviewBolt, SimilarSites** — free traffic estimation pages for any domain. Each uses its own model. Individually unreliable, collectively triangulable.
- **Cloudflare Radar** — domain popularity rank (free, public API). No absolute visit numbers but a clean relative signal.
- **BuiltWith free lookup** — tech stack per site. Useful for understanding which adult sites run on what CDN, which share infrastructure.
- **Wappalyzer, Wayback Machine** — Wayback archives Similarweb's own rank pages, meaning historical traffic numbers can be reconstructed for free by crawling archive.org snapshots.
- **Apify free tier** — $5/month in credits. Enough to run PornHub and OnlyFans scrapers for small research jobs.
- **Common Crawl** — petabytes of free web crawl data. Most major adult sites are indexed. Host on BigQuery and query at will.

**Academic and research datasets:**

- **The Journal of Sex Research** and **SAGE** publications routinely include real traffic breakdowns in peer-reviewed adult-industry papers. Free via university proxy or Sci-Hub.
- **arXiv preprints** — researchers studying NSFW communities publish datasets alongside papers. 2511.15726 (2025) analyzes NSFW sharing patterns with a public dataset.

### The complete free-data picture

If the goal is to measure adult web traffic seriously without paying:

1. **For aggregate per-site traffic** — Similarweb extension + HypeStat + SiteWorthTraffic + Cloudflare Radar rank. Triangulate across four estimators.
2. **For content-level popularity** — scrape public view counts on tubes. Aggregate to category.
3. **For demographic and behavioral data** — Pornhub Insights annual report. Free, authoritative, better than anything else.
4. **For real-user performance** — CrUX on BigQuery.
5. **For historical Reddit NSFW** — Arctic Shift.
6. **For search interest** — Google Trends + Google autocomplete.
7. **For cam platform live state** — Chaturbate affiliate API (best free data on the adult web).

The paid APIs sell convenience, not truth. The data is almost always findable for free, if you are willing to scrape, triangulate, and read the annual reports nobody else reads.
