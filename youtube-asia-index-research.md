## Step 4: Key Patterns for Your Crypto Top 100 Index Video

### Winning Single-Fund Video Formats (ranked by effectiveness):

**Format 1: "Retiring Off JUST [Fund]"** (393K-321K views)
- Pick ONE fund, make the case it's all you need
- Show compound growth projections, monthly income scenarios
- Chapters: Fund explained > Why this one > How much to invest > When you'll be free
- **Your version:** "Retiring Off JUST the Crypto Top 100 Index"

**Format 2: "I Invested in [Fund] for X Days"** (34K-1M views)
- Personal experiment with real money + real results
- Specificity in title ("437 days", "$15/day", "505 shares")
- Chapters: Why I started > The strategy > Month-by-month > Results > What I learned
- **Your version:** "I Invested in the Crypto Top 100 Index for 90 Days - Here's What Happened"

**Format 3: "[Fund] - The Greatest/Only Fund You Need"** (34K-192K views)
- Pure advocacy/conviction pick
- Data-heavy: historical returns, fees, holdings breakdown
- Compare to alternatives to prove superiority
- **Your version:** "The ONLY Crypto Index Fund You'll Ever Need"

**Format 4: "Don't Make This Mistake with [Fund]"** (838K-1M views)
- Mistake/contrarian hook drives curiosity
- Teaches through what NOT to do
- Higher click-through rate than positive framing
- **Your version:** "The Crypto Investing Mistake Everyone Makes (And the Fix)"

### Video Structure (from top performers):
- **8-15 minutes** sweet spot for single-fund videos (shorter than listicles)
- **5-10 chapters** with clear, descriptive names
- **Screen recordings** of the actual platform/buying process
- **Real portfolio screenshots** with real numbers (authenticity wins)
- **One clear thesis** stated in first 30 seconds

### Thumbnail Patterns for Single-Fund Videos:
- Fund ticker/logo prominently displayed (VOO, SCHD, VTSAX)
- Dollar amount or percentage return
- Face with confident/surprised expression
- "JUST THIS ONE" or "ONLY 1" text overlay
- Green charts going up
