One fee. The rest is the network's.

## The schedule

| Action | Fee | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Place a wager | 0% | — |
| Lose a wager | 0% protocol fee (you lost the wager itself) | — |
| Win a wager | 0.3% of net winnings | DAO treasury |
| Claim winnings | 0% protocol fee | Solana network gas only |
| Refund a voided round | 0% | Solana network gas only |
| Connect a wallet | 0% | — |
| Browse the catalog | 0% | — |

The 0.3% is calculated on the *winnings* — the amount transferred from losers to winners — not on the original stake. A winner who staked $60 and is owed $99.88 paid 0.3% on the $40 of redistributed loser-pot, not on the full claimable amount. See the worked example in [Parimutuel Methodology](/product/parimutuel-methodology).

## Network fees

Solana charges gas on every transaction. The protocol does not see this gas; the network does. Typical Solana fees are fractions of a cent. They are charged by the chain, not by nsgame. The protocol cannot waive them and does not collect them.

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum wager | $0.10 |
| Maximum wager | uncapped |
| Minimum claim | none |
| Maximum claim | bounded only by the pool |

There is no daily limit. There is no cumulative-loss limit. There is no withdrawal cap. The protocol does not know who you are; it cannot apply a per-user constraint beyond the wallet itself.

## Where the rake goes

To the DAO treasury. The DAO is in formation; until mainnet, the rake is held by the founding team in a multisig wallet and transferred to the DAO at mainnet. See [Decentralization & DAO](/trust/decentralization-and-dao). The honest sentence is: the DAO is in formation; details will be published when finalised.

The rake is not split with operators. It is not used to subsidise gas. It is not retained as profit. It is moved on-chain to a wallet the DAO controls, and the chain records the move.

## How this compares

| Venue | Take | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Polymarket | 0% on Geopolitical/World Events; up to ~1.8% peak on Sports markets via taker fee | order-book maker/taker model |
| Kalshi | per-market transaction fee on expected earnings; varies by event | regulated derivatives exchange |
| Stake.com (casino) | 3.5–4.5% house edge baked into RNG | every wager, every spin |
| nsgame | 0.3% on winnings only | parimutuel; no house, no order book |

Polymarket charges nothing on most markets and a price-curve fee on a few. Kalshi charges a transaction fee within a regulated exchange wrapper. Stake keeps a margin on every wager regardless of outcome. nsgame charges only when there is a winner, only on the redistributed portion, and routes the result to the DAO. The differences are not cosmetic — they are different industries pretending to be similar.

The protocol does not subsidise users. It does not run promotions. It does not offer rakeback, deposit bonuses, VIP tiers, or referral kickbacks. The fee table above is the entire commercial relationship.
