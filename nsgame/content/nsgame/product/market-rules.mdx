Two formats. Twenty-five fights today. A market resolves the way it was deployed to resolve, and that is the only contract that matters. The roster around it moves; the rules under each round do not.

## The catalog today

Fifty named subjects, paired into twenty-five PvP fights. Stars take fifteen on a four-hour cadence. Cams take ten on a two-minute cadence. The number is a count, not a promise.

The catalog is curated. The operating principle is simple: focus liquidity on the most popular trades. The roster grows when a new pair pulls more attention than the worst-performing one it would replace. Pairs that fail to attract a pool are retired. Updates happen on a regular basis. A larger catalog with thin pools is worse than a smaller catalog with deep pools — which is why we grow only when a new pair earns its way in.

Each pair index is encoded into `threshold_bps` on the on-chain Market PDA. That is mechanical; it matters because it is the reason no name is quietly swapped for another mid-cycle. A round resolves under the names it opened with. Roster moves apply to future rounds, never to a round in flight.

## Format F1 — Gain Race

**Used by.** Every Stars market. Every Cams market resolves both F1 and F2.

**Definition.** Two subjects, A and B. Both have a baseline view count at the moment the round opens. Both have a closing view count at the moment the round locks. Whoever's count grew more, wins.

**Win condition.**

```
gain_A = views_A_close − views_A_open
gain_B = views_B_close − views_B_open
winner = max(gain_A, gain_B)
```

Absolute deltas, not percentages. A subject starting at one million views can outgrow a subject starting at one thousand and still lose if the smaller subject doubled. The stake is the gain itself.

**Data source.** xvideos or xnxx or pornhub for Stars. The specific source per pair is published from the oracle node. See [Resolution Sources](/product/resolution-sources) and [Data Source Methodology](/trust/data-source-methodology).

**Resolution method.** The Goldilock oracle network reads view counts at open and at close. The reading is signed by a multi-oracle quorum. The signed settlement posts on-chain. See [Settlement Rules](/product/settlement-rules).

**Edge cases.**

- *Tie.* If `gain_A == gain_B` to the integer, the round voids. Both sides refund. See [Refund Policy](/product/refund-policy). Statistically rare; not impossible.
- *Missing data.* If the source returns a stale or missing reading at open or close, the round voids. The protocol does not invent numbers.
- *Source change mid-round.* If the underlying tube site changes its view-count semantics during a round in a way that breaks comparability — denominator change, public-API removal, definition flip — the round voids and the market is paused until the change is documented. The honest path is a refund, not a guess.
- *Reading at lock missed by network jitter.* The oracle uses the closest reading within the resolution window; the window is small enough that drift below a few seconds does not change the answer. If no reading exists in the window, the round voids.

## Format F2 — Viewer Total

**Used by.** Cams markets only.

**Definition.** Two cam rooms, A and B. The protocol measures concurrent viewer count over the two-minute window. Whoever ends with the higher representative viewer total, wins.

**Win condition.**

```
total_A = representative viewer count for A across the window
total_B = representative viewer count for B across the window
winner = max(total_A, total_B)
```

Representative is either peak or mean across the window — fixed per market at deploy time, not chosen retroactively. The exact aggregation is published in the oracle's resolution methodology. See [Data Source Methodology](/trust/data-source-methodology).

**Data source.** chaturbate. Public concurrent-viewer metric. Read at sub-window intervals during the round.

**Resolution method.** Multi-oracle quorum reads the underlying viewer feed at agreed intervals during the round. At lock the readings are aggregated, signed, and posted. The aggregation rule is part of the program; the readings are part of the chain.

**Edge cases.**

- *Tie.* If `total_A == total_B`, the round voids. Refund.
- *Cam goes offline mid-round.* A room that goes offline during the window keeps the readings it accumulated. If both rooms go offline before any reading was taken, the round voids.
- *Source rate-limits the oracle mid-round.* If the oracle cannot get enough readings to satisfy the aggregation rule, the round voids. The threshold is documented per source.
- *A room is removed by the platform mid-round.* The round voids. The subject removal does not finish the bet.

## What counts as a "valid" round

A round is valid when:

1. The on-chain market exists with non-zero stake on both sides.
2. The oracle has open and close readings (F1) or sufficient interval readings (F2) within the resolution window.
3. The signed settlement transaction lands on-chain before the resolution deadline.

If any of those fail, the round voids. The path is documented in [Refund Policy](/product/refund-policy).

A round with stake on only one side voids automatically. There is nobody to win against.

## What counts as a "valid" subject

A subject is one of the names in the live catalog at the moment a round opens. The list is on-chain via the Market PDA seeds. If a subject is delisted from the website per [Subject Removal Policy](/trust/subject-removal-policy), the website hides the market — but the protocol still resolves rounds that were already open. The chain does not forget; the website can.

## What is not in scope

- **Three-way races.** The schema is binary. No A vs B vs C.
- **Cross-board pairs.** A Stars subject does not race a Cams subject. They live on different sources, different cadences, different resolution methods.
- **Mid-round roster swaps.** A name cannot be substituted into an existing pair index. A round resolves under the pair it opened with. New pairs and retirements take effect for future rounds, never in flight.
- **Manual resolution.** No human decides who wins. The oracle reads, signs, posts. If the oracle cannot read, the round voids. There is no override.

## Where the rules live

The Market PDA on Solana — `DQwMnwQGYuLDvciSFZNgUvcHkA3Buyhk3ejgbACvSydA`. The seeds are `(source_id, pair_index, close_time, settlement_time)`. The catalog file is `nsgame/lib/markets/pairs.ts`. The resolution code is in the Goldilock oracle daemon.

Rules in three places, all of them readable. The format and resolution logic do not move between deploys; the roster does, on a regular basis, in the direction of liquidity.
