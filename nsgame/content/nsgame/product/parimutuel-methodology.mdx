A parimutuel pool has no house. It has a pot, contributors, and a rule for how the pot divides among the right ones. nsgame keeps 0.3% off the winnings and gives the rest back, proportionally to how much each winner staked. There is no order book. There is no bookmaker. There is no edge baked into a price.

## The mechanics, in three lines

1. Every wager joins one of two sides — A or B. Both sides feed the same pool.
2. When the round resolves, one side wins. The losing side's stake is transferred to the winning side.
3. Each winner gets back their original stake plus a share of the loser pot, proportional to their stake within the winning side. The protocol takes 0.3% of the winnings transferred from losers to winners. That 0.3% is the rake; it routes to the DAO treasury.

The implied odds you see in the UI are not prices set by anyone. They are derived from the current stake distribution. If the pool is 70% on A and 30% on B, A is implied at roughly 70% likely — because that is what the bettors collectively believe. Disagree by staking the other side. Move the price by being right.

## Worked example

A round opens. One hundred wagerers stake a total of $1,000. Sixty percent on A, forty percent on B.

```
Total pool:        $1,000
Stake on A:          $600
Stake on B:          $400
```

The round closes. A wins.

```
Winning side stake (A):                $600
Losing side stake to redistribute (B): $400
Rake on the winnings (0.3% of $400):    $1.20  → DAO treasury
Net winnings to distribute:           $398.80
```

Each winner on A gets their original stake back, plus their proportional share of $398.80.

A bettor who staked $60 on A — ten percent of the winning side — receives:

```
Stake returned:         $60.00
Share of winnings:      10% × $398.80 = $39.88
Total claimable:        $99.88
Profit:                 $39.88
```

A bettor who staked $6 on A — one percent of the winning side — receives:

```
Stake returned:          $6.00
Share of winnings:       1% × $398.80 = $3.988
Total claimable:         $9.988
Profit:                  $3.988
```

The winnings ratio scales with stake share within the winning side. The rake is constant at 0.3% of the redistributed pot. Nobody on the losing side claims anything.

Notice the rake comes from the *winnings*, not from the *stake*. A losing bettor does not pay rake on their loss — they paid the loss itself. A winning bettor pays 0.3% on the money the protocol moved from the loser to them. The bettor who staked but only got their own money back (which doesn't happen on a binary win, but illustrates) would pay zero.

## What this is not

**Not an order book.** Polymarket pairs a YES buyer against a NO buyer, each at their own limit price, and lets the market clear at the spread midpoint. There are makers, takers, fees on takers, sometimes maker rebates. nsgame has none of that. There is one pool, two sides, one rake on the winners.

**Not a casino.** Stake.com runs games where the house keeps 3.5–4.5% on every wager — the house edge is baked into the RNG. nsgame keeps 0.3% only when there is a winner to take it from. If a round voids, the rake is zero; if a round one-sides and gets refunded, the rake is zero. The protocol does not take a cut for existing.

**Not a fixed-odds bookmaker.** Sportsbooks set prices, accept the wager, and absorb the risk on their balance sheet. A bookmaker can lose money on a market. The parimutuel protocol cannot — winners are paid only out of losers' stakes. The pool always balances.

The closest analogue is horse-racing parimutuel, which is exactly the model nsgame inherits and the model Stake's racing book inherits — minus the operator margin, minus the regulator, minus the venue. A pool, two sides, a rule.

## Why the implied price moves

The pool's distribution is the price. As one side accumulates more stake, the implied probability of that side rises and the implied payout multiple on that side falls. A late bet on the underdog, if it wins, pays more than an early bet on the same side, because the pool was tilted further when the late bet entered.

This is the discipline of parimutuel: betting late means betting on a clearer signal but betting against a richer pool. There is no free position. The market punishes hesitation as much as it punishes overconfidence.

## Edge cases the math cares about

**One-sided round.** If only A or only B has stake when the round locks, there is no winner-versus-loser dynamic. The round voids and the staked side gets refunded. Auto-refund applies. See [Refund Policy](/product/refund-policy).

**Tied outcome.** F1 ties at the integer view-count delta and F2 ties at the aggregated viewer total — both void. Refund. The math doesn't divide by a non-existent winner.

**Single winner.** A round with only one bettor on the winning side gets the entire net winnings. Same formula; the share is 100%.

**Voided round.** Stakes return at 1:1. No rake is taken. The pool was never converted into winnings; there is nothing for the protocol to skim.

## Where the rake goes

0.3% of every winning payout routes to the DAO treasury. The DAO is in formation. Until mainnet, the rake is held by the founding team in a multisig wallet and transferred to the DAO treasury at mainnet. See [Decentralization & DAO](/trust/decentralization-and-dao).

The rake is not split with the winning side. It is not used to subsidise losers. It is not held against future operating costs. It goes to the DAO. What the DAO does with it is what DAOs do with treasuries — the answer is not yet finalised because the DAO is not yet finalised. See [Audit & Mainnet Roadmap](/trust/audit-and-mainnet-roadmap).
