Twenty-five live fights today. Two boards. Parimutuel pools on Solana. Pick a side, stake, wait for the round to close, claim if you were right. The product fits in a paragraph because there is nothing to hide.

## The catalog

Fifty named subjects, paired into twenty-five PvP fights. That is what the audience supports today, not a cap on the protocol. The roster is curated. The operating principle is to focus liquidity on the most popular trades — pairs that earn their pool stay; pairs that don't, leave. Updates happen on a regular basis. The set will be the one worth trading.

| Board | Window | Markets | Format |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Stars | 4 hours | 15 | F1 — view-count gain race |
| Cams | 2 minutes | 10 | F1 — gain race + F2 — viewer total |

Every market is binary. A versus B. The pool decides the price.

## What you do

1. Open nsgame.org. The wallet asks first; everything else asks second.
2. Connect a Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, anything that signs). No email. No password. The wallet is the account.
3. Affirm you are over the age of majority. Soft gate. The chain does not know your age; the website asks anyway. See [Age Gate](/legal/age-gate).
4. Pick a fight. Pick a side. Stake at least $0.10. There is no ceiling.
5. Watch the pool grow until the window closes. Implied odds shift as the other side stakes against you.
6. The round locks. No more bets.
7. The Goldilock oracle reads the data source, signs the settlement, posts it on-chain. See [Goldilock](/trust/goldilock-oracle-network).
8. Winners claim. Losers do not.

That is the entire loop. Anyone who has ever wagered on anything can finish reading this page and start.

## What the protocol takes

0.3% of each winning payout, routed to the DAO treasury. The DAO is in formation. During testnet the rake is held in a multisig by the founding team and transferred to the DAO at mainnet. See [Decentralization & DAO](/trust/decentralization-and-dao).

There is no deposit fee. There is no withdrawal fee. The Solana network charges its own gas; the protocol does not.

Min bet: $0.10. Max bet: uncapped. The pool is what the pool is.

## What the protocol is not

It is not an order book. There is no maker, no taker, no spread. Polymarket pairs YES against NO; nsgame pairs A against B and prices both by stake share.

It is not a casino. There is no house edge. The 0.3% is a tax on victory, not a margin on every bet. Stake.com keeps 3.5–4.5% on every spin; nsgame keeps 0.3% only when someone wins.

It is not custodial. Funds sit in a Solana program PDA until the round resolves. No team account holds them. No reset button exists.

## The two boards in one breath

**Stars.** Fifteen pairs of porn-star names. Every four hours a new round opens. The market measures view-count growth on the underlying tube site over those four hours. Whoever climbs faster wins. See [Resolution Sources](/trust/data-source-methodology).

**Cams.** Ten pairs of cam-room names. Every two minutes a new round opens. F1 is the same gain race; F2 is a viewer-total race. Whoever ends with more eyeballs wins. Two minutes is short on purpose — short rounds compound into a habit faster than long ones.
