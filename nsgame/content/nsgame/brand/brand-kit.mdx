A brand kit for a protocol that does not own itself. Use these assets accurately. Misuse is not a copyright fight; it is a clarity fight, which is harder.

## Wordmark

The wordmark is **nsgame**. Lowercase. Always. No capitalization at the start of sentences, in headlines, in URLs, in titles, in legal language. `nsgame`, never `Nsgame`, `NSGame`, `NSGAME`, or `Ns Game`. If your style guide auto-capitalizes the first word of a sentence, override it.

The domain is nsgame.org. There is no other.

## Logo

- Mark: [`/brand/logo.svg`](/brand/logo.svg)
- Mark, dark backgrounds: [`/brand/logo-dark.svg`](/brand/logo-dark.svg)

Use the mark as published. Do not redraw, restretch, recolor, or rotate it. Maintain clear space equal to the height of the lowercase letters around all sides. Minimum size: 24px tall on screen, 8mm tall in print.

## Permitted uses

- Editorial coverage — articles, reviews, analysis, criticism.
- Partner integrations — wallets, aggregators, indexers, oracle dashboards.
- Fan content — explainers, threads, memes, art that does not imply endorsement.
- Journalism — without prior approval, with accuracy.

## Prohibited uses

- Implying endorsement, partnership, or affiliation that does not exist.
- Dressing up tokens, projects, or services as "official nsgame" anything. The protocol has no official anything.
- Fundraising or solicitation under the nsgame name.
- Modified logos that change the wordmark, palette, or proportions.
- Use in connection with a token sale, presale, airdrop, or whitelist. A token is planned; nothing claiming to be it today is real. See [Token Status](/trust/token-status).

## A note on the name

It is short on purpose. It does not stand for anything. It does not need to.
