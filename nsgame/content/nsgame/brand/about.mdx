Every industry hides its numbers behind a different curtain. Pornography hides them in plain sight, then refuses to admit they are numbers. nsgame prices them.

## What this is

nsgame is a DAO-governed parimutuel prediction market on Solana. The subjects are adult-tube performance signals — porn-star view counts and cam-room viewer counts, scraped from the public surfaces of xvideos, xnxx, pornhub, and chaturbate. Two boards. Twenty-five fights today. Fifty named subjects, paired.

The Stars board runs four-hour windows over fifteen view-count races. The Cams board runs two-minute windows over ten viewer-count races. Each market is a head-to-head: A versus B, binary outcome, pool decides the price. Twenty-five live markets today. The number is a count, not a promise. The roster moves as the audience moves. Pairs that earn their pool stay; pairs that don't, leave.

## Why bother

Prediction markets reveal information. They aggregate what the polls miss, what the pundits soften, what the analysts will not say into a single number anyone can read. The literature on this is thirty years old and still surprises people.

The rest of finance refuses to price this industry. Banks decline the wires. Card networks decline the merchants. Public markets decline the listings. The numbers exist anyway — billions of views, millions of viewers, a cultural metric the culture pretends not to measure. Someone will price them eventually. We thought it should be us.

## How resolution works

A multi-oracle network — **Goldilock** — reads the public metrics, signs the outcome, and posts settlement on-chain. During testnet, the founding team operates the oracle nodes. At mainnet, the DAO elects them. The methodology is published from the oracle node. Anyone can re-derive the result. The chain is the disclosure.

If the data fails — a tube site rate-limits, the oracle stalls, a reading goes garbage — the round voids. Stakes return. See [Round Mechanics](/product/how-it-works) and [Market Rules](/product/market-rules).

## Who runs it

No one, in the sense the question usually means. There is no incorporated entity. No N.V., no LLC, no Holdings. A DAO is in formation. It will hold the treasury, fund development, elect the oracle operators, and decide anything that needs deciding. Until it exists in full, the founding team holds the multisig and writes the code. The honest sentence is the one we keep repeating: *the DAO is in formation; details will be published when finalised.*

The website lives at nsgame.org. The protocol lives on Solana. The two are not the same thing. The website is something we control. The protocol is something we don't. See [Decentralization & DAO](/trust/decentralization-and-dao) and [Goldilock Oracle Network](/trust/goldilock-oracle-network).
