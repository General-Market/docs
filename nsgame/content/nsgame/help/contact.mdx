There is no support desk. There is no phone line. There is no inbox monitored by a person whose job is to apologize. The protocol is decentralized; the help surface is honest about what that means.

What exists, in order of usefulness:

## Discord

For general questions, market discussions, and the kind of help another user can give faster than we can — the Discord server.

[FACT TBD: Discord invite URL] — link in the footer.

The Discord is community-moderated. Founding-team members read it; they do not always answer. nobody on Discord can recover a lost seed phrase, reverse a wrong-network transfer, or override an oracle resolution. Those things are not solvable; the chain is the limit.

## Subject removal

If you are a subject of a market and want to be delisted from the website, use the form: [Subject Removal Policy](/trust/subject-removal-policy).

The workflow: submit the form → we send a verification link → proof of ID confirms the request → the market is delisted from nsgame.org. The protocol cannot delist anyone — anyone running their own oracle, anyone reading the chain, can keep the market visible. The website is something we control. The protocol is something we don't.

## DMCA / takedown

For copyright claims related to images, names, or trademarks shown on the website: [DMCA Policy](/legal/dmca-policy).

The DMCA process applies to website content. It does not apply to on-chain data — there is nothing to take down at the protocol layer.

## Security disclosures

If you have found a vulnerability in the contracts, the oracle, or the website, follow the disclosure process at:

[https://nsgame.org/.well-known/security.txt](https://nsgame.org/.well-known/security.txt)

A bounty program will be published before mainnet. Until then, responsible disclosure is appreciated and reciprocated with whatever credit you want.

## What we do not offer

- **No support email.** Email is a queue we cannot promise to drain.
- **No live chat.** A live chat implies someone is paid to be live. No one is.
- **No phone number.** A phone number implies an entity. There is no entity.
- **No account recovery.** Wallets are non-custodial. We never had your keys.

The absence of these channels is not negligence — it is the consequence of being a protocol rather than a venue. If that posture does not serve you, neither does the product. See [Decentralization & DAO](/trust/decentralization-and-dao) for why.
