Words borrowed from prediction markets, from Solana, and from the adult-tube taxonomy nsgame is built around. Here, briefly, is what each one means in this codebase.

## A

**Auto-refund.** A refund that returns to the wallet without requiring a claim transaction — applied where gas costs would exceed the refunded stake.

## B

**Board.** One of the two market surfaces. Stars (4-hour windows, 15 markets) or Cams (2-minute windows, 10 markets).

## C

**Cams.** The 2-minute board. Ten head-to-head fights between cam-room subjects, F1 (gain race) and F2 (viewer total) formats.

**Catalog.** The live set of available markets. Twenty-five PvP fights today — curated, not capped. Updated on a regular basis to focus liquidity on the most popular trades.

**Claim.** The transaction a winner signs to retrieve their share of the pool from escrow.

**Connect.** The act of authorizing a wallet to interact with nsgame.org. Connection signs a message; it does not move funds.

**Curation.** The discipline applied to the catalog. Pairs that earn their pool stay; pairs that don't, leave. The roster grows when a new pair pulls more attention than the worst-performing one it would replace.

## D

**DAO.** The decentralized autonomous organization that will hold the treasury, elect oracle operators, and govern the protocol. Currently in formation.

**Devnet.** Solana's testing network. Free SOL, no monetary value, identical contract semantics to mainnet.

## F

**F1.** Gain race. The market resolves to whichever subject's view-count grew more during the round.

**F2.** Viewer total. The market resolves to whichever subject had the higher absolute viewer count at lock time. Cams board only.

## G

**Goldilock.** The multi-oracle network that reads data sources and posts settlements on-chain. Operated by the founding team during testnet, by elected DAO members at mainnet.

## I

**In-formation.** The state of the DAO during testnet. Treasury, election cycles, slashing rules — assembled but not yet finalised.

## L

**Lock.** The moment a round closes to new bets. The pool freezes; the oracle waits for the resolution window.

## M

**Mainnet.** Solana's production network. nsgame moves to mainnet only after audits of the oracle and contracts complete.

**Multisig.** A wallet that requires multiple signatures to release funds. The treasury sits in a multisig held by the founding team during testnet, transferring to the DAO at mainnet.

## O

**Oracle Network.** See **Goldilock**.

## P

**Pair.** One of the twenty-five PvP fights. Two named subjects, one binary outcome.

**Parimutuel.** A pool-based wagering format. The pot is split among winners proportional to stake, minus rake. No house, no order book, no fixed odds.

**Pool.** The collected stakes on each side of a market. The two pools determine the implied odds and, after settlement, the payout multiple.

## R

**Rake.** The protocol fee. **0.3% of each winning payout**, routed to the DAO treasury.

**Refund.** The return of a stake when a round voids. Claim-based by default; auto-refund where ergonomically better.

**Resolve.** The on-chain action that records a winner. Performed by the oracle network after the resolution window closes.

**Rotation.** The act of adding new pairs and retiring underperforming ones. Applies to future rounds, never to a round already in flight. See **Curation**.

**Round.** One instance of a market. Opens at start time, locks at close, resolves when the oracle posts settlement.

## S

**Settle.** Synonymous with resolve in nsgame's vocabulary. The oracle reads the data, decides the winner, posts the result.

**Sign.** Approve a wallet message or transaction. Signing a message proves identity. Signing a transaction moves funds.

**Slot.** Solana's unit of time, roughly 400ms. Used for finality counts.

**Solana.** The blockchain nsgame runs on. High-throughput, low-fee, the right shape for two-minute rounds.

**Source.** A data origin. The current sources are xvideos, xnxx, pornhub, and chaturbate. See [Resolution Sources](/product/resolution-sources).

**Stake.** The amount of collateral a user commits to one side of a market. Minimum **$0.10**. Maximum uncapped.

**Stars.** The 4-hour board. Fifteen head-to-head fights between porn-star subjects, F1 (gain race) format.

**Subject.** A named person or room whose performance signals are the market's outcome. Subject removal is documented in [Subject Removal Policy](/trust/subject-removal-policy).

## T

**Threshold.** An on-chain field that encodes the pair index for each market PDA. Doubles as the catalog identifier — see [PLAN.md](https://github.com/) for context.

**Transaction.** A signed instruction sent to Solana. Bets, claims, and refunds are transactions; connecting a wallet is not.

**Treasury.** The pooled rake. Held in a multisig by the founding team during testnet; transferred to the DAO at mainnet.

## V

**Void.** A round that did not resolve due to data or oracle failure. Stakes refund. See [Refund Policy](/product/refund-policy).

## W

**Wallet.** A Solana account — Phantom, Solflare, hardware. The wallet is your identity, your password, and your bank. We never see the keys. See [Wallet Setup](/help/wallet-setup).
