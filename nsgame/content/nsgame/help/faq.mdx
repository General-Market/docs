The same questions, asked in slightly different words, arrive every day. Here they are with the words removed.

## What is nsgame?

A parimutuel prediction market on Solana. Subjects are adult-tube performance signals — porn-star view counts, cam-room viewer counts. Twenty-five hardcoded PvP fights, fifty named subjects, two boards. Pools resolve on-chain when the oracle posts settlement. See [How It Works](/product/how-it-works).

## How do I start?

Connect a Solana wallet. Pick a side in any market. Stake at least $0.10. Wait for the round to close. If you won, claim. The whole arc takes two minutes on the Cams board. See [Wallet Setup](/help/wallet-setup).

## What does it cost?

A 0.3% rake on each winning payout — routed to the DAO treasury. No deposit fees. No withdrawal fees. Solana network fees apply, paid in SOL. See [Fee Schedule](/product/fee-schedule).

## What's the minimum bet?

$0.10. Below that, the gas to place the bet costs more than the bet itself. The protocol still allows it; the wallet still signs. The arithmetic is the only objection.

## Is there a maximum bet?

No. The pool is the only ceiling. A larger stake against a thin opposing pool means a smaller multiple — that is the parimutuel mechanic, not a limit.

## What happens if the data fails?

The round voids and stakes refund. If the oracle goes down, if the data-node returns garbage, if a tube site rate-limits us mid-round — nobody wins, everybody gets their stake back. Refunds are claim-based by default; auto-refund where ergonomically better. See [Refund Policy](/product/refund-policy).

## What if my market subject asks to be removed?

The website delists them. The protocol cannot. Submit the form at [Subject Removal Policy](/trust/subject-removal-policy) — we send a verification link, proof of ID confirms the request, the market disappears from nsgame.org. Anyone running their own oracle can keep showing it. Decentralization is honest about what it cannot do.

## Is there a token?

A token is planned. Details will be published when finalised. Anything claiming to be the nsgame token today is fraudulent. See [Token Status](/trust/token-status).

## Are you regulated?

No. There is no incorporated entity, no licence, no jurisdiction. The DAO is in formation. The protocol settles on Solana; every transaction is public. That is the disclosure. See [Decentralization & DAO](/trust/decentralization-and-dao).

## Where do my funds sit?

In your wallet — until you stake. While staked, they sit in the program's escrow account on Solana. After settlement, winners claim back to the same wallet. Non-custodial. We never hold your keys. We cannot recover them.

## Who runs the oracle?

The Goldilock network. During testnet, the founding team operates the oracle nodes. At mainnet, DAO-elected operators take over. Election cycles, slashing rules, token weighting — all TBA. See [Goldilock Oracle Network](/trust/goldilock-oracle-network).

## What's Goldilock?

The multi-oracle network that reads the data sources, signs settlements, and posts them on-chain. Methodology is published from the oracle node — anyone can re-derive the resolution from the same public metrics.

## Is this gambling?

It is wagering on a measurable, public outcome. Whether your jurisdiction calls that gambling, prediction-market trading, or something else is a question for your jurisdiction. See [Risk Disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure) and [Restricted Territories](/legal/restricted-territories).

## Why adult-industry signals?

Because they update every two to four hours, the data is public, and nobody else is making markets on them. A prediction market needs a measurable subject and a frequent reading. Adult-tube performance fits both. The taste question is yours.

## Is the website age-gated?

Yes. A checkbox affirmation on entry, plus a confirmation message after entry. Soft gate, not hard ID. See [Age Gate](/legal/age-gate).

## When does mainnet launch?

After audits of the oracle and the contracts complete. No date. The path is testnet → audit → mainnet. See [Audit & Mainnet Roadmap](/trust/audit-and-mainnet-roadmap).

## What chain is this on?

Solana. Devnet today. Mainnet after audits. The chain choice is not under reconsideration.

## What if I lose my private keys?

Your funds are gone. Non-custodial means non-recoverable. Write your seed phrase down on paper. Store it somewhere a fire wouldn't reach. We cannot help you. Nobody can.

## How does refund work?

If a round voids, the stake returns to the wallet that placed it. Claim-based by default — open the market, click the refund button. Auto-refund applies where the gas math makes claiming absurd. See [Refund Policy](/product/refund-policy).

## Does the DAO exist yet?

The DAO is in formation. Treasury, oracle election, governance details — TBA. The protocol is live on devnet; the governance layer is being assembled around it. The honest sentence: details will be published when finalised.
