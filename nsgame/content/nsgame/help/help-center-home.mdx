A short site. A curated catalog. Twenty-five fights today, two boards, one pool per side. The roster moves to follow the audience. Most questions answer themselves once you watch a round close. The rest are below.

The protocol is on Solana. The DAO is in formation. The oracle network — Goldilock — runs on the founding team during testnet and transitions to elected operators at mainnet. There is no operator entity to file a complaint against, no support email, no live chat. The chain is the disclosure. The docs are the rest.
