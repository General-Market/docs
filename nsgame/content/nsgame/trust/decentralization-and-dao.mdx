There is no company behind nsgame. No N.V., no LLC, no Holdings, no parent, no subsidiary. There is a Solana program, a website, a small founding team running the lights, and a DAO in formation that will inherit the whole thing. The honest sentence is the one we repeat: *the DAO is in formation; details will be published when finalised.* What follows is what is true today and what becomes true at mainnet.

## The shape of the system

Three layers. They are not interchangeable.

- **The protocol.** A Solana program. It accepts bets, locks pools, distributes payouts, and keeps no secrets. Anyone can read the chain. Anyone can submit transactions. Anyone can run a frontend against it.
- **The website.** nsgame.org. A window onto the protocol. The website chooses what to display, what to delist, which jurisdictions to refuse a connection from. The website is something we control. The protocol is something we don't.
- **The Goldilock oracle network.** A multi-signature oracle that reads public tube-site signals and posts settlements on-chain. Today, founding-team-operated. At mainnet, operated by DAO-elected nodes. See [Goldilock — The Oracle Network](/trust/goldilock-oracle-network).

The protocol is the thing. The website is convenience. The oracle is the bridge.

## Today

We are on Solana devnet. The system has been deployed. The bets are real, the SOL is test SOL, the audits have not happened. Treat everything below as testnet truth.

- **No incorporated entity.** There is no operator company. No licence to disclose, because there is no licensee. The protocol is the licence, in the same sense that gravity is the licence for falling.
- **The founding team operates Goldilock.** Two oracle nodes, one signing key, one repository, one team. This is acceptable for testnet. It is not acceptable for mainnet, which is why mainnet has not happened.
- **The treasury is a multisig.** The 0.3% rake on each winning payout flows to a multisig wallet held by the founding team. The multisig is a placeholder. It is not the DAO, it is what the DAO will replace.
- **Governance is informal.** Decisions are made in Discord, recorded in commits, executed by a small set of keys. This is also testnet truth. It will not survive mainnet.
- **Subject removal goes through the website.** The website operates the takedown form, verifies identity, removes markets from the UI. The protocol does not change. See [Subject Removal Policy](/trust/subject-removal-policy).

A small operating surface during testnet. The page describes it because the chain already does.

## At mainnet

Mainnet happens after audits. Of the program. Of the oracle. Of the data-source methodology. See [Audit & Mainnet Roadmap](/trust/audit-and-mainnet-roadmap). When mainnet happens, the system rotates one degree toward the DAO.

- **The DAO holds the treasury.** The 0.3% rake routes from the multisig to a DAO-controlled treasury. Spend decisions become DAO decisions.
- **The DAO elects Goldilock operators.** Oracle nodes are no longer founding-team boxes. They are operator slots filled by election, with stake at risk and slashing for misbehaviour. Quorum and election rules are TBA.
- **The DAO decides which pairs the protocol carries.** The 25 PvP fights live today are a curated roster, updated on a regular basis to focus liquidity on the most popular trades. At mainnet, the curation moves from the founding team to the DAO — adding, retiring, or renaming subjects becomes a governance action. The discipline does not change: liquidity per market, not catalog stability.
- **The DAO sets the parameters.** Rake, minimum bet, refund rules, blocklist — all of it becomes mutable through governance, immutable between governance actions.
- **The website remains.** Someone will keep running nsgame.org. It may be the founding team. It may be the DAO. It may be a third party. The protocol does not require the website. The website is a convenience the DAO can choose to renew or to abandon.

## What the DAO will not do

A DAO is a treasury and a vote, not a regulator. It cannot issue you a refund the protocol does not owe you. It cannot delist a market from the chain. It cannot prevent a competing frontend from showing what nsgame.org has chosen to hide. It cannot make you safe. It can only allocate the rake, elect the operators, and decide what the catalog looks like next.

Anyone telling you a DAO will solve every grievance is selling you a different product.

## The honest framing

The argument for the DAO is not that it is fair. It is that it is the only governance compatible with a protocol that nobody owns. The alternative — a quiet operator, an undisclosed multisig, a roadmap with no horizon — is the model that has weighed down prediction markets that came before this one. The DAO is the structure that fits the chain.

## What stays true under either regime

- The protocol settles on Solana. Every transaction is public. There is no AML programme to disclose, because the ledger is the disclosure.
- Wallet is identity. There is no KYC and there will not be. See [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).
- The 0.3% rake is the only fee. $0.10 is the minimum bet. There is no maximum. See [Fee Schedule](/product/fee-schedule).
- The protocol cannot delist a subject. The website can. That distinction does not change at mainnet. See [Subject Removal Policy](/trust/subject-removal-policy).

The DAO is in formation. Details will be published when finalised. Until then, the chain is the disclosure, the website is the front door, and the founding team is the placeholder. None of this is permanent. That is the point.
