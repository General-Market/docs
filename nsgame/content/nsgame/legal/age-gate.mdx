You must be an adult to use nsgame.org. Specifically, you must have reached 18 years of age, or the age of majority in your jurisdiction — whichever is greater. If you have not, leave now. Do not connect a wallet. Do not check the box.

## 1. The gate

A checkbox affirmation appears on first visit. Checking it constitutes a representation that you have reached the age of majority and intend to access content concerning the adult industry as the subject matter of prediction markets.

After entry, the site displays a confirmation message. The message reads, in substance:

> nsgame is for adults. The subjects of these markets are adult performers; the metrics are public; the bettors must be adults too. By proceeding you are reaffirming that you have reached the age of majority in your jurisdiction.

The check is soft. The framing is not.

## 2. What "the subjects are adults" means

The performers whose public metrics drive nsgame markets are adults working in the adult industry on platforms — xvideos, xnxx, pornhub, chaturbate — that perform their own age-of-majority verification at the point of content production. nsgame relies on the upstream platforms' compliance and reads only their public counters. We do not produce, host, or distribute adult content. This site shows numbers and names; not the things the numbers count.

This statement is the section-2257-style framing for an entity that is not a producer, but borrows its trust from those who are.

## 3. Why a soft gate

A hard gate — government ID upload, third-party age-verification service — would be inconsistent with the protocol's posture. nsgame is non-custodial, no-KYC, wallet-based. We do not collect identity documents to bet $0.10. The gate is therefore a representation, not a proof. You attest. You bear the consequence of attesting falsely.

If your jurisdiction requires hard age verification, the website may not be available to you, or may not be enough. Check your rules.

## 4. If a minor accessed the site

Contact us via the Discord link in the footer. The website will revoke access from the implicated wallet. The chain cannot be unwound — but the chain is not where the harm is. The harm is that a minor saw the page. We will help where we can.

## 5. Parental controls

Parents can use operating-system, browser, and ISP-level parental controls to block adult-themed sites including this one. RTA-style labelling is honored. We do not run an in-house allowlist or registry; we rely on the standard infrastructure that already protects minors from sites of this kind.

## 6. The gate's limits

A soft gate is exactly what it sounds like. It is not a guarantee. It is a line drawn in the sand of a website where the tide of the chain is not affected. Knowing this, decide for yourself whether the gate is enough.

## 7. Closing

The protocol does not check ages. The chain does not check ages. The website asks. Be honest with the box.
