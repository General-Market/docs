If your copyrighted work appears on nsgame.org without permission, send a takedown notice. The website will respond. The protocol cannot.

## 1. What the website can remove

nsgame.org displays market metadata — names, thumbnails, descriptive copy, public-metric snapshots. If any of that material infringes copyright you own, the website can:

- Remove the offending image, text, or thumbnail from the site.
- Disable the market's display in the UI.
- Replace the offending element with a neutral placeholder.

The website is something we control. Any of the above is doable.

## 2. What the chain records

Bets, settlements, pool balances, and the program's instruction history are written to Solana. They are public, immutable, and reachable by anyone with an RPC endpoint. The website cannot remove on-chain data. No website can. We are honest about this.

If your copyright concern is the existence of a bet or pool — not the imagery or copy that surrounds it — the request is for the chain, and the chain does not negotiate.

## 3. How to send a takedown

Use the same form as the [Subject Removal Policy](/legal/subject-removal-policy). Submit through that form and select the copyright-infringement reason. Include:

- A signature — physical or electronic — of a person authorized to act for the copyright owner.
- Identification of the work claimed to be infringed (link, title, description sufficient to locate it).
- Identification of the material on nsgame.org that is alleged to infringe, with enough detail to find it (URL, market id, screenshot).
- Your name, mailing address, telephone, and email.
- A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorized.
- A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

The form is the only intake. Email and Discord are not designated.

## 5. Counter-notice

If material of yours was removed and you believe the removal was a mistake or misidentification, submit a counter-notice through the same form, selecting the counter-notice reason. Include:

- Your signature, name, address, telephone, and email.
- Identification of the removed material and its prior location.
- A statement, under penalty of perjury, of good-faith belief that the removal was a mistake or misidentification.
- A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of [FACT TBD: jurisdiction once entity / DAO formation finalised] for purposes of this notice.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, the website may restore the material. The protocol's ledger is unaffected at every step.

## 6. Repeat infringers

The website may revoke access from any wallet associated with repeated, substantiated infringement notices. Revocation follows the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use-policy). The chain continues to honor the wallet.

## 7. False claims

Knowingly false takedown notices and counter-notices are subject to the legal consequences of perjury and misrepresentation under applicable law. Do not use this process to harass, suppress lawful speech, or remove competitors' market metadata.

## 8. The honest distinction

A takedown is a deletion from a window. The chain is the room. We can pull the curtain. We cannot dismantle the room. Treat the curtain accordingly.
