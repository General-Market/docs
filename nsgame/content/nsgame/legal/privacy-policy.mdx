There are two layers. The website collects a little. The protocol collects nothing — because the protocol is a public chain, and a public chain does not collect, it records.

## 1. What the website collects

nsgame.org collects the minimum needed to run a frontend.

- **Connected wallet address.** Necessary to fetch positions, sign transactions, and display balances. Stored in your browser; sent to the chain when you act.
- **Analytics events.** PostHog, configured for product analytics. Page views, button clicks, anonymized session replays where enabled. No screen recordings of wallet seed phrases — those never enter the page.
- **Cookies.** Three categories. Essential, analytics, preferences. See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy).
- **Logs.** Standard server logs from the hosting layer — IP, user agent, timestamps. Retained briefly for debugging and abuse investigation.

That is the list. There is no email field. There is no name field. There is no address field. There is no phone field. There is no KYC document upload. There is no identity verifier in the loop.

## 2. What the protocol records

The protocol settles on Solana. Every transaction is public. Every wallet, every bet, every settlement, every refund is on the chain forever — readable by anyone, indexable by anyone, attributable to whoever claims the wallet.

This is the AML disclosure: there is nothing for us to screen, because the ledger is the screening surface. If a court, a regulator, or a journalist wants to know what happened, they read the chain.

The protocol is not anonymous. It is pseudonymous. A wallet is a name without a face — until the face attaches itself, and then the wallet is the face.

## 3. PostHog analytics

We use PostHog (US Cloud) to understand which pages people read and which buttons they press. PostHog receives the events listed above, tied to a randomly generated anonymous distinct ID stored in your browser. We do not pair the distinct ID to your wallet address in the analytics dataset.

You can opt out via the cookie banner or by sending a Do Not Track signal — both are honored.

## 4. What we do not do

We do not sell data. There is little to sell. We do not run advertising on nsgame.org. We do not share data with brokers. We do not build profiles for resale. The economics of the site do not require it; the rake does the work that ads would otherwise be hired to do.

## 5. Subject information

The subjects of nsgame markets are adult performers identified by stage name. The website displays public metrics — view counts, viewer counts — that the performers' chosen platforms publish openly. The website does not collect private data about subjects. Subjects who wish to be delisted use the [Subject Removal Policy](/legal/subject-removal-policy). The website can delist; the chain cannot.

## 6. Children

The site is for adults. See [Age Verification](/legal/age-gate). If you believe a minor has accessed the site, contact us via the Discord link in the footer. We will not pursue minors; we will revoke access and leave the chain alone.

## 7. Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Wallets sign transactions in the wallet, not in the page. We do not custody funds. We do not custody keys. There is nothing of yours to steal except attention.

## 8. International transfers

PostHog is hosted in the United States. The Solana network is global. Your IP traverses whichever route your provider chooses. If you are concerned about cross-border data flow, the protocol does not require the website — interact with the chain directly.

## 9. Retention

We retain server logs for a short window for debugging. We retain PostHog analytics for the platform's default retention. We do not retain wallet-to-identity mappings, because we never collect them.

The chain retains everything forever. That is the chain.

## 10. Your rights

If a privacy law in your jurisdiction grants you rights — access, deletion, portability — exercise them via the contact link in the footer. We will respond honestly. For data the website holds, we can delete. For data the chain holds, we cannot. No one can.

## 11. Changes

This policy may change. The current version lives at nsgame.org/legal/privacy-policy.

## 12. Closing

The most private thing we can do is not collect. The second most private is to be precise about what we do. This document is the second.
