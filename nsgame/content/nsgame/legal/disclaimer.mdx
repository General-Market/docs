The shorter the disclaimer, the more it has to say. This one is short.

## 1. Not advice

Nothing on nsgame.org is financial advice. Nothing is investment advice. Nothing is legal advice. Nothing is tax advice. Nothing is medical, romantic, or career advice either, but those are easier to spot.

A market price is not a forecast. It is a price. People with money on a side will tell you their conviction; the price tells you the average of their convictions, weighted by stake. That is all.

## 2. Not adult content

nsgame does not host adult content. The protocol settles bets on public adult-industry metrics — view counts and viewer counts published by tube and cam sites. The subjects of the markets are adult performers; the bettors must be adults; the metrics are numbers. The site shows you numbers and names. It does not show you the things the numbers are counting.

## 3. The DAO is in formation

The DAO is in formation; details will be published when finalised. Until then, the founding team operates the website, runs the oracle network during testnet, and holds the rake in a multisig destined for the DAO treasury at mainnet. There is no incorporated entity. There is no licence to disclose. The protocol is the licence — read the chain if you want a regulator.

## 4. Smart-contract risk

The contracts are software. Software has bugs. The contracts are scheduled for external audit before mainnet; until then they run on devnet. You may lose funds to a bug. You may lose funds to a chain reorganization. You may lose funds to a Solana-level outage. None of these are recoverable. See [Risk Disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure).

## 5. Oracle risk

The oracle network — Goldilock — reads public adult-industry metrics and posts settlements on-chain. During testnet it is operated by the founding team. At mainnet it transitions to DAO-elected operators. Oracles can fail. Data sources can change their APIs. Rate limits can blind us mid-round. When that happens, the round is voided and stakes are refunded. When the failure is more interesting than that, refer to [Risk Disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure).

## 6. Regulatory risk

Prediction markets occupy contested legal ground. Adult-industry-themed prediction markets occupy more contested ground than most. Your jurisdiction may treat using nsgame as gambling, as derivatives trading, as obscenity, as innocent fun, or as something for which a category does not yet exist. We do not know your jurisdiction. You do. See [Restricted Territories](/legal/restricted-territories).

## 7. Subject risk

Markets reference adult performers by stage name. Performers leave the industry. Performers rebrand. Performers ask to be delisted — the website can comply, the protocol cannot. See [Subject Removal Policy](/legal/subject-removal-policy).

## 8. No warranty

The website is provided as-is. The protocol is provided as-is. The oracle is provided as-is. To the maximum extent permitted by law, no warranty is offered, no liability is accepted. Bet only what you can incinerate without grief.

## 9. Closing

If you came here for certainty, you came to the wrong protocol. If you came for an honest description of what nothing here guarantees, that is what this page is.
