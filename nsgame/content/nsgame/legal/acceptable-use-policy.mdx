The protocol bans nothing. It cannot. Anyone with a wallet, an RPC endpoint, and a will to act can submit transactions. This policy governs the website. The website can revoke its connection to a wallet. The chain will continue to honor it.

## 1. Who this binds

Anyone connecting a wallet to nsgame.org. Acceptance of these terms is implicit in the connection.

## 2. Disallowed conduct on the website

The website may revoke its connection to your wallet — block the address from loading the site, hide markets, refuse to broadcast transactions through its endpoints — for any of the following:

- **Multi-account abuse from the same beneficial wallet.** Operating several wallets you control to bypass display limits, manipulate UI rankings, or simulate organic interest. The chain does not care. The site does.
- **Hostile scraping.** Automated requests at rates that degrade the site for everyone else. We expose public RPC endpoints for legitimate programmatic access; abusing the human-facing site is the wrong door.
- **Attempted deanonymization of subjects.** Coordinated efforts to identify, contact, harass, or doxx the adult performers who are subjects of nsgame markets. The performers' real identities are none of the protocol's business.
- **Posting illegal content.** If chat or social features exist on the site, do not post anything that violates applicable law — child sexual abuse material, terrorist content, content that incites violence. The site will remove it. So will the chain, but only because the chain stores hashes; the IPFS gateway it points at can pull. Anyone reposting from the original CID cannot be unwound.
- **Market-manipulation-via-collusion, when detectable.** Coordinated bet placement designed to skew settlement of a parimutuel pool you also control on both sides — to exploit the rake, to grief other bettors, to extract round-cancel refunds in bad faith. The website will revoke. The chain will pay according to its rules.
- **Attempts to break the protocol through the website.** Scripted reentry attempts, transaction spam through the site's RPC, fuzzing the contracts via the frontend's submit flow. Use a node directly. The site is not a fuzzing harness.
- **Impersonation of nsgame, the DAO, the founding team, or oracle operators.** Especially anything pretending to be the nsgame token. There is no nsgame token yet. Anything claiming to be one today is fraudulent.

## 3. What the website can do

When the website determines a wallet violates this policy, it may:

- Revoke access to nsgame.org from that wallet.
- IP-block the originating address or range.
- Decline to broadcast transactions through the site's RPC endpoints.
- Hide markets, positions, or social posts associated with the wallet.

The chain remains available. Other frontends remain available. Direct program interaction remains available. The website is a door; we can close it.

## 4. What the website cannot do

The website cannot:

- Reverse a settled bet.
- Cancel a wallet's on-chain positions.
- Confiscate funds.
- Force the oracle to re-resolve a round.
- Modify the protocol's rake schedule, refund logic, or settlement rules.

These are properties of the protocol. The protocol is the protocol.

## 5. Subjects

The subjects of nsgame markets are adult performers identified by stage name. They are not parties to your bet. They have not opted in to being market subjects. Treat them as you would the subject of any public-metric market — you bet on the number, not the person. See the [Subject Removal Policy](/legal/subject-removal-policy) for delisting.

## 6. Reporting abuse

If you observe behavior that violates this policy — yours or someone else's — report via the Discord link in the footer. We will not always respond; we will always read.

## 7. Appeals

There is no formal appeal process. If you think the website's revocation was wrong, contact the founding team through the same Discord. Reasonable explanations get reasonable consideration. Threats, lawsuits, and theatre get nothing.

## 8. Closing

The protocol is permissionless because permissionlessness is the only thing it can offer. The website is permissioned because the website is a courtesy. Treat the courtesy accordingly.
