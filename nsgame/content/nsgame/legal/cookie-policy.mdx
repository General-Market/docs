Cookies are how websites remember things between page loads. nsgame.org uses them sparingly. The list is short.

## 1. The three categories

**Essential.** Required for the site to function. Wallet connection state, session integrity, CSRF tokens. You cannot opt out of these without breaking the site.

**Analytics.** PostHog. Anonymous distinct ID, page-view events, click events. Used to understand which pages get read and which markets get clicked. You can opt out — and the site continues to work.

**Preferences.** Theme choice, age-gate confirmation, locale. Your accepted age-gate is remembered so you are not re-asked on every visit. Opt-out drops these; the gate will reappear.

That is the inventory.

## 2. The banner

A consent banner appears on first visit. It asks you to accept analytics and preferences cookies. Essential cookies are not gated by the banner because, by definition, the site cannot run without them.

You may change your choice later via the cookie settings link in the footer.

## 3. Third parties

PostHog is the only meaningful third party. It receives analytics events from the page. See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) for what PostHog gets and how to opt out.

The site does not run advertising trackers. There are no Facebook pixels, no Google Ads tags, no TikTok pixels, no LinkedIn Insight tags, no DoubleClick. None of the apparatus a normal commercial site is built on. The economics of the protocol do not need it.

## 4. How to opt out

Three ways:

- Click the cookie settings link in the footer and toggle analytics off.
- Send Do Not Track from your browser. We honor it.
- Block cookies in your browser entirely. The site still loads. The age gate will reappear each visit. Your wallet will need to reconnect each session.

Opt-out is honored on the page that processes the next request. There is no review process.

## 5. Closing

The cookie disclosure is the most-clicked, least-read document on the internet. Yours is short on purpose.
