These terms govern your use of nsgame.org. They do not govern the protocol. The website is something we control. The protocol is something we don't.

## 1. What you are agreeing to

By connecting a wallet to nsgame.org, you accept these Terms. There is no signup form. There is no email. The wallet signature is the consent.

If you do not accept these Terms, disconnect and leave. The protocol will keep running without you. So will the website.

## 2. Who can use this site

You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority in your jurisdiction — whichever is greater. The site is gated by a checkbox affirmation and a confirmation message after entry. The gate is soft. The honesty about it is not. See [Age Verification](/legal/age-gate).

You must not be located in a [Restricted Territory](/legal/restricted-territories). The website applies a standard decentralized-protocol blocklist. The protocol checks nothing.

You must use a self-custodied Solana wallet. Custodial wallets, exchange wallets, and shared wallets are unsupported. If you lose your keys, the protocol cannot help you. Neither can the website.

## 3. The protocol versus the website

nsgame is a Solana program. It is open. Anyone can read the chain. Anyone can submit transactions. Anyone can run a frontend.

nsgame.org is one such frontend. The website can change its catalog, its copy, its blocklist, its uptime. The protocol cannot be modified by the website operator. It is changed only by deployment, governed eventually by the DAO.

Two consequences follow:

- The website may delist a market, a subject, or a wallet. The protocol will continue to settle the corresponding pools. The chain does not care what the website displays.
- An outage at nsgame.org does not stop the protocol. You may use any other frontend, or interact directly with the program.

The website is convenience. The protocol is the thing.

The protocol is a neutral wagering layer. Markets are minted by interaction with a permissionless smart contract. nsgame.org curates which markets the website displays. See [Listing Posture](/trust/listing-posture) for the legal posture and [Subject Removal Policy](/trust/subject-removal-policy) for the takedown workflow.
