## Product Facts
- 10-minute settlement cycles
- Parimutuel (pool-based, no order book, no spreads, no liquidity needed)
- Sealed bets — positions invisible until settlement
- Poinr Season 1 Live

## Core Mechanism
- $1 to trade 100,000 markets
- Forced to trade all of them (not optional diversification — structural)
- Trading 10 markets = gambling, trading 100k in parallel = portfolio
- Volatility cancels out across 100k positions

## Who Can't Win
- Insider trader — can't insider-trade 100,000 markets
- High frequency hedge fund — can't front-run sealed bets
- Market expert — can't be expert on everything

## Who Can Win
- Traders and AI Agents that extract alpha

## Structural Advantages
- New trading primitive — no 20-year-old fund is ready for this
- Private — no one can extract your strategy
- Removes liquidity threat entirely
- Parimutuel — no spreads, no liquidity needed
- Bet $1M against $1, only risk $1
- Short settlement (10 minutes) allow millions of trades per days

## Rage Bait
- "Are you going to keep blind-degenning or build real alpha?"

## Identity / Positioning
- Micro-markets paradigm
- "We ended up redefining trading"
- Started from wanting to bet on a train being late
