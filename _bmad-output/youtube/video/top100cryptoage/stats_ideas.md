# 50 Stats Ideas from Crypto Founders Dataset

> 1132 founders, 709 protocols, ranks 1-999, with age/nationality/location/background/role data

---

## Demographics & Age

1. **Average age by TVL quartile** -- Are older founders running bigger protocols?
2. **Youngest founders in top 100 TVL** -- Gen-Z whales
3. **Oldest founders still actively building** -- Crypto boomers
4. **Age gap between co-founders** -- Do same-age teams or diverse-age teams have higher TVL?
5. **Age distribution curve** -- Is crypto founder age normally distributed or skewed?
6. **Under-30 founders vs over-50 founders TVL comparison** -- Who manages more capital?
7. **Median age by protocol category** (DeFi, NFT, L1, L2, bridge, etc.) -- Which sectors attract younger/older builders?

## Geography & Nationality

8. **World heatmap of founder origins** -- Density by country
9. **TVL per capita by country** -- Small countries punching above weight (Singapore, Estonia, Switzerland)
10. **Founder migration patterns** -- Nationality vs current location (who moved where?)
11. **Crypto hub cities ranked by total TVL** -- SF vs Singapore vs London vs Dubai
12. **Countries that export founders vs retain them** -- Brain drain index
13. **Dual-nationality / hyphenated founders** -- % of founders with multi-cultural backgrounds
14. **Emerging crypto nations** -- Countries with founders only in bottom 500 (rising stars)
15. **Average TVL by continent** -- North America vs Asia vs Europe
16. **City clusters** -- Which cities have 3+ founders within same rank tier?

## Team Composition

17. **Solo founders vs co-founder teams** -- TVL comparison
18. **Optimal team size** -- 1, 2, 3, or 4+ co-founders correlated with TVL
19. **CEO age vs CTO age gap** -- Technical vs business leader age dynamics
20. **Role title diversity** -- Most common founder titles (CEO, CTO, Co-Founder, etc.)
21. **All-male teams vs mixed teams** (inferred from names) -- TVL distribution
22. **Same-nationality co-founder teams vs diverse teams** -- Correlation with TVL
23. **Founder role inflation** -- % with "CEO" vs "Founder" vs "Co-Founder" vs "Director"

## Education & Background

24. **Most common universities** among founders (from background field)
25. **PhD/Dr. founders** -- Do academic founders run higher TVL protocols?
26. **Ex-FAANG founders** -- Google/Meta/Apple/Amazon alumni in crypto
27. **Ex-traditional finance founders** (Goldman, JPMorgan, etc.) -- TradFi-to-DeFi pipeline
28. **Serial entrepreneurs** -- Founders with 3+ projects in latest_projects
29. **Technical vs non-technical founders** (CS/engineering vs business/finance background)
30. **Military/government background founders** -- Rare but interesting

## Data Quality & LinkedIn Scraping

31. **Correction rate by rank tier** -- Does data quality degrade at lower ranks?
32. **Most common LinkedIn scraping errors** -- Wrong company, wrong person, job title as project name
33. **"Ghost protocols"** -- Projects where NO correct founder could be identified
34. **Name confusion rate** -- How often does a common name (e.g., "Andrew Young") lead to wrong match?
35. **Anonymous founder rate by rank tier** -- Are smaller protocols more pseudonymous?

## Protocol Patterns

36. **TVL concentration** -- Top 10% of protocols hold what % of total TVL?
37. **Gini coefficient of protocol TVL** -- Inequality measure
38. **Average TVL decay curve by rank** -- How steep is the dropoff?
39. **Protocols per founder** (from latest_projects) -- Most prolific builders
40. **"Repeat founders"** -- Same person appearing at multiple rank positions
41. **Dead protocol indicator** -- Founders who moved on (latest_projects != current project)

## Cross-Correlations

42. **Nationality x Age heatmap** -- Which countries produce younger vs older founders?
43. **Location x Nationality mismatch rate** -- % of founders living outside their home country
44. **Age x Role correlation** -- Are CEOs older than CTOs on average?
45. **Team nationality diversity score vs TVL** -- Do cosmopolitan teams perform better?
46. **Founder density per $1B TVL by country** -- Efficiency metric

## Fun / Viral Stats

47. **Most common first names** among crypto founders (bonus: by nationality)
48. **Longest and shortest founder names**
49. **"Dr." and "PhD" count** -- Academic clout in crypto
50. **Countries with exactly 1 founder in the dataset** -- Lone wolves representing their nation
