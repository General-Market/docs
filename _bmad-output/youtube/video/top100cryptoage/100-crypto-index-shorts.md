# 100 Crypto Index YouTube Shorts Ideas

> **Concept:** Each video is a 60-second visual index — a ranked list, a live ticker, a category breakdown — that people can *see* and instantly understand. The hook grabs in <3 seconds. The format is: **bold claim + visual proof + "you didn't know this"**.
>
> **Hook formula used:** Every hook follows proven viral patterns — Curiosity Gap, Contrarian Statement, Shocking Stat, Visual Contradiction, or Identity Challenge.

---

## CATEGORY 1: SECTOR INDICES (The "Top X in Y" Format)

### 1. Top 10 Crypto Gaming Tokens in 60 Seconds
**Hook:** "The gaming crypto market is worth $14B — and you can't name 5 tokens."
**Why it hooks:** Identity challenge + knowledge gap. Gamers AND crypto people both feel called out. Visual ticker of logos racing to rank makes it unmissable.

### 2. Top 10 AI Crypto Projects Nobody Talks About
**Hook:** "Everyone's buying NVIDIA. These 10 AI cryptos are up 300% and you've never heard of them."
**Why it hooks:** Contrarian jealousy trigger. Viewers feel they're missing money. The "you've never heard" pattern forces a watch to prove you wrong.

### 3. Top 10 Real-World Asset (RWA) Tokens — The Biggest Trend in 2026
**Hook:** "$18 billion in real estate, bonds, and gold are now ON the blockchain. Here's the index."
**Why it hooks:** Shocking dollar figure + tangible assets people already understand. "Gold on blockchain" is instantly visual.

### 4. Top 10 DeFi Protocols by Total Value Locked
**Hook:** "There's more money in DeFi than in most countries' banks. Here's where it all sits."
**Why it hooks:** Scale comparison to something people know (banks). Creates urgency to see where the money actually is.

### 5. Top 10 Meme Coins by Market Cap — The Joke Economy
**Hook:** "A dog picture is worth $25 billion. Here's every meme coin that outperformed your portfolio."
**Why it hooks:** Absurdity + ego bruise. The "outperformed your portfolio" is a direct identity challenge.

### 6. Top 10 Layer 1 Blockchains Ranked by Speed
**Hook:** "Visa does 65,000 transactions per second. This blockchain does 400,000."
**Why it hooks:** Concrete comparison to Visa (everyone knows Visa). The number shock creates a "wait, what?" moment.

### 7. Top 10 Layer 2 Solutions — The Invisible Scaling War
**Hook:** "You use Layer 2s every day and don't even know it. Here's who's winning the war."
**Why it hooks:** "You already use it" creates personal relevance. "War" implies stakes and drama.

### 8. Top 10 Privacy Coins — What Governments Are Trying to Ban
**Hook:** "These 10 coins are banned in 6 countries. Here's why governments are scared."
**Why it hooks:** Forbidden fruit + authority fear. People always click on "banned" content.

### 9. Top 10 Stablecoins by Market Cap
**Hook:** "Tether prints more money than most central banks. Here are the 10 digital dollars running crypto."
**Why it hooks:** Provocative comparison (Tether vs central banks). "Digital dollars" is instantly graspable.

### 10. Top 10 Oracle Networks — The Cryptos That Feed Real Data to Blockchain
**Hook:** "Without these 10 projects, every DeFi app would be blind. They're the internet of blockchains."
**Why it hooks:** "Blind" is a vivid metaphor. Makes invisible infrastructure suddenly feel essential and urgent.

|-----------|---------|---------|

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Best visual format for each type:**
- **Ticker/Leaderboard:** Live-updating ranked list with logos sliding into position
- **Race:** Side-by-side progress bars or animated charts
- **Comparison:** Split screen, before/after, or overlay
- **Timeline:** Horizontal scroll with markers and price overlays

**Ideal length:** 45-58 seconds (enough to rank 10 items at ~5 sec each with a 3-sec hook and 5-sec CTA)

**CTA pattern:** "Follow for Part 2" or "Which index do you want next?" (drives comments + follows)
