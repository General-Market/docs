## Important Corrections

The following corrections were identified during research. The original CSV contained LinkedIn-scraped data that in several cases pointed to the wrong individuals or organizations:

1. **Michael Schwartz & Aviv Sadra (listed as Obol co-founders):** These individuals are co-founders of Obol App, a cash flow management SaaS platform based in Israel -- NOT Obol Network, the Ethereum distributed validator project. The actual founder of Obol Network (DVT) is **Collin Myers**.

2. **Vipal Karavadra (listed as Fluid Lending founder):** This person is the founder of Fluid Commercial Finance Brokers, a UK-based traditional finance brokerage -- NOT the Fluid DeFi lending protocol. The actual founders of Fluid (DeFi) are **Sowmay Jain and Samyak Jain** (brothers from Kota, India), who built it through Instadapp.

3. **Romeen Sheth & Alex Farman-Farmaian (listed as Compound co-founders):** No public evidence connects either to Compound Labs (the DeFi lending protocol). Compound Labs was founded by **Robert Leshner** and **Geoffrey Hayes**. Alex Farman-Farmaian is CEO of Compound Planning (a $4B+ wealth management firm) -- an entirely separate entity.

4. **Anthony Bassenden (listed as Hyperliquid founder):** No public records confirm this person as a founder. Hyperliquid was founded by **Jeff Yan** (Harvard, ex-Hudson River Trading). Bassenden appears only as an independent trader.

5. **Alex Kuzmanov & Michael Assor (listed as Nexo founders):** No verifiable public records connect these individuals to Nexo leadership. Nexo's confirmed co-founders are **Antoni Trenchev**, **Kosta Kantchev**, **Georgi Shulev**, and **Kalin Metodiev** (all Bulgarian).

6. **Jason Quake (listed as Drift Protocol CEO):** No person by this name was found at Drift Protocol. Drift was co-founded by **Cindy Leow** (CEO) and **David Lu**, headquartered in Sydney, Australia.

7. **Iulian Serban & Ekaterina Kochmar (listed under Korbit exchange):** These are founders of **Korbit Technologies**, a Montreal-based AI edtech startup -- NOT the Korean cryptocurrency exchange Korbit (founded 2013).

8. **Jitendra Gupta (listed under Jupiter Lend):** This is the founder of **Jupiter**, an Indian neobanking fintech -- NOT Jupiter DEX/Lend on Solana.

9. **Albert Wenger (listed under Stacks sBTC):** He is Managing Partner at Union Square Ventures. No specific public connection to Stacks/sBTC was confirmed.

10. **Faisal Alnajdawi (listed as Gnosis partner):** No public connection to Gnosis (the blockchain project) was found. The known Gnosis co-founders are Martin Koeppelmann, Stefan George, and Friederike Ernst.
