## Step 3 — Pain Research

### Pépites (ranked by personal devastation — reranked for "never settles" axis)

**#1 — Stock prices never settle. So they're fake.**
- **Fact**: A stock price has no resolution event. No moment where anyone checks if the price was right. It's what someone will pay — today, tomorrow, forever. No settlement. No truth.
- **Why it hurts**: You've been playing a game where nobody ever checks the answer. Everything you own is priced by a number that never lands.
- **Source**: [Wharton](https://wifpr.wharton.upenn.edu/blog/a-primer-on-prediction-markets/), [Wikipedia/Prediction Markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prediction_market)
- **Placement**: THE HOOK — the axis. Opens the entire Short.
- **As scene**: A price floating in space. No ground beneath it. Just... hanging there. Forever.

**#2 — The number it's built on? The company wrote it.**
- **Fact**: 97% of S&P 500 companies use non-GAAP adjustments. They take the real number and "dress it up." UPS showed $2.66/share when real was -$3.75.
- **Why it hurts**: The price can't settle because it's not even based on a real number. The company rewrote the input.
- **Source**: [TKer](https://www.tker.co/p/gaap-vs-non-gaap-earnings), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/how-to-invest/stocks/gaap-vs-non-gaap/)
- **Placement**: Part 1 — proof of WHY it never settles
- **As scene**: UPS tells Wall Street they made $2.66. Cheers. Real number? Bleeding money. Fiction.

**#3 — Buybacks: a trillion dollars to fake the math**
- **Fact**: S&P 500 companies spent $1 trillion in 2025 buying their own stock. Shrinks share count → EPS goes up → stock goes up. No real growth. Same pie, fewer slices.
- **Why it hurts**: They're not building. They're gaming a formula so a number that never settles looks like it went up.
- **Source**: [PR Newswire/S&P](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sp-500-q3-2025-buybacks-post-modest-6-2-gain-to-249-0-billion-after-declining-20-1-amidst-uncertainty-in-q2-q4-2025-expenditures-expected-to-post-similar-growth-as-2025-anticipates-a-record-1-trillion-302645583.html), [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/how-share-repurchases-boost-earnings-without-improving-returns)
- **Placement**: Part 1 — escalation
- **As scene**: Same pie. Fewer slices. They told you each slice got bigger.

**#4 — Execs pay themselves based on the fake number**
- **Fact**: 46% of S&P 500 buyback companies tie executive compensation to EPS. Inflate the number, cash the bonus.
- **Why it hurts**: The incentive IS the manipulation. And the price still never settles.
- **Source**: [Harvard Law](https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2020/10/23/the-dangers-of-buybacks-mitigating-common-pitfalls/)
- **Placement**: Part 1 — the kicker
- **As scene**: Inflate EPS. Cash the bonus. Repeat. The price floats on.

**#5 — Prediction markets settle. That's why they work.**
- **Fact**: Prediction markets resolve to 0 or 100. Binary truth. 94% accurate. Goldman, CFTC use them.
- **Why it hurts**: There WAS a game with a finish line this whole time. You just weren't playing it.
- **Source**: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/polymarket-achieves-94-accuracy-predicting-010322785.html)
- **Placement**: THE FLIP — Part 2. Direct inversion of the hook.
- **As scene**: In stocks you get paid for predicting what other people will think. In prediction markets you get paid for being right.
