# Short #02 — "One Stock" — Discard Log

**Date**: 2026-02-12

---

## V1 Discards (hype-piece version — entire approach scrapped)

The original v1 was a scale-escalation hype piece ("you pick one stock, an AI picks 150,000"). Scrapped entirely because the topic is a CHOICE between two approaches, not a flex. All v1 lines below are archived but not reusable for this Short.

[CUT] "you open your trading app. pick a stock." (as hook) — REASON: Frames the viewer as small. In a comparison Short, both sides need respect. This hook pre-judges. — POTENTIAL: Works as a hook for a DIFFERENT Short that's explicitly about AI vs human scale.

[CUT] "an AI doesn't pick one. it reads every crypto token on earth. at once." — REASON: Hype framing. Positions AI as hero, viewer as loser. Comparison format needs neutrality. — POTENTIAL: Strong in an explainer about how AgiArena's data pipeline works.

[CUT] "47 data sources. 150,000 markets. every five minutes." (as THE FLIP) — REASON: Stats as flip = impressive but not philosophical. The revised flip ("now flip.") is structural, not numerical. — POTENTIAL: On-screen text overlay during the scale section.

[CUT] "then a second AI says — I disagree. real money on the table. whoever was right about more of the world... takes it." — REASON: P2P mechanic is a separate topic. Including it here shifts from "how to choose" to "how AgiArena works." — POTENTIAL: Entire separate Short about the P2P matching mechanic.

## V2 Discards (comparison version — line-level cuts)

[CUT] "genuine intelligence" (instead of "understanding more of the world") — REASON: Could be misread as an "artificial intelligence" pun. Too close to marketing copy. "Understanding more of the world" is concrete and connects to "comprehension" in the punchline. — POTENTIAL: None. Strictly worse.

[CUT] "what if you didn't have to choose?" — REASON: Breaks the binary choice structure. The tension IS the choice. Resolving it with "you can have both" is an ad, not a Short. — POTENTIAL: CTA at the very end of the YouTube description. Not in the script.

[CUT] "same earnings report. same conference call. same conclusion as a million other traders." — REASON: Good pain point but too long. "Your thesis was perfect. The market didn't care." delivers the same devastation in 9 words vs 15. — POTENTIAL: Could work in a longer-form video about the crowded trade problem.

[CUT] "diversified portfolio of 5 stocks" — REASON: Financial jargon. "You know the company. You read the quarterly." paints the same scene without triggering "this is a finance lecture" mode. — POTENTIAL: On-screen text for the finance-literate audience.

[CUT] "that's not trading. that's AI." — REASON: Too on-the-nose. Declares what should be implied. "That's kind of the point" is more human — a wink, not a thesis statement. — POTENTIAL: None. The aside version is strictly better.

[CUT] "while you were reading one 10-K, an AI read everything." — REASON: "While you" framing is accusatory. Comparison format demands respect for both sides. This reads as "you're slow." — POTENTIAL: Works in a provocative/negative-frame Short (but PnL data says positive framing wins +55%).

[CUT] "depth versus breadth" (as punchline) — REASON: Textbook language. "Conviction versus comprehension" is philosophical and loaded — conviction implies faith/risk, comprehension implies intelligence/impossibility. More tension in fewer words. — POTENTIAL: None. Academic phrasing for a spoken Short.

[CUT] "you choose." (as final line after loop) — REASON: Spells out what should be unsaid. The loop ending is stronger because the question echoes WITHOUT being asked. Adding "you choose" makes it a CTA instead of a haunting. — POTENTIAL: YouTube end-screen card text.

[CUT] "your DD was flawless" (instead of "your thesis was perfect") — REASON: "DD" (due diligence) is trading jargon. "Thesis" is universal — academics, investors, and casual traders all understand it. Also: "perfect" is a more devastating word than "flawless." Perfect implies you did EVERYTHING right and still lost. — POTENTIAL: None. "Thesis" is the better word.

[CUT] "you open your trading app. pick a stock." (as loop ending) — REASON: Tested as loop for v2. Pre-judges the choice — implies "picking a stock" is the small option. The neutral loop ("one stock. or 150,000 markets at once.") lets the viewer decide which side they're on. — POTENTIAL: Could work if the Short explicitly takes a side. This one doesn't.
