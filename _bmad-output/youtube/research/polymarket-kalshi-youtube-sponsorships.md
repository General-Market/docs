## The Creator Gap: Zero Standalone Prediction Market Creators

### What EXISTS in prediction market content:

| Type | Examples | Platform | Scale |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| X/Twitter traders | @PredMTrader, various anon accounts | X/Twitter | Small, fragmented |
| Journalists | Chris Gerlacher (@GerlacherC), Daniel Wallach (@WALLACHLEGAL), Dustin Gouker (@DustinGouker) | X/Twitter | Niche |
| Newsletters | "Event Horizon" (Dustin Gouker), "Closing Line" | Substack | Niche |
| Directory | predictionmarkets.directory lists "creators" | Web | Directory only |
| Sponsored integrations | 241+ videos across hundreds of existing channels | YouTube | Wide but shallow |

### What DOES NOT EXIST:

- A dedicated "Prediction Market YouTube Channel" with regular content
- A TikTok creator focused on prediction market reactions/picks/education
- A "Polymarket Shorts" creator doing bet slip reveals, reactions, or analysis
- A prediction market equivalent of BookItWithTrent or SportsBettingTikTok
- Any creator with 10K+ followers whose PRIMARY content is prediction markets

### Why the Gap Exists

**1. Regulatory uncertainty**
YouTube updated its gambling policy in November 2025 to crack down on gambling content, but prediction markets fell through a gap — YouTube's policy "didn't address content promoting prediction markets." Creators are uncertain about the rules.

**2. Spray-and-pray over cultivation**
Both platforms prefer paying for sponsored integrations across hundreds of existing channels (140 + 101 = 241) rather than cultivating dedicated creator relationships. The $83/tweet Kalshi model is volume arbitrage, not community building.

**3. Event-driven spikes, not sustained engagement**
Prediction markets spike around events (elections, Super Bowl) and collapse between them. After the 2024 election, both platforms saw "massive drops in users." Hard for a creator to sustain daily content.

**4. Young industry**
The boom only started in late 2024/2025. Volume went from $9B (2024) → $44B (2025). The creator ecosystem hasn't had time to develop.

**5. Content format mismatch (perceived)**
Prediction markets seem analytical (probabilities, contract pricing, hedging). This APPEARS unsuited to short-form, high-energy content — but this is actually a solvable problem. Sports betting was equally "analytical" before personality-driven creators made it entertainment.

## The Opportunity (For AGI Arena)

The data paints a very clear picture:

1. **$44B+ industry** with two well-funded platforms spending aggressively on YouTube sponsorships
2. **241 sponsored videos in 90 days** = massive demand from platforms to reach YouTube audiences
3. **Zero dedicated prediction market creators** = the category is completely unowned
4. **Sports = 89% of Kalshi revenue** = the sports betting / prediction market audiences are converging
5. **NFL banned them from TV** = YouTube/TikTok is their only path to audiences
6. **3M+ app downloads in one month** = the audience exists and is growing explosively
7. **Both platforms are hiring influencers at $83/tweet** = the bar for creator quality is on the floor

A prediction market Shorts creator right now would be the equivalent of being the first sports betting TikToker in 2019. The audience is there. The platforms would pay you. And nobody is doing it.
