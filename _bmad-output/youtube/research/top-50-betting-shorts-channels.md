## Key Findings

### 1. TikTok dominates, YouTube Shorts is a repost channel
~30 of the top 55 creators are TikTok-first. YouTube Shorts is where content gets crossposted, not where it originates. The discovery engine for betting Shorts is TikTok's algorithm.

### 2. The biggest accounts are personalities, not analysts
Pat McAfee, Dave Portnoy, Shannon Sharpe, Big Cat — the mega-tier is dominated by **entertainment personalities** who weave betting into their brand. Pure "picks" channels max out at 200-500K followers. Personality-driven betting content reaches 2-10M+.

### 3. PrizePicks/Underdog are the creator economy's backbone
Almost every mid-tier creator (100K-500K) has promo codes with PrizePicks, Underdog Fantasy, Sleeper, or Chalkboard. These DFS platforms essentially fund the TikTok sports betting creator economy.

### 4. The "Ladder Challenge" was a defining viral format
Turning $10 into $10,000 across a series of escalating bets drove massive views in 2024-2025 for Dr. Locks, JBigsDFS, CallingOurShot, and many others.

### 5. Barstool is an ecosystem, not one channel
Portnoy + Big Cat + Marty Mush + Rico Bosco + Brandon Walker + Picks Central collectively generate more betting short-form content than any other entity. Clips get reposted across dozens of fan accounts.

### 6. Female creators are massively underrepresented
Only 3 notable female creators: Amanda Casey Vance (1M+), KenzBrooksBets (93K), Kelly In Vegas (300K+). Major growth opportunity.

### 7. Prediction market short-form creators DON'T EXIST
Polymarket and Kalshi spent on 240+ sponsored YouTube videos in 90 days. But zero standalone prediction market Shorts creators have emerged. The category is wide open.

### 8. The scam/tout problem is massive
Vegas Dave, Mazi VS (14 felony charges), Cody Covers Spreads, Steve Stevens — many of the largest accounts have fraud allegations. This creates a trust vacuum that legitimate creators can fill.

### 9. UK/Australia have different ecosystems
UK: Twitter + Telegram + dedicated apps (Footy Accumulators). Australia: Dabble + social betting features. Neither has the TikTok/YouTube Shorts culture of the US.

### 10. Brazil is the non-English leader
Lucas Tylty (1.1M), Mundo Bet (619K), Theo Borges (255K), Quero Apostar (202K). Brazil's sports betting culture + YouTube adoption = dominant Portuguese-language presence.
