### Key Patterns

1. **Economic crisis = trading content.** Turkey and Argentina, both facing hyperinflation and currency crises, skew heavily toward trading/technical content (gold, forex, dollar analysis). Viewers need actionable daily signals, not long-term investing theory.

2. **Wealth = passive investing content.** Wealthier, stable economies (Germany, Sweden, Canada, Netherlands) skew toward ETFs, index funds, FIRE, and evidence-based investing. Less urgency, more long-term planning.

3. **Young demographics = general finance.** Countries with younger populations (India, Indonesia, Philippines, Nigeria) focus on financial literacy basics: budgeting, saving, understanding markets for the first time.

4. **East Asia = macro/investing.** South Korea and Japan have deep retail investor cultures where economics and stock analysis dominate. Corporate channels (brokerages like Kiwoom, Mirae Asset) compete alongside independent creators.

5. **Crypto correlates with weak local currency or young demographics.** UAE, Nigeria, Indonesia, and Argentina all have disproportionately high crypto content in their top 10.

## Cross-Country Observations

1. **South Korea dominates in scale** -- 10 finance channels above 1M subscribers. Reflects Korea's massive retail investor culture.

2. **India has the largest subscriber counts** -- Pushkar Raj Thakur alone has 14.2M. Sheer audience size means even lower engagement translates to massive view counts.

3. **Turkey's economic volatility creates uniquely high demand** -- hyperinflation and lira depreciation make finance content essential viewing. Finance has the highest YouTube ad CPM in Turkey.

4. **Smaller markets (Sweden, Colombia, Nigeria, Poland)** have fewer large creators, with a sharp drop-off after the top 2-3 channels.

5. **Many creators are cross-border** -- Mark Tilbury (UK/Dubai/US), Cristian Arens (Peru/LatAm), Hugo Botto (Spain/Canada), Omar Educacion Financiera (Mexico/LatAm).

6. **Only 2 confirmed Shorts-specific data points exist publicly**: Yomi Denzel at ~9.9K (France) and Nischa at ~64K (UAE) -- both from HypeAuditor.
