## B2B Finance Relevance Ranking

### Direct Inspiration (copy structure)
| Channel | Why | Adapt How |
|---------|-----|-----------|
| **Zack D. Films** | 3D explainer + voiceover, one concept per short | "How a flash crash works", "Why this ETF beat the S&P" |
| **Economics Explained** | Already B2B finance, animated | Direct competitor format |
| **PolyMatter** | Geopolitics + economics, clean visuals | "Why China's bond market matters for your portfolio" |
| **MagnatesMedia** | Business stories, company rise/fall | "The hedge fund that broke the Bank of England" |
| **Bright Side** | Mass-appeal fact drops | Simplify complex finance into "did you know" |
| **The Infographics Show** | Hypothetical + educational | "What if every ETF was tokenized?" |

### Hook Style Inspiration (adapt the format)
| Channel | Why | Adapt How |
|---------|-----|-----------|
| **Kurzgesagt** | Premium visual credibility | High-trust for institutional finance content |
| **Veritasium** | "Thing you thought you knew is wrong" | "You've been wrong about index funds" |
| **RealLifeLore** | Maps + data visualization | Use charts/maps for macro finance |
| **Fireship** | Fast-paced "100 seconds of X" | "DeFi in 100 seconds", "ETFs in 100 seconds" |
| **Half as Interesting** | Witty, short, one surprising fact | "Why your bank charges you for free checking" |
| **What If** | Hypothetical scenarios | "What if Bitcoin replaced the dollar?" |
| **Two Minute Papers** | Research explained simply | "This new AI trading paper changes everything" |
| **Cleo Abram** | Optimistic tech explainer | "This fintech is making investing actually fair" |

### Skip for B2B Finance
5-Minute Crafts, DaFuq!?Boom!, Jake Fellman, WatchMojo, Mr. Nightmare, Top5s, Nutshell Animations, Low Budget Stories, TierZoo, Casual Geographic, Storybooth, Ben Lionel Scott, Jay Shetty

---

## Key Format Patterns (2025)

1. **3D Animated Explainer** (Zack D. Films) - Highest growth format, 3x retention
2. **Fast-Paced Voiceover + Stock/Animation** (Bright Side, Infographics Show) - Volume play
3. **One Concept = One Short** (MinutePhysics, Fireship) - Clean, shareable
4. **"What You Think vs Reality"** (Veritasium) - High engagement hook
5. **Story-Driven** (MagnatesMedia, Nas Daily) - Emotional connection
6. **Data Visualization** (RealLifeLore, Economics Explained) - Authority builder
