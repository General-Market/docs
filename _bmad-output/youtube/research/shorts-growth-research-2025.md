## 5. Hook & Retention Playbook for B2B Finance Shorts

### The 3-Second Rule

- **64% of viewers decide to continue within 2.5 seconds**
- Over 80% of Shorts engagement happens within the first 10 seconds
- The algorithm prioritizes completion rate above all other signals

### Proven Hook Formulas for Finance

| Hook Type | Example | Why It Works |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Contrarian claim** | "Stop diversifying your portfolio" | Challenges assumed knowledge, creates curiosity gap |
| **Surprising stat** | "97% of ETFs underperform their benchmark" | Data-driven authority + shock value |
| **Pain-point question** | "Are you paying hidden fees on your index fund?" | Speaks directly to viewer's fear |
| **Payoff preview** | Show the result/chart first, then explain how | Promise of value upfront |
| **Identity challenge** | "If you own an index fund, you need to hear this" | Self-selection hook, viewer feels personally addressed |
| **Authority + controversy** | "I managed a hedge fund for 15 years. Here's what Wall Street won't tell you." | Credibility + insider knowledge tease |

### Visual Format Best Practices

| Element | Best Practice |
|---------|---------------|
| **Length** | 20-25 seconds for highest completion rate |
| **Pattern interrupts** | Visual change every 2-3 seconds (zoom, cut, text pop, scene change) |
| **Text overlays** | Bold, high-contrast captions (Alex Hormozi style) |
| **Triple-layer engagement** | Visual + on-screen text + voiceover simultaneously |
| **Music** | Background music + voice narration = 17% better completion |
| **Trending audio** | Using trending sounds in first 5 seconds = 21% more algorithmic boost |

### Completion Rate Benchmarks

| Completion Rate | Algorithm Treatment |
|----------------|-------------------|
| 90-100% | Maximum distribution, viral potential |
| 70%+ | Priority in suggested/recommended feeds |
| 50-60% | Solid performance, steady growth |
| Below 50% | Algorithm treats as "skippable," limited reach |

### The B2B Finance Shorts Formula

Based on what works across all the top creators analyzed:

```
[Bold hook in first 1.5 seconds]
   -> "Here's why..." (context in 5 seconds)
      -> Visual proof (data, chart, animation) in 10 seconds
         -> Insight/reveal (the "aha") in 5 seconds
            -> CTA or brand anchor in 3 seconds
```

**Total: ~25 seconds. Optimal for completion rate.**
