## 5. Chinese Variable Interest Entities #Shorts
**Channel:** Patrick Boyle | **Views:** 130K | **Date:** N/A | **ID:** DyClYlUnidc
**Link:** https://youtube.com/shorts/DyClYlUnidc

### Script:
A quick video about Chinese stocks and investing
in Chinese stocks as a foreigner. Under Chinese law, foreigners are not allowed
to own certain large important Chinese companies. This makes it hard for these companies to
raise capital and list their stocks on foreign stock exchanges. They’ve come up with a work around which
is called a variable interest entity. They set up a Cayman Islands company that
anyone can invest in. This company enters a contract with the Chinese
company, giving it, not ownership, but certain economic interests and rights. This is then listed in the U.S., and people
invest, and basically pretend that they own shares in the Chinese company or that the
Chinese company is a subsidiary of the Cayman Islands holding company — even though it’s
not. Right now, US and Chinese regulators are paying
attention to these structures and that’s why some of the biggest Chinese companies
are down 20-40 percent year to date. It’s good to know what you are investing
in. Bye
