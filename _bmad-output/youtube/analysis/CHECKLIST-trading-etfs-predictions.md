# Research Checklist: Trading / ETFs / Prediction Markets Shorts

*Derived from 1,200+ shorts analysis across 25 creators. Tailored to this niche.*

---

## Quick Reference: What Works in This Niche

Based on the closest comparable creators (Mark Tilbury 2.9M avg, Humphrey Yang 780K avg, How Money Works 530K avg, Patrick Boyle 172K avg, Economics Explained 57K avg), the **winning fact types** for trading/ETFs/prediction markets are:

| Fact Type | Why It Works Here | Avg View Multiplier |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Counter-Take (#10) | "Everyone thinks X asset/strategy works, data says opposite" | 2-5x |
| Comparison (#12) | "$100 in X vs $100 in Y over 10 years" | 3-5x |
| Money Fact (#21) | Specific $ return, loss, or cost that shocks | 2-3x |
| Physical Object (#7) | Gold bars, trading floor, Bloomberg terminal, chart on screen | 2x |
| Nobody (#2) | "A random trader who turned $X into $Y" | 2-3x |
| Statistic (#11) | "95% of day traders lose money" type data | 1.5-2x |

**Avoid as primary:** Hack/Tip, Personal Experience, Technical Explanation without visual, Pure Prediction/Trend

## THE CHECKLIST

Use this for every short. Check boxes as you go. **Aim for 8+ checks before scripting.**

### Phase 1: Find the Hook Fact (pick ONE primary angle)

- [ ] **Counter-Take angle available?** Can you flip a common trading/investing belief?
  - "Everyone says diversify, but the richest investors did the opposite"
  - "ETFs were supposed to be safe — here's the one that lost 99%"
  - "Prediction markets are more accurate than polls — here's why that's actually a problem"
  - Sources: academic finance papers (SSRN, NBER), hedge fund letters, SEC enforcement actions

- [ ] **Comparison angle available?** Can you put two assets/strategies side by side?
  - "$1,000 in S&P 500 vs $1,000 in prediction markets over 5 years"
  - "The worst ETF vs the best savings account"
  - "What Polymarket said vs what actually happened — 10 events"
  - Sources: portfolio backtesting tools (Portfolio Visualizer, Backtest), historical price data (Yahoo Finance, FRED)

- [ ] **Money Fact angle available?** Is there a specific $ number that shocks?
  - "This ETF manager lost $20B in a single day"
  - "A Polymarket trader made $48M on the 2024 election"
  - "The hidden fee that cost ETF investors $3.5B last year"
  - Sources: SEC filings (EDGAR), fund fact sheets, Bloomberg/Reuters reports, Polymarket leaderboards

- [ ] **Nobody angle available?** Is there a person behind this story?
  - "A 26-year-old who turned prediction market arbitrage into a full-time income"
  - "The janitor who died with $8M in index funds"
  - "The trader who broke an entire ETF"
  - Sources: news archives, Reddit AMAs, court cases (PACER), SEC whistleblower filings

- [ ] **Historical Event angle available?** Is there a dramatic past moment?
  - "The day the VIX ETF blew up and wiped out $2B"
  - "When prediction markets called Brexit wrong — and right"
  - "The flash crash that made ETFs trade at $0.01"
  - Sources: financial crisis post-mortems, SEC investigation reports, CFTC enforcement actions

---

### Phase 2: Apply View Multipliers (stack as many as possible)

- [ ] **Counter-intuitive angle applied?** Does the fact contradict what the viewer assumes?
  - The viewer thinks: "ETFs are safe" → Your fact: "This one lost 99.97%"
  - The viewer thinks: "Prediction markets are gambling" → Your fact: "They predicted X better than the CIA"
  - *This is the #1 view multiplier (2-5x). Every short should have this.*

- [ ] **Specific dollar amount in hook?** Is there a concrete $ number in the first 3 seconds?
  - Good: "$48M from one Polymarket bet"
  - Bad: "A lot of money from prediction markets"
  - *Money numbers in titles = 2-3x boost consistently across all finance creators*

- [ ] **Named entity referenced?** Brand, company, person, or specific fund name?
  - Good: "Vanguard's S&P 500 ETF", "Polymarket", "Kalshi", "Renaissance Technologies", "Cathie Wood's ARKK"
  - Bad: "An index fund", "a prediction market", "a hedge fund"
  - *Named entities = 3-5x boost (Tilbury: Costco 12M, Ferrari 8.4M vs generic 1.7M)*

- [ ] **Visual anchor identified?** Something the viewer can SEE?
  - Trading chart with dramatic move (show the actual candle)
  - Bloomberg terminal screenshot
  - Physical gold/silver bar (Humphrey Yang: 19M views with silver bar)
  - Polymarket odds chart flipping in real-time
  - Map showing where money flows
  - *Physical/visual anchors = 2x boost minimum*

- [ ] **Absurd/WTF detail included?** Is there one "you can't make this up" element?
  - "He bet his entire net worth on a prediction market... from his mom's basement"
  - "This ETF holds only one stock and it's a meme coin"
  - "The SEC approved it by accident"
  - *Absurd details = 2-3x boost (Caleb Hammer, MrBallen, ZackDFilms all prove this)*

- [ ] **Single killer stat identified?** One number so extreme it rewires thinking?
  - "95% of actively managed funds underperform the S&P 500 over 15 years"
  - "Prediction markets had 85% accuracy vs polls' 53% in 2024"
  - "The average day trader loses 36% per year"
  - *2-3 facts max per short (Law 6: Goldilocks Fact Density). ONE killer stat > 7 mediocre ones*

---

### Phase 3: Structure Check (before scripting)

- [ ] **Story arc present?** Setup → Tension → Payoff/Twist?
  - Setup: "Everyone was buying this ETF..."
  - Tension: "But nobody read the fine print..."
  - Payoff: "...and in one day, it went to zero"
  - *Shorts with narrative arcs get 10-42x more views than info-dumps (Nas Daily: 6.2M anecdotes vs 147K tips)*

- [ ] **Self-contained?** Does this work as a standalone short? No "part 2" needed?
  - *Series fragmentation = view killer (Johnny Harris Iran 3-part: 121K each vs standalone Greenland: 7.4M)*

- [ ] **Dinner table test?** Would someone retell this fact at dinner?
  - "Did you know there's a market where you can bet on whether it'll rain in NYC?"
  - "Did you know one ETF accidentally held $0 in assets for 3 days?"
  - *If you can't retell it in one sentence, the hook is too complex*

- [ ] **NOT a pure tip?** You're NOT just saying "here's what you should do"?
  - *Hack/Tip is the #1 most-produced AND #1 lowest-performing type across ALL 25 creators*
  - Transform tips into stories: "Do DCA" → "This guy DCA'd $50/week for 30 years. Here's what happened."

- [ ] **NOT pure prediction without evidence?** If you're making a forecast, is it backed by data?
  - *Economics Explained's prediction shorts avg 40K views. Predictions without data = abstract theory trap*
  - Good: "Polymarket gives 73% odds to X. Here's the $48M bet behind it."
  - Bad: "I think the market will crash in 2026."

### Phase 4: Research Sources by Topic

#### For ETF Facts:
- [ ] SEC EDGAR — fund prospectuses, N-CSR filings (expense ratios, holdings)
- [ ] ETF.com / ETFdb.com — fund screeners, flows data, closures
- [ ] Morningstar — performance data, category rankings
- [ ] Bloomberg ETF IQ archives — unusual ETF stories
- [ ] r/ETFs, r/Bogleheads — real user stories and edge cases
- [ ] Vanguard/BlackRock/State Street quarterly reports — AUM data, fee changes

#### For Trading Facts:
- [ ] FINRA BrokerCheck — broker disciplinary actions, fines
- [ ] SEC Enforcement Actions page — fraud cases with specific $ amounts
- [ ] CFTC Weekly Commitment of Traders — positioning data
- [ ] Academic papers: Brad Barber & Terrance Odean research (day trader performance data)
- [ ] "Flash crash" and "meme stock" post-mortems (SEC reports)
- [ ] r/wallstreetbets — real stories of extreme gains/losses (verify independently)

#### For Prediction Market Facts:
- [ ] Polymarket — live odds, historical accuracy, notable bets
- [ ] Kalshi — CFTC-regulated events, unique contract types
- [ ] Metaculus — calibration data, accuracy track records
- [ ] PredictIt archives — political market data
- [ ] Academic: Philip Tetlock's research (superforecasting accuracy data)
- [ ] Robin Hanson's research on prediction markets vs experts
- [ ] CFTC rulings on prediction market legality (regulatory drama = great content)
- [ ] Election prediction accuracy comparisons (markets vs polls vs models)

#### For "Nobody" Trading Stories:
- [ ] SEC whistleblower awards (they publish the amounts: $114M, $279M)
- [ ] FINRA arbitration cases — real investor disputes with outcomes
- [ ] Court filings (PACER) — insider trading cases, fund blowups
- [ ] News archives: "teacher turned trader", "janitor died millionaire" type stories
- [ ] Polymarket/Kalshi leaderboards — top traders, their strategies

## Quick-Reference: Fact Type Templates for This Niche

### Template 1: The Counter-Take (highest multiplier)
> "Everyone says [common trading belief]. But [specific data source] found that [surprising opposite]. Here's what actually happens: [2-3 sentence explanation with one killer stat]. This means [one-line implication]."

**Example:** "Everyone says you should diversify across 30+ stocks. But a Vanguard study found that after 20 stocks, adding more barely reduces risk — and Warren Buffett only holds 5 positions worth 75% of Berkshire's portfolio."

### Template 2: The Comparison (highest raw views)
> "[Asset/Strategy A] vs [Asset/Strategy B]. If you put $[amount] in each [time] ago, here's what happened: [A result] vs [B result]. The difference? [one surprising explanation]."

**Example:** "$10,000 in the S&P 500 vs $10,000 following Polymarket's top predictions since 2020. The S&P gave you $16,200. The prediction market strategy? $31,400. Here's why nobody talks about this."

### Template 3: The Nobody Story (strongest narrative)
> "In [year], a [ordinary descriptor] named [Name] [did something extraordinary with markets]. [2-3 sentences of escalation]. By the time [resolution], [shocking outcome with $ number]."

**Example:** "In 2024, a French trader named Théo bet $30M on Polymarket that Trump would win. Everyone called him insane. On election night, he made $48M in profit — the largest single prediction market win in history."

### Template 4: The Money Fact (reliable hook)
> "$[shocking number]. That's how much [specific entity] [gained/lost/spent] on [specific thing]. Here's how: [brief explanation]. The craziest part? [absurd detail]."

**Example:** "$20B. That's how much Bill Hwang lost in two days when his leveraged ETF bets collapsed. Archegos Capital went from $36B to $0 faster than any fund in history. The craziest part? Most of Wall Street didn't even know he existed."

### Template 5: The Historical Event (dramatic anchor)
> "On [specific date], [dramatic event happened in markets]. [What everyone expected]. [What actually happened]. [One-line consequence with $ impact]."

**Example:** "On February 5th, 2018, the XIV ETF — designed to profit from calm markets — lost 96% of its value in a single day. $1.8B in investor money vanished in hours. The fund was literally designed to go to zero, and it was in the fine print the whole time."

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid (from data)

| Don't Do This | Do This Instead | Evidence |
|---------------|----------------|---------|
| "5 tips for better trading" | "This one mistake costs traders $X/year" | Hack/Tip = lowest performer for 15+ creators |
| "I think the market will..." | "Polymarket gives 73% odds to X, here's the $48M behind it" | Predictions without data avg 40K (Economics Explained) |
| "Let me explain how ETFs work" | "This ETF broke in 2018. Here's what nobody tells you." | Technical explanations without hook avg 5-32K |
| Show your face talking | Show the chart, the terminal, the object | Physical anchors = 2-34x boost (Humphrey Yang) |
| "Part 1 of 3" | One self-contained story | Series fragmentation kills views (Harris: 121K parts vs 7.4M standalone) |
| Generic "the market" | "Vanguard's VOO" or "Cathie Wood's ARKK" | Named entities = 3-5x boost |
| "Studies show that..." | "A 2023 UC Berkeley study of 50,000 traders found..." | Specific source + number = credibility + shareability |
