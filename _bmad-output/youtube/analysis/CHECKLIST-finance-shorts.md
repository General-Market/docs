# Research Checklist: Finance Shorts

*Derived from 721 shorts analysis across 22 creators. Tailored to the finance niche.*

---

## Quick Reference: What Works in Finance Shorts

Based on the top performers (The Plain Bagel 193K avg, Patrick Boyle 172K avg, Vivian Tu 104K avg, Money & Macro 92K avg, Andrei Jikh 76K avg, Logically Answered 7.4M ceiling), the **winning fact types** for finance content are:

| Fact Type | Why It Works Here | Avg View Multiplier |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Counter-Take (#1) | "Everyone thinks X about money, but the data says the opposite" | 3-5x |
| Corporate Autopsy (#2) | "Remember [brand you loved]? Here's how they died" | 2-10x (high variance) |
| Money Fact (#3) | Specific shocking $ amounts that rewire thinking | 2-4x |
| Named Entity (#4) | BlackRock, GoPro, Buffett, FTX — recognition = clicks | 1.5-3x |
| Novel Concept (#5) | Introducing a term viewers never heard ("productivity debt") | 3-5x |
| Crisis/Scandal (#6) | Financial drama, corruption, corporate collapse | 2-4x |

**Avoid as primary:** Generic Hack/Tip, Trend/Prediction without data, Technical Explanation without story, Personal Opinion without evidence, Podcast clip repurposing

## THE CHECKLIST

Use this for every short. Check boxes as you go. **Aim for 8+ checks before scripting.**

### Phase 1: Find the Hook Angle (pick ONE primary)

- [ ] **Counter-Take available?** Can you flip a common financial belief?
  - "No, BlackRock and Vanguard don't secretly control everything"
  - "Was Toyota right about EVs all along?"
  - "Sam Altman never ran a successful startup"
  - "Your big tax refund is actually bad news"
  - Sources: academic finance papers (SSRN, NBER), Wayback Machine for claims, FRED data, SEC filings

- [ ] **Corporate Autopsy available?** Is there a recognizable brand with a rise-and-fall story?
  - "GoPro is going bankrupt — and it's the CEO's $285M salary that did it"
  - "How Newegg went from gamer paradise to refund nightmare"
  - "The iPhone company that's now worth less than a Starbucks franchise"
  - Sources: SEC EDGAR annual reports, company earnings calls, Crunchbase funding data, news archives
  - **Critical:** Only use CONSUMER brands the average person recognizes. B2B brands (Sequoia, SPACs) don't work.

- [ ] **Money Fact available?** Is there a specific dollar amount that shocks?
  - "This CEO paid himself $285 million while his company collapsed"
  - "Renaissance saved $6.8 billion in taxes — then paid $7 billion in fines"
  - "$124 trillion is about to change hands in the biggest wealth transfer in history"
  - Sources: SEC filings (EDGAR), proxy statements (executive compensation), fund fact sheets, IRS data

- [ ] **Financial Scandal / Drama available?** Is there a villain, a collapse, or a scheme?
  - "The FTX collapse in one minute — Gen Y Bernie Madoff"
  - "South Africa had the world's best power company. Then corruption destroyed it."
  - "How insiders manipulated Polymarket to profit from the Ukraine war"
  - Sources: SEC enforcement actions, CFTC orders, DOJ press releases, court filings (PACER), inspector general reports

- [ ] **Novel Concept available?** Can you introduce a term nobody's heard before?
  - "Productivity debt — you're borrowing from your future self and you don't even know it"
  - "The Gini coefficient says we're living in pre-French-Revolution inequality"
  - "Variable Interest Entities — you don't actually own Chinese stocks"
  - Sources: academic papers, economics textbooks, Federal Reserve research, IMF working papers

- [ ] **Country Crisis available?** Is there a nation in economic turmoil with tangible human impact?
  - "Egypt's currency just collapsed — and they're building a $58B vanity capital"
  - "Pakistan's economy is unraveling — ships full of medicine are sitting idle"
  - "South Africa has daily blackouts because corruption gutted their power grid"
  - Sources: IMF country reports, World Bank data, Reuters/AP dispatches, central bank announcements

### Phase 2: Apply View Multipliers (stack as many as possible)

- [ ] **Counter-intuitive angle applied?** Does the fact contradict what the viewer assumes?
  - Viewer thinks: "BlackRock controls everything" → Fact: "No, here's why that's wrong"
  - Viewer thinks: "Toyota is behind on EVs" → Fact: "They might have been right all along"
  - Viewer thinks: "A big tax refund is great" → Fact: "It means you gave the govt a free loan"
  - *This is the #1 view multiplier (3-5x). Every short should have this.*

- [ ] **Specific dollar amount in hook?** Is there a concrete $ number in the first 3 seconds?
  - Good: "$285 million salary while the company died"
  - Bad: "The CEO was overpaid"
  - *Dollar amounts in hooks = 2-4x boost consistently across all 22 creators*

- [ ] **Named entity referenced?** Brand, company, person, country, or specific fund?
  - Good: "GoPro," "Warren Buffett," "Renaissance Technologies," "Toyota," "BlackRock"
  - Bad: "A company," "a famous investor," "a hedge fund," "a car maker"
  - *Named entities = 3-5x boost (Logically Answered: GoPro 7.4M vs LG Phones 6K)*

- [ ] **Humor or wit included?** Is there at least one funny, ironic, or memorable line?
  - "Gen Y Bernie Madoff" (Boyle on SBF)
  - "Hermione had no magical powers and blew billions trading" (Boyle on Caroline Ellison)
  - "Want to be a millionaire? Learn how to strip. — S.T.R.I.P." (Vivian Tu)
  - *Humor = 2-5x boost. Socratica proves it: same channel, jokes vs no jokes = 10x difference*

- [ ] **Story arc present?** Setup → Tension → Payoff/Twist?
  - Setup: "GoPro was the camera that changed extreme sports..."
  - Tension: "But the CEO paid himself $285M while revenue collapsed..."
  - Payoff: "...and now the company is heading for bankruptcy"
  - *Narrative shorts get 5-50x more views than info-dumps across all creators*

- [ ] **Visual anchor identified?** Something the viewer can SEE?
  - Stock chart with dramatic crash candle
  - Bloomberg terminal screenshot
  - Physical product (GoPro camera, gold bar, car)
  - Country map with crisis data overlay
  - Trading P&L screen (real account)
  - *Physical/visual anchors = 2x boost minimum (Warrior Trading, The Trading Channel)*

- [ ] **Absurd/WTF detail included?** One "you can't make this up" element?
  - "He paid $1.98 LESS per share just to avoid paying bankers" (Buffett)
  - "Anthropic employees tricked their AI into ordering a PS5 from a vending machine"
  - "The SEC approved it by accident"
  - *Absurd details = 2-3x boost. These are what people retell at dinner.*

- [ ] **Universal topic as entry point?** Can someone who doesn't care about finance still relate?
  - Good: Leggings → opportunity cost. Hiring → business finance. Productivity → debt metaphor.
  - Bad: "Understanding theta decay in options" (pre-requires interest in trading)
  - *Universal on-ramps = 5-10x reach expansion (Vivian Tu: leggings 554K vs Roth IRA 12K)*

### Phase 3: Structure Check (before scripting)

- [ ] **Self-contained?** Does this work as a standalone short? No "part 2" needed?
  - *Series fragmentation = view killer across all 22 creators. Every top performer is standalone.*

- [ ] **Dinner table test?** Would someone retell this fact at dinner?
  - "Did you know GoPro's CEO paid himself $285 million while the company went bankrupt?"
  - "Did you know we're at the same wealth inequality as right before the French Revolution?"
  - "Did you know Buffett docked $1.98 per share just to spite the investment bankers?"
  - *If you can't retell it in one sentence, the hook is too complex.*

- [ ] **NOT a generic tip?** You're NOT just saying "here's what you should do"?
  - *Hack/Tip is the #1 most-produced AND #1 lowest-performing type across all 22 creators.*
  - Transform tips into stories: "Diversify" → "This ETF held only one stock. It went to zero."
  - Transform tips into counter-takes: "Save money" → "Your savings account is losing you money."
  - **ONE exception:** Tips work when hyper-specific with a named brand + exact $ savings (Tata car: 5.8M)

- [ ] **NOT a pure prediction without evidence?** If forecasting, is it backed by data?
  - Bad: "I think the market will crash in 2026"
  - Good: "The Gini coefficient is at 0.83 — the same as pre-French-Revolution France"
  - *Ungrounded predictions avg bottom quartile across all creators (Adam Khoo, Economics Explained)*

- [ ] **NOT repurposed from longer content?** Was this designed natively for shorts?
  - *The single most devastating anti-pattern in the data:*
  - Native shorts average: 50-150K views
  - Repurposed podcast clips: 375-1.9K views
  - *That's a 50-100x difference. Design every short from scratch.*

- [ ] **NOT promotional?** Zero self-promo, cross-promo, or brand deals without educational value?
  - *Self-promotion is the #1 view killer across all 22 creators, no exceptions.*
  - If you must promote, front-load educational value (Vivian Tu model: teach first, plug last)

### Phase 4: Research Sources by Topic

#### For Corporate Autopsy / Brand Stories:
- [ ] SEC EDGAR — 10-K annual filings, proxy statements (executive pay)
- [ ] Crunchbase — funding rounds, valuations, investor history
- [ ] Bloomberg/Reuters archives — key turning point articles
- [ ] Company earnings call transcripts (Seeking Alpha, The Motley Fool)
- [ ] r/stocks, r/investing, r/wallstreetbets — retail investor sentiment at key moments
- [ ] Glassdoor employee reviews — inside perspective during decline

#### For Counter-Take / Myth-Busting:
- [ ] SSRN / NBER working papers — academic evidence that contradicts popular belief
- [ ] Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) — hard data to disprove claims
- [ ] Wayback Machine — prove what the original claim actually said
- [ ] Snopes / PolitiFact (for political-financial claims)
- [ ] CBO reports — nonpartisan budget/economic analysis
- [ ] NBER recession dating committee — official cycle data

#### For Financial Scandal / Drama:
- [ ] SEC Enforcement Actions page — fraud cases with specific $ amounts
- [ ] DOJ press releases — criminal financial cases
- [ ] CFTC enforcement orders — commodity/derivatives fraud
- [ ] FINRA BrokerCheck — broker disciplinary actions
- [ ] Court filings (PACER) — insider trading, fund blowups
- [ ] Congressional hearing transcripts (C-SPAN archives)

#### For Country Economic Crisis:
- [ ] IMF Country Reports (Article IV Consultations)
- [ ] World Bank Databank — GDP, inflation, debt-to-GDP ratios
- [ ] Central bank press releases (Fed, ECB, BOJ, etc.)
- [ ] Reuters/AP wire dispatches — on-the-ground reporting
- [ ] Trading Economics — real-time economic indicators by country
- [ ] Foreign Affairs / The Economist longform archives

#### For Money Facts / Shocking Numbers:
- [ ] SEC proxy statements — executive compensation data
- [ ] Forbes billionaires list / Bloomberg Billionaires Index
- [ ] IRS Statistics of Income — aggregate tax data

- [ ] Company earnings reports — revenue, profit, loss specifics

#### For Novel Concepts:
- [ ] Economics textbooks (Mankiw, Acemoglu, Piketty)
- [ ] Behavioral economics research (Kahneman, Thaler, Ariely)
- [ ] Federal Reserve working papers / staff research
- [ ] McKinsey / BCG / Deloitte research reports (free summaries)

## Quick-Reference: Fact Type Templates for Finance

### Template 1: The Counter-Take (highest consistency)
> "Everyone says [common financial belief]. But [specific source] found that [surprising opposite]. Here's what's actually going on: [2-3 sentence explanation with one killer stat]. This means [one-line implication for the viewer]."

**Example:** "Everyone says BlackRock and Vanguard secretly control everything. But here's the thing — they're index funds. The shares they hold are YOUR shares. They vote on proxies, but they don't choose which stocks to buy. The conspiracy falls apart the second you understand what an index fund actually is."

### Template 2: The Corporate Autopsy (highest ceiling)
> "[Brand everyone knows] used to be [what everyone remembers]. But [dramatic turning point]. The CEO [shocking personal detail with $ amount]. And now [current state]. Here's how it happened in [time frame]."

**Example:** "GoPro was the camera that made everyone feel like an action hero. At its peak, it was worth $13 billion. But the founder paid himself $285 million in a single year while revenue collapsed 40%. Now the company is heading for bankruptcy, and the story of how it fell is wilder than any video they ever shot."

### Template 3: The Money Shock (most reliable hook)
> "$[shocking number]. That's how much [specific entity] [gained/lost/spent] on [specific thing]. Here's how: [brief explanation]. The craziest part? [absurd detail]."

**Example:** "$6.8 billion. That's how much Renaissance Technologies saved by dodging taxes with a clever options trick. They'd buy basket options from banks, change the portfolio underneath, and reclassify short-term gains as long-term. The craziest part? They just agreed to pay $7 billion to settle — more than they saved."

### Template 4: The Scandal Story (strongest narrative)
> "In [year], [person/entity] [did something that sounds normal]. But behind the scenes, [dramatic reveal]. By the time [resolution], [shocking outcome with $ number]. And the wildest part: [absurd detail]."

**Example:** "In 2022, Sam Bankman-Fried was being called the new JP Morgan. Jim Cramer loved him. He donated $40 million to politicians. But behind the scenes, his girlfriend was losing billions trading with customer money. By the time FTX collapsed, $8 billion was missing. And the wildest part: his hedge fund was run by a 28-year-old Harry Potter fan."

### Template 5: The Novel Concept (highest shareability)
> "There's a concept in [field] called [memorable name]. It means [simple definition]. Here's why it matters to you: [personal relevance with specific data]. [One-line reframe that changes how you see the world]."

**Example:** "Economists use something called the Gini coefficient — a score from 0 to 1 measuring inequality. Zero means everyone has the same. One means one person has everything. Right before the French Revolution, France was at 0.85. Modern America right now? 0.83. That's why you're stressed at the grocery store while the headlines say the economy is great."

### Template 6: The Country Crisis (most underserved niche)
> "[Country]'s economy is [extreme verb]. [Tangible human consequence you can picture]. Here's what happened: [2-3 sentences of cause-and-effect]. And [what comes next — stakes]."

**Example:** "South Africa used to have the best power utility in the world. Today, they have daily blackouts lasting 10+ hours. What happened? Decades of corruption gutted Eskom — the national power company — from the inside. Now the president has declared a state of disaster, and 60 million people are living in the dark."

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid (from 721 shorts of data)

| Don't Do This | Do This Instead | Evidence |
|---------------|----------------|---------|
| "5 tips for better investing" | "This one mistake costs investors $X/year" | Hack/Tip = lowest performer for 15+ creators |
| "I think the market will..." | "The Gini coefficient is at 0.83 — same as pre-revolution France" | Predictions without data avg bottom quartile |
| "Let me explain how options work" | "This options trick saved a hedge fund $6.8 billion in taxes" | Technical explainers without story avg 2-3K |
| Chop your podcast into clips | Design a standalone 60-second narrative | Repurposed clips: 1K avg. Native shorts: 100K avg. |
| "Check out my podcast/course/book!" | Front-load value, slip promo in final 3 seconds | Cross-promo is the #1 universal view killer |
| "A company went bankrupt" | "GoPro is going bankrupt" | Named entities = 3-5x boost (GoPro 7.4M vs LG 6K) |
| Show your face explaining a concept | Show the chart, the product, the scandal document | Visual anchors = 2x minimum boost |
| "Studies show that..." | "A 2023 UC Berkeley study of 50,000 traders found..." | Specific source + number = credibility + shareability |
| Talk to "everyone" | Talk to people who've felt [specific emotion] about money | Universal emotional on-ramps = 5-10x reach expansion |
| Post 50 mediocre shorts/month | Post 4 perfect shorts/month | Plain Bagel (4 shorts, 193K avg) > tastylive (50 shorts, 1.9K avg) |
