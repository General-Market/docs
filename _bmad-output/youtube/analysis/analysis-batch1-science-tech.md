### Creator's Research Formula:
Fireship's dominant strategy is practical developer tips and hacks (42%), followed by technical explanations of CS concepts made accessible through analogy (27%). The highest-performing shorts flip common assumptions (Counter-Take) or compress sweeping timelines into rapid-fire lists. The research is almost entirely "lived knowledge" -- programming tools, CLI commands, language quirks -- rather than external research. When Fireship does venture into counter-intuitive territory (floating point math, RGB vs RBY), views spike dramatically.

## Key Cross-Creator Insights

1. **The Counter-Take is universally high-performing.** Every creator who uses it sees above-average views. Veritasium's counter-takes average 15.2M; even Fireship's average 5.2M. The "you thought X but actually Y" structure is the most reliable view multiplier across niches.

2. **Experiments are Veritasium's unfair advantage.** No other creator in this batch primarily uses physical demonstrations. This gives Veritasium a unique "prove it on camera" hook that drives massive engagement (avg 16.4M for experiment-primary shorts).

3. **Geography facts have a low ceiling but high volume.** RealLifeLore publishes geography-primary shorts at 48% frequency but they average only 510K views. However, when geography facts are paired with counter-takes ("WAY further south than you think"), views jump to 3-4M.

4. **Practical tips are a volume play, not a viral play.** Fireship's hack/tip shorts average 1.1M -- solid but not spectacular. The tip format is highly repeatable and low-effort to research but rarely goes mega-viral.

5. **Morbid/visceral processes outperform at Infographics Show.** "What happens when you get shot" (2.6M) vastly outperforms their other formats. The curiosity gap around bodily harm/death drives clicks in this niche.

6. **Kurzgesagt's "what if" comparisons are their sleeper hit.** Despite being only 6% of their shorts, comparison/thought-experiment shorts (continents swap at 9.1M) dramatically outperform their typical technical explanations.

7. **Military anecdotes are RealLifeLore's viral lever.** The B-2 and B-1 bomber mission stories (5.3M, 4.4M) dramatically outperform their geography baseline, suggesting narrative-driven military facts have cross-over appeal beyond the geography niche.
