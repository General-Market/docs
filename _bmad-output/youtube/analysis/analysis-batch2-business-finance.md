### Creator's Research Formula:

His highest performers consistently use The Comparison format -- placing two surprising things side-by-side to create an immediate "wow" moment. He favors brand-name hooks (Costco, Ferrari, MrBeast, McDonald's) as entry points for financial lessons. The content requires minimal deep research but maximum framing skill.

## Key Insight
Across all 5 creators, **The Hack/Tip** is the most commonly used fact type but consistently the lowest-performing relative to other types for each creator. Tips and advice are easy to produce but don't drive views. The highest-performing fact types are those that trigger surprise or emotion: parables/anecdotes (Nas Daily), counter-intuitive reveals (Tilbury, Humphrey), and dramatic comparisons (Tilbury). Creators who rely on first-person advice (Ali Abdaal, Hormozi) have significantly lower view counts per short than those who use external researchable facts (Nas Daily, Humphrey, Tilbury).
