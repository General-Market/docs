# Trading / ETF / Prediction Markets — Creator Landscape Research

*Compiled from 4 parallel research agents, 200+ web searches, Feb 2026*

---

## Executive Summary

**The "Fireship of Finance" does not exist.** No creator combines quant/engineering-level depth + Shorts-first format + entertainment value + consistent viral views. This is the single biggest gap in YouTube Shorts.

**Prediction markets have zero dedicated Shorts creators** despite $12B/month trading volume and 50M+ users across Polymarket + Kalshi.

**The most technically deep creators don't do Shorts. The creators who crush Shorts aren't technical.** This gap IS the opportunity.
