## Key Insight: The View-Count Formula

The highest-performing shorts across all channels share these fact type patterns:

1. **Nobody/Underdog stories with money figures** crush everything else -- a person you have never heard of doing something that earned an extreme amount of money (vandal making $200M, chef making $45M, man making infinite money with AmEx).

2. **Counter-takes with a contrarian money claim** are the second-best performer -- the title must directly contradict what viewers believe (Swiss rich without homes, Bill Gates owns more Apple than Microsoft, Walmart truckers earning more than Goldman analysts).

3. **Simple anecdotes with a punchline twist** go viral when they are self-contained stories anyone can retell (milk bottle pricing, Hooters toy Yoda, Amazon lichen book).

4. **Technical explanations and predictions without a human story** consistently underperform -- economics theory, macroeconomic forecasts, and process explainers without a character rarely break out.

5. **The Goldilocks zone** for fact density is 2-3 researchable facts per short. Shorts overloaded with statistics (like many EE shorts) tend to get lower views than those built around one killer fact with narrative framing.
