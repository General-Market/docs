## PART 2: INTRO SYNTHESIS — Templates & Formulas

### His TOP 3 Most Common Intro Formulas

#### Formula 1: The In-Medias-Res Live React Fragment (~7 videos)

Begins mid-scene, reproducing live stream energy. Viewer is dropped into an ongoing moment without framing.

- *"J'etais tranquille sur mon ordinateur quand soudain un mec m'envoie un message sur Discord"* (V1)
- *"Non, non mais je t'explique."* (V12)
- *"Tu mettras peut-etre un disclaimer au debut, ca fait vraiment bizarre."* (V8)
- *"qui se rappelle de Ashton Hall."* (V21)

**Logic:** Because his videos are edited from live REACT sessions, the cold open reproduces the authentic energy of the stream. Signals "this is real, unscripted, you're in the room with me."

#### Formula 2: The Shock/Provocation One-Liner (~6 videos)

Single provocative claim or fragment, designed to cause alarm before relief:

- *"Des poupees sexuelles pour enfants."* (V4)
- *"Bon, je sais pas vous, mais moi j'adore les enfants."* (V18)
- *"Je suis desole."* (V22)
- *"Quoi ? Anime grossophobe ?"* (V3)
- *"J'ai trompe ma meuf car elle ne m'a pas fait a manger."* (V13)

**Logic:** Mirrors the video title's energy. Shock is calibrated to be immediately defused by humor, creating relief and intimacy.

#### Formula 3: The Personal Story / Named Character (~5-6 videos)

Opens with a specific memory, person, or anecdote:

- *"Faut que je vous parle d'Anna Louise."* (V6)
- *"Ah, ca rappelle des souvenirs."* (V11)
- *"A la derniere soiree MS, il y avait cette fille la qui m'a dit..."* (V22)

**Logic:** Anchoring abstract arguments in concrete human memory. "Show don't tell" version of a thesis statement.

---

### Evolution Over Time

**Early videos (V1-V6):** Longer, more meandering intros. Often 400+ words before the pivot. Live stream energy is raw, barely edited.

**Mid-period (V7-V15):** Develops the "meta-preview" move — explicitly telling the audience what the hook is. In V3: *"c'est plutot l'accroche putacli que je vais utiliser."* Also develops ironic self-introduction (V9, V14).

**Later videos (V16-V22):** Much crisper. V22 opens with two words. V20 opens with a direct question. Intros land thesis within 150-200 words instead of 400+.

**Key evolutionary shift:** From "reproducing the live moment as-is" to "engineering the live moment's best entry point." Voice stays identical, editorial precision increases.

| Intro Type | Avg Views |
|-----------|-----------|
| Bold claim / thesis-first | ~69,000 |

**Finding:** Bold claim / thesis-first openers produce significantly higher view counts. Shock/provocation openers paradoxically perform worst — perhaps because the clickbait tag is more visible, reducing click-through from genuine interest.

---

### The "LoannLV Intro Template" — Reverse-Engineered

**Beat 1 — The Hook Dissonance (0-10 seconds / first 1-3 sentences)**
Shock claim, named personal memory, live stream fragment, or ironic self-accusation. Never a formal greeting. Never "Bonjour a tous."

**Beat 2 — The Setup Narrative (words 20-200)**
Contextualizes the hook with story, drama recap, or additional stakes. Live stream footage material — reacting to clips, tweets, memories.

**Beat 3 — The Pivot Declaration (words 150-400)**
Either explicit ("On va parler de X") or embedded in a thesis statement. Frequently self-aware: *"c'est pas le sujet de cette video... c'est plutot l'accroche putacli."*

**Beat 4 — The Permission Slip (implicit)**
Signals to the viewer why this topic matters to them personally — through YouTube creator economy content, personal stakes, or parasocial relationship.

## PART 5: STORYTELLING STRUCTURES

### Framework 1: Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS) — Most Dominant (~60%)

His primary backbone.

**V13:** Problem: *"Chat GPT est toujours d'accord avec nous"* -> Agitate: *"c'est le meilleur moyen pour jamais se remettre en question et foirer toutes ces relations"* -> Solve: *"ne croyez pas que votre metier sera epargne [par l'IA]... mais je pense pas que ce soit si grave."*

**V3:** Problem: Anime is being piled on -> Agitate: *"Vous ne serez jamais assez parfait pour ces gens"* -> Solve: *"excusez-vous si votre code moral vous dit de le faire... mais pitie, gardez de l'honneur."*

### Framework 2: ABT (And-But-Therefore) — At Topic Transitions

**V4:** "Shein has same problems as Zara AND it's cheaper, BUT the real issue is France's tax structure, THEREFORE the solution is deregulation not more taxes."

**V15:** "YouTube is becoming like TV AND that's what people complain about, BUT the real problem is YouTube for children, THEREFORE the question is whether YouTube can do what TV did well."

### Framework 3: The Contrarian Thesis Reveal — His Signature

Almost every video has a moment where he articulates a position contradicting what the title promised:

- V3 title suggests pro-Anime defense -> actual thesis: never apologize to people who want you to fail
- V4 title suggests pro-Shein -> actual thesis: deregulate France
- V16 title suggests racism discussion -> actual thesis: Tibo InShape's book and ego
- V18 title suggests moral panic -> actual thesis: nuanced discussion of fan-creator consent dynamics

Typically revealed at 200-400 word mark and reinforced in outro.

### Framework 4: The "Foil Character" Structure (~70% of videos)

Almost always introduces a named foil whose behavior he reacts to, then uses as springboard:

- TiboInShape as foil in V1, V3, V16
- Squeezie as foil in V3, V5, V9, V10, V15, V19
- MrBeast as foil in V15, V19
- ChatGPT as foil in V13
- Ashton Hall as foil in V21

### Nested Stories & Open Loops

**Nested Stories:** Consistently embeds story-within-story. V1: Lucas Vert nested inside TiboInShape analysis. V22: 15-year-old girl anecdote inside Bill Clinton apology history. V6: Anna Louise memory inside cultural analysis of "genant."

**Open Loops:**
- V20: *"A la fin de cette video, je vais vous reveler combien mois j'ai gagne."* — held open for thousands of words
- V19: *"De maniere generale, on va en parler dans une future video qui arrive tres bientot"* — cross-video loop
- V16: Ends by spontaneously generating next video topic: *"Ca m'a donne envie de parler des livres de youtubeur..."* — creating the next open loop live

Cross-video open loops are his most sophisticated serialization technique.

### Transition Types

1. **Explicit Meta-Pivot:** *"c'est pas le sujet de cette video. On va dire que c'est plutot l'accroche putacli"* (V3)
2. **The "D'ailleurs" Bridge:** His principal transition word. Appears in virtually every video.
3. **Clip/React Pivot:** Video clips as automatic section breaks.
4. **Rhetorical Question Anchor:** *"Mais pourquoi Chine ce serait de la merde ?"* closes one section, next section answers.
