## PART 5: WHAT SYSTEMATICALLY MAKES LESS MONEY

### The 3 Hook Types That Always Underperform

#### 1. SEASONAL URGENCY — avg 1,715 views (worst)
- "Scale your Q4", "Checklist Q4"
- **Why it fails:** Expires instantly, no replay value, feels like homework, sounds like every other creator
- **Fix:** Never make the season the hook. Make the TACTIC the hook and mention the season inside.

#### 2. MISTAKE/LOSS AVERSION (on narrow topics) — avg 2,075 views
- "Error #1 in Testing", "This mistake costs 1M€", "3 FAUSSES croyances"
- **Why it fails:** Negative framing without celebrity drama. The audience feels lectured, not entertained. Nobody wants to be told they're wrong UNLESS it's wrapped in entertainment (tier list) or authority (guest).
- **Exception:** "WORST Businesses to Launch" got 10K views because it's a TIER LIST with negative framing, not a lecture.
- **Fix:** Always pair mistakes with ranking/entertainment format or celebrity validation.

#### 3. ADVANCED TACTICAL HOW-TO — avg 2,120 views
- "Scale 100K to 300K", "Funnel d'Ads", "SCALER productivité"
- **Why it fails:** Audience too narrow. Title self-selects for people already at 100K+ which is tiny. Zero entertainment value. Sounds like a course module, not a YouTube video.
- **Fix:** Wrap the tactic in a story. "How I went from 100K to 300K in 60 days" instead of "How to scale from 100K to 300K."

> **The Valere's audience is 80% ASPIRATIONAL, 20% OPERATIONAL.**

### 9.2 — THE VERBAL TIC INVENTORY

Full frequency analysis across ~30 videos:

#### "Bref" — The Universal Transition

**Function:** Narrative landing gear. Closes a tangent, signals return to structure.
**Frequency:** 4-9 per video (tutorials), 7-13 per video (podcasts). Present in every single video.
**Signature combo:** "Bref, tout ça pour dire que..." — double-tic that both closes and pivots.

#### "Encore une fois" — Authority Reinforcement

**Function:** Reframes each new point as a restatement of established fact rather than a new claim.
**Frequency:** 0-17 per video. Peaks in V1 (17 occurrences = every 90 seconds). Nearly absent in short videos.
**Pattern:** Dominant in tutorial/breakdown format. Disappears in podcast format.

#### "Tout ça pour dire que" — Story Landing

**Function:** Extracts the lesson from a long anecdote or tangent. Always signals "here's the takeaway."
**Frequency:** 0-13 per video. Dominant in V5 podcast (13 = every 3.5 minutes). Absent in structured tutorials.
**Inverse of "Encore une fois":** Where "Encore une fois" reinforces known truths, "Tout ça pour dire" distills new truths from raw experience.

#### "OK?" / "D'accord?" — Comprehension Check

**Function:** Micro-engagement device. Creates illusion of dialogue in monologue.
**Frequency:** 1-12 per video for "OK?", 0-8+ for "D'accord?"
**Peak:** V22 has a 28-repetition "D'accord?" cluster in the outro (likely ASR artifact of rapid speech, but signals real verbal tic density).

#### "BOOM" / "Boum" / "Bam" — Kinesthetic Marker

**Function:** Marks sudden positive inflection points. Always accompanies a result or transformation moment.
**Frequency:** 0-19 per video. Highest in V5 podcast (19 = every 2 minutes) and V28 (8).

### 9.10 — MASTER STORYTELLING CHEAT SHEET

**To tell a story like The Valere:**

1. Open with a specific number, never rounded ("381,000 dollars", not "about 400K")
2. Use "mais" as your pivot — it's your ABT engine
3. Name your frameworks, even if they already exist (Script Tengu > "one-belief VSL")
4. Structure in 3s, ascending in secrecy ("la troisième, la plus importante...")
5. Use "Bref" to land tangents and "Encore une fois" to reinforce authority
6. Scatter "BOOM" at every result moment — it's a dopamine marker
7. Qualify your audience early ("si tu fais déjà X"), disqualify before the CTA
8. Place your Bridge explicitly: name the current state, name the mechanism, name the desired state
9. Confess a real failure before your biggest claim — vulnerability before authority
10. Deny your commercial intent before selling ("j'ai rien à foutre, je touche 20%... bref, clique")
11. End with warmth: "C'était Val, like, abonne-toi, on se retrouve bientôt"

**What separates him from imitators:**
- The numbers are always real and specific (bank screenshots, trade history, live dashboards)
- The vulnerability is genuine (false retirement at 19, overpaying staff, CEO hiring failures)
- The philosophy is earned (he philosophizes AFTER delivering tactical value, never instead of it)
- He names concepts in real-time (V5 shows "Natural CAC" being named live, not polished)
