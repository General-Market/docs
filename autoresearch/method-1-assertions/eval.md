# Method 1: Binary Assertion Evaluation

## Assertions (each is YES/NO)

1. **Hook speed**: Does the script grab attention in the first 3 seconds (before any explanation)?
2. **Pattern interrupt**: Is there at least one moment that breaks the visual/audio rhythm before the halfway mark?
3. **Concrete over abstract**: Does every claim use a specific number or name rather than a vague category? (e.g., "XQC" not "popular streamers")
4. **No corporate language**: Is the script free of words like "innovative", "cutting-edge", "leverage", "unlock", "synergy", "game-changing", "revolutionize", "ecosystem"?
5. **Escalation**: Do the market examples escalate in absurdity/surprise? (trains → streamers → pumpfun → 4chan → ice cream machines → military → space)
6. **The knife**: Does the final section land with weight — a pause, a shift in tone, something that makes the viewer stop?
7. **Under 90 seconds**: Could this script be performed in under 90 seconds at natural pacing?
8. **Visual specificity**: Does each market mention include a concrete visual cue (not just text)?
9. **The pivot**: Is there a clear structural break between "look at all these markets" and "here's why they matter" (the dark pool / no leaks section)?
10. **Single CTA**: Does the script end with exactly one call to action, not two or three?
11. **Cioran voice**: Would the prose make a reader pause mid-sentence at least once? Is there at least one sentence with nihilist tenderness or dark humor?
12. **No feature list**: Does the script avoid becoming a bullet-point feature dump? (each point must earn its place through rhythm or surprise)
13. **VC-legible**: Would a VC who has never heard of Vision understand what the product does within 30 seconds?
14. **Emotional arc**: Does the script move through at least 2 distinct emotions (curiosity → awe, humor → seriousness, confusion → clarity)?
15. **Rewatchability**: Is there at least one moment designed to make someone rewind or rewatch?

## Scoring
Pass rate = assertions_passed / 15 × 100

## Rules
- Each assertion is strictly binary. No "partially."
- The evaluator gives a 1-sentence justification for each NO.
- The agent modifying the script CANNOT see or modify this file.
