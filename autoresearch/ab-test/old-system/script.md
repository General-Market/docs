# Vision — VC Presentation Video Script

## Context
- Product: Vision by General Market — sealed parimutuel prediction market
- 98+ data sources, 400,000+ markets
- Testnet Point Season 1 Live
- URL: GeneralMarket.com

## Tone
Cioran method. Short declarative sentences. No corporate fluff. Setup → pivot → knife. The last sentence carries all the weight.

## Rhythm — Visual & Audio
Three acts. **Act 1 — Bombardment:** Accelerating cuts. First three examples hold ~3 seconds each. Then ~2 seconds. Then ~1.5. By military/solar, the cuts are almost too fast to read. The viewer should feel overwhelmed by volume. Sound: each data source brings its own ambient audio (departure board clicks, stream chat pings, radar beeps) — layering, building, never resolving. **Act 2 — Silence:** All sound cuts instantly. Three seconds of nothing. The "400,000 more" counter. The viewer catches their breath. **Act 3 — Breathing:** Feature lines appear one at a time, two seconds each. A single low tone holds under the text — barely audible. The logo fades in slowly. The thesis lands in stillness. Total runtime: ~75 seconds.

"Exclusive Trade."

GeneralMarket.com
