# Method 2: Sectioned Scoring Evaluation

## Dimensions (each scored 0-10)

### 1. HOOK (first 5 seconds)
- 0: No hook. Opens with context or explanation.
- 3: Hook exists but is generic ("What if you could...").
- 5: Hook is specific but doesn't create urgency.
- 7: Hook creates genuine curiosity — viewer would not skip.
- 10: Hook is so unexpected the viewer physically leans forward.

### 2. ESCALATION
- 0: Examples are random, no build.
- 3: Some ordering but no sense of crescendo.
- 5: Clear progression in one dimension (mundane → exotic).
- 7: Multi-dimensional escalation (absurdity + scale + emotional weight).
- 10: Each example tops the last AND the sequence feels inevitable in retrospect.

### 3. RHYTHM & PACING
- 0: Monotonous — every beat is the same length and energy.
- 3: Some variation but feels accidental.
- 5: Intentional rhythm with at least one clear break.
- 7: Musical — fast sections contrast with slow, silence used deliberately.
- 10: The pacing itself tells a story. Speed and silence are rhetorical devices.

### 4. VOICE (Cioran fidelity)
- 0: Corporate. Reads like a pitch deck.
- 3: Attempts dark humor but lands as edgy-for-edginess.
- 5: Good prose but could be any startup.
- 7: Distinctive voice — nihilist tenderness, paradox, compression. Would not be mistaken for another brand.
- 10: Sentences that make you stop reading. "The universe does not care about your prediction."

### 5. CLARITY
- 0: A non-crypto person would understand nothing.
- 3: The product is vaguely clear but the value prop is not.
- 5: A VC gets what it is within 30 seconds.
- 7: A VC gets what it is AND why it's different within 30 seconds.
- 10: Crystal clear AND nothing is over-explained. Every word earns its place.

### 6. VISUAL IMAGINATION
- 0: Script is text-only, no visual direction.
- 3: Generic visual cues ("show the product").
- 5: Specific visuals per section but they're literal (screenshot of dashboard).
- 7: Visuals create their own narrative layer — they communicate independently of the words.
- 10: A viewer on mute would understand 70% of the message from visuals alone.

### 7. CLOSING POWER
- 0: Fizzles out or ends with a generic CTA.
- 3: Has a CTA but no emotional resonance.
- 5: Clean ending, single CTA, competent.
- 7: The ending recontextualizes everything before it.
- 10: The last 10 seconds are the reason someone shares the video.

## Scoring
Final score = average of all 7 dimensions (0-10 scale)

## Rules
- Score each dimension independently with a 1-sentence justification.
- The evaluator gives specific line references for high and low scores.
- The agent modifying the script CANNOT see or modify this file.
