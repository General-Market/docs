# Vision — VC Presentation Video Script

## Context
- Product: Vision by General Market — sealed parimutuel prediction market
- 98+ data sources, 400,000+ markets
- Testnet Point Season 1 Live
- URL: GeneralMarket.com

## Tone
Cioran method. Short declarative sentences. No corporate fluff. Setup → pivot → knife. The last sentence carries all the weight.

## Visual Rhythm
Fast cuts. Each "Exclusive Trade" card appears for ~2 seconds with the data source visual, then smash-cuts to the next. Building speed. Building absurdity. Building the realization that these are ALL real markets.

Pace accelerates: first three sources hold ~2.5s each, middle three ~2s, final three ~1.5s. The cuts get faster until the rhythm suddenly stops at the payoff.

A small counter in the bottom-right corner ticks up with each source: "1 / 98+" → "2 / 98+" → etc. Subtle. The viewer notices it peripherally — and realizes these are just the first nine.

## Audio
No voiceover. Text only. Ambient electronic pulse that accelerates with each cut. Each "Exclusive Trade" card lands with a low-frequency thud. Silence after "And 400,000 more." The ending block plays against near-silence — just a faint hum.
