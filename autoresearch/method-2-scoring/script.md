# Vision — VC Presentation Video Script

## Context
- Product: Vision by General Market — sealed parimutuel prediction market
- 98+ data sources, 400,000+ markets
- Testnet Point Season 1 Live
- URL: GeneralMarket.com
- Audience: Crypto-native VCs. They have seen 10,000 pitch decks. They will give this video 5 seconds of attention before deciding. The video is designed to survive that filter.

## Tone
Cioran method. Short declarative sentences. No corporate fluff. Setup → pivot → knife. The last sentence carries all the weight.

## Delivery
No voiceover. All text on screen. The video trusts silence and typography. If a VC needs someone to read it to them, they are not the target.

## Runtime
Target: 45 seconds. The cold open takes 5. The montage takes 25. The closing takes 15. Everything that matters happens before the viewer considers skipping.

## Format
16:9 landscape. This is not a TikTok. It is a document that happens to move.

## Typography
"Exclusive Trade" cards: white text, black background, clean sans-serif (Inter or Helvetica Neue). The font weight increases subtly across the montage — Light → Regular → Medium → Bold. By the last card, the text is heavier. The viewer may not notice, but their brain does.

Data source description text: bottom-left, smaller, same font. Appears 0.5s after the visual cuts in — the image lands first, the words arrive second. The viewer sees the data source, forms a question, then gets the answer. This micro-delay is the source of the humor.

## Visual Rhythm
Each "Exclusive Trade" card starts at ~2 seconds. As the montage progresses, the cards compress — 2s, 2s, 1.5s, 1.5s, 1s, 1s, 0.5s, 0.5s. The acceleration is felt before it's noticed. By the military/solar entries, the cuts arrive faster than the viewer can process. Then: dead stop. Silence. "And 400,000 more." The deceleration is the argument.

## Sound Design
The sub-bass hit from the first "Exclusive Trade" card returns on every subsequent card — but shortened each time, matching the visual compression. First hit: full 0.5s decay. Last hit: a clipped 0.1s punch. The montage develops a heartbeat that accelerates toward cardiac arrest, then flatlines at the break. After "And 400,000 more," the closing section has no bass. Only silence between the text. The absence of the heartbeat IS the sound design of the ending.

## Visual Layer: Running Counter
Bottom-right corner carries a persistent counter: cumulative live markets. It ticks up with each source reveal — 847 → 14,291 → 23,508 → 32,749 → ... The counter accelerates with the cuts. On the "400,000 more" card, it spins rapidly to 400,000+ and stops. On mute, the counter alone tells the story: *there are this many markets, and they keep coming.*
