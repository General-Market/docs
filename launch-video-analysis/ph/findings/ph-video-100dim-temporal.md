## How to read this table

- **r** = Spearman correlation with votes. Positive means "more of this = more votes."
- **high vs low** = median votes for videos above vs below the dimension's median. E.g. "406 vs 319" means videos with above-median transitions got median 406 votes, below-median got 319.
- **Trend arrows**: ↑↑ = grew >0.2 from 2024→2026, ↑ = grew 0.1-0.2, → = stable, ↓ = faded 0.1-0.2, ↓↓ = faded >0.2
