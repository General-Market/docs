3. **Scroll behavior** - Natural scroll patterns, variable speed
4. **Navigation patterns** - Don't teleport between elements, follow natural reading flow
5. **Typing cadence** - Variable speed, occasional pauses, not constant WPM
6. **Session duration** - Don't log in and immediately perform actions
7. **Browser fingerprint** - Use AdsPower's anti-detect profiles
8. **IP reputation** - VPS IPs are scrutinized more heavily
